We had two weeks to pick our Florida town. So we built the guide we wished we'd had.

In 2005, after selling their business, Mark and Betsy Scott had two weeks to choose where in Florida to retire and raise three kids. Twenty years later, this is what we'd tell a friend.

  • Honest about the tradeoffs. Insurance, storms, season crowds and the airport drive, for every town.
