Privacy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version
  • We never sell your information. We share it with a local agent only when you ask to hear from one or add your number for a call.
  • We use Google Analytics to see which pages people read. It sets cookies. We don't run ads or use advertising cookies.
  • You're in control. Unsubscribe from any email, reply STOP to any text, and we'll delete what we have about you if you ask.

This policy covers floridacoastal.co and the emails and texts we send. Florida Coastal is run by the Scott family. Here's what we collect, why, and what we never do.

What you give us

When you ask for the guide or sign up for the monthly letter, ask about a town, ask for your top 5 by email, answer our follow up questions or ask to claim a town as an agent, we save what you type: your name, email, phone if you add it, when you're hoping to move and your question. For your top 5 we also save the towns we matched you to and your answers to our questions.

How we use it

To answer you, send what you asked for, follow up about your move and send the monthly letter if you signed up. If you ask to hear from a local agent, or add your number for a call, we share your name, contact details, your towns and your question with that agent so they can help. We never sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Calls and texts

If you give us your phone number, Florida Coastal and the local agent we connect you with may call or text you about your move, including by automated means. You don't have to agree to this to use the site or buy anything. Msg and data rates may apply, and how often we text depends on your conversation with us. Reply STOP to any text to stop, or HELP for help. We never share your number or your consent to be texted with anyone for their own marketing.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits: which pages are read, roughly where visitors are, what device they use, where they came from (including campaign tags on links) and whether people finish our questions. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser that can last up to two years, and Google processes this data under its own policy. You can turn it off with Google's opt out add on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

We also keep our own simple visit counts in our database. Those use a random id that lasts only while your tab is open and don't include your name or email. We don't run ads, use advertising cookies or sell data to ad networks. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it sends them.

On your device

Your answers, top 5 and a few settings (like which towns you're comparing) are saved in your own browser so they're still there when you come back. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Who helps us run the site

Vercel hosts the site. Supabase stores what you send us. Google runs Analytics and Fonts. An email service sends our emails from hello@floridacoastal.co. They handle your information only to provide those services for us.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a Do Not Track signal. There's no common standard for it yet, so the site works the same either way. You can still block or delete cookies, or use the Google opt out above.

Kids

This site is for adults planning a move. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child sent us something, email us and we'll delete it.

How long we keep it and how we protect it

We keep your information while we're helping you with your move and for our records after, and delete it sooner if you ask. We use reasonable safeguards, like encrypted connections and access limited to our family, but no system is perfect.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can reply STOP to any text. To see, fix or delete what we have about you, email hello@floridacoastal.co or reply to any email from us and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.

Contact

Florida Coastal, hello@floridacoastal.co.
