Naples, Marco Island and the Everglades

The polished southwest corner of the state, with calm Gulf beaches, golf and a walkable downtown in Naples. Past Marco Island the road ends at Everglades City and Chokoloskee, on the edge of the Ten Thousand Islands and Everglades National Park.
