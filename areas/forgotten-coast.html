The Forgotten Coast

East of Panama City the high rises stop. Port St. Joe, Apalachicola and Carrabelle are small working waterfront towns on quiet bays, St. George Island has the long empty beaches, and the coast runs on east past St. Teresa to the river town of St. Marks, half an hour from Tallahassee.
