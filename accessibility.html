Accessibility

Last updated November 10, 2026

The short version

We want everyone planning a move to be able to use this site, whatever device, browser or assistive technology they use. If something doesn't work for you, email hello@floridacoastal.co and we'll get you the information another way.

What we aim for

We're working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. That means readable text with strong contrast, pages that work with a keyboard and a screen reader, descriptions for meaningful images, and forms with clear labels.

Where we know we fall short

Our maps are visual by nature. Everything on them is also available as a list: every town is on the Beach Towns page, and the Vibe Map lists each style’s towns beside the map. If a map or the quiz gets in your way, tell us and we'll help you directly.

Tell us what's not working

Email hello@floridacoastal.co with the page and what happened. We read every message, and we'll get back to you and fix what we can.
