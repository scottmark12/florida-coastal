The Best Florida Beach Towns for a Social Scene Aren't on the Best Beaches

We scored 167 coastal towns on how easy it is to meet people. The winners have real downtowns. Only 2 of Florida's 26 best beach towns made the cut.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 3, 20266 min read

Most nightlife lists stop at Miami. Ours looks for towns where a good night out is a short walk from home. We score every one of the 167 towns on Florida's coast the same way, from things we can count and check, never reviews or ads.

Thirty three towns carry our Social Scene style. We ranked them on nightlife and meeting people. Every score below shows where the town ranks out of all 167 (here's how we score).

1. Miami (South Beach)

Miami and Fort LauderdaleTypical home price about $524K

No. 1 on our listNightlife 5/5 #1 of 167Meet people 5/5 #1 of 167

Art deco along Ocean Drive, a beachwalk for sunrise runs and dinners that start late. You run the beachwalk at sunrise, work from a cafe and head out to dinner late.

Why it ranks
Big city energy on the beach
Walkable
Keep in mind
Flooding, traffic, high insurance

2. Key West

Florida KeysTypical home price about $1.05M

No. 2 on our listNightlife 4/5 #8 of 167Meet people 5/5 #2 of 167

Key West scores a 5 for meeting people, and if you've been, you know why. Every evening the town walks down to Mallory Square for the sunset. It's also about $1.05M for a typical home, a long way from the mainland, with high insurance. A wonderful place to be social, and a big commitment to live.

Why it ranks
One of a kind walkable island
Big social scene
Keep in mind
Long evacuation route, high insurance, small lots

3. St. Petersburg

St. Pete and the Clearwater BeachesTypical home price about $495K

No. 3 on our listNightlife 4/5 #4 of 167Meet people 5/5 #9 of 167

A sunny waterfront city with a walkable downtown, the Dalí Museum, murals on every block and Gulf beaches 20 minutes away. You walk the waterfront parks to the Pier, wander the Saturday Morning Market and pick a brewery for the afternoon.

Why it ranks
A big arts, food and music scene
A walkable downtown on the water
Keep in mind
Flooding in low lying neighborhoods, rising prices

Miami and Fort LauderdaleTypical home price about $693K

No. 4 on our listNightlife 4/5 #10 of 167Meet people 5/5 #6 of 167

A small island city ringed by the Middle River, built around the restaurants, bars and shops of Wilton Drive. You kayak the river loop around the city in the morning and walk Wilton Drive for dinner and a show at night.

Why it ranks
A walkable, social main street
Ten minutes from the beach
Keep in mind
Flooding at king tides, traffic

The Palm BeachesTypical home price about $404K

No. 5 on our listNightlife 4/5 #6 of 167Meet people 5/5 #10 of 167

The downtown of Palm Beach County, with Clematis Street, a waterfront, the Norton Museum of Art and a Brightline train station. You walk the waterfront to the Saturday GreenMarket, spend the afternoon at the Norton and stay out on Clematis Street.

Why it ranks
A real downtown with nightlife and arts
The airport and the train are minutes away
Keep in mind

A boating city of canals and marinas, where the boat goes out in the afternoon and dinner ends up on Las Olas. You take the boat out through the canals, then grab dinner on Las Olas.

Why it ranks
Huge boating scene
Close to a big airport

8. Sarasota

No. 8 on our listNightlife 3/5 #14 of 167Meet people 5/5 #18 of 167

The arts capital of the Gulf coast, with an opera, a ballet, The Ringling museum, Lido Beach just over the bridge and Siesta Key's famous sand minutes away. You walk the bayfront at sunrise, spend the afternoon at The Ringling and see a show downtown at night.

Why it ranks

10. Miami

Southeast AtlanticTypical home price about $570K

No. 10 on our listNightlife 5/5 #2 of 167Meet people 5/5 #32 of 167

Miami is the obvious answer, and it earns it. It's the only town on the coast with a 5 for nightlife and a 5 for meeting people. Brickell, Edgewater, Coconut Grove and Wynwood each feel like a different city, and The Underline, a park and trail under the Metrorail tracks with courts, gardens and a bike path, is the kind of place where a weekly routine builds itself. Go in clear eyed. Traffic is real, low streets flood and condo fees keep climbing.

Why it ranks
Big city food, arts and nightlife on the water
Major airport and international connections close by
Keep in mind
Hurricane exposure, king tide and rain flooding in low streets, heavy traffic, rising condo fees and assessments
See Miami's full scorecard →

Also carrying the style, in our order: St. Augustine, Tampa, Boca Raton, Atlantic Beach, Clearwater Beach, Cocoa Beach, Panama City Beach, Lake Worth Beach, Fort Myers, Hollywood, Jupiter, Daytona Beach, Hallandale Beach, Fort Walton Beach, Melbourne, Jacksonville, Cocoa Village, Gulfport, Panama City, Treasure Island, Jensen Beach, Madeira Beach and Port Salerno.

Being social doesn't cost more

You might expect the lively towns to be the expensive ones. In our data, a town's meeting people score has almost nothing to do with its home prices. 11 of the 33 towns in this style come in under about $400K, including Daytona Beach (about $211K), Hallandale Beach (about $245K), Pensacola (about $267K) and Panama City (about $284K). What you usually give up is the walk to the water. For more towns at the lower end, see Florida's most affordable beach towns.

Show up to the same thing every week

That's most of it. Pick two things, the Saturday market on Palafox, an art walk in Fort Myers, First Friday in Eau Gallie, the Friday concerts at the Landing, and go every week for a month. The faces start to repeat. Our guides to finding your people on the Florida coast and your first 90 days in a new town go deeper.

Before you choose a town

  • Walk the downtown. Go on a weeknight, not just a Saturday. See what's open and who's out.
  • Time the beach drive. Melbourne is about 15 minutes, Pensacola about 20, Fort Myers 30 to 40. Decide what you'll actually do. The beach towns where you can't walk to the beach helps.
  • Check the flood zone and get insurance quotes for the exact address before you make an offer. Start with our insurance guide and hurricane season guide.
  • Try it off season. How to test drive a town has a plan.

The beach is why people move to Florida. The people are why they stay.
