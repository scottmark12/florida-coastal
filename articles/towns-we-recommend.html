The Florida Beach Towns We Recommend Most

Out of the 167 towns we cover, these are the 31 we'd send a friend to first. They're picked for what daily life feels like there, not for who tops a spreadsheet.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 16, 20267 min read

Most lists like this rank towns on a formula and call the winner the best. We have a formula too, and it scores all 167 towns we cover. This isn't that list.

These are the towns we'd actually tell a friend to look at first. They're the 31 we built Florida Coastal around, before we added the rest. Every one of them gives you something you can't get just anywhere: a downtown you can walk, a beach worth the move, a harbor full of boats, or a pace slow enough to notice the sunset.

None of them is perfect. Some cost more than they should, some are far from an airport, and a few are quiet enough that you'll drive for a good dinner. We've noted what each one does best, and the town pages cover the rest.
