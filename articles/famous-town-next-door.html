Skip the Famous Beach Town. Live in the One Next Door.

The town you have heard of comes with traffic, meters and prices. Five famous names and the quieter neighbor to look at first.

Coast Guides·By Florida Coastal·Oct 3, 2026·3 min read

The beach town you have heard of earned its name. It also earned the traffic, the parking meters and the prices that come with it.

Here is the trick. You do not have to live in the famous town to use it. Live next door and visit when you feel like it. Five pairs to start with.

Instead of Clearwater Beach: Indian Rocks Beach

Clearwater Beach ranked second in the country in Tripadvisor's 2026 awards. That is great for a vacation and less great for a Tuesday grocery run across the causeway.

Indian Rocks Beach is a few miles south on the same Gulf sand. Beach access sits at the end of quiet side streets, and Crabby Bill's still anchors the main road. One thing to ask about: elevation and flood insurance, because Helene hit this stretch hard in 2024.

Instead of Siesta Key: Englewood

Siesta Beach lands on best beach lists year after year, and the village is busy to match.

Englewood is farther down the same coast and a full gear slower. Englewood Beach and Stump Pass Beach State Park are both open, and Dearborn Street has its own market. Ask how any home you like came through the 2024 storms.

Instead of Key West: Islamorada

Key West is the end of the road, with bars open late every night and a median sale price around $1.05 million (Redfin, August 2026).

Islamorada is roughly 80 miles closer to the mainland. It trades Duval Street for fishing docks, an art walk on the third Thursday and quiet side streets. It is not cheaper. Nowhere in the Keys is. What you get is less drive and less noise.

Instead of Daytona Beach: New Smyrna Beach

Daytona Beach has the name, the speedway and the big event weekends. It is also ninth on U-Haul's 2025 growth list, so the secret is out.

New Smyrna Beach is just down the coast. It has a real main street, a farmers market, a monthly wine walk and a surf beach. On our scorecard it has no score under 3, which only four of our 167 towns can say.

Instead of Fort Lauderdale: Lauderdale by the Sea

Fort Lauderdale gives you the skyline, the marinas and the airport. Lauderdale by the Sea, just north, gives you a small beach village where you park once and walk to dinner.

All of Fort Lauderdale is still a short drive away. Know before you go: the fishing pier has been closed since 2022 and is not expected back before 2028.
