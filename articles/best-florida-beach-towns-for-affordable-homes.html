The Most Affordable Florida Beach Towns, and What You Actually Give Up

Compare the lowest recorded house and condo sale prices in our verified January–June 2026 samples.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 3, 20266 min read

The lowest recorded prices for houses and condos tell two different stories. Our January–June 2026 county samples put New Port Richey at $240K for houses and Port Charlotte at $122K for condos.

We compared like with like: single-family houses in one list and condos in the other, using the same six months. These figures cover qualified sales within the named Census city or community boundaries. We ranked only towns with enough sales and reliable parcel coverage. A town missing from a list may still have lower prices.

What those prices buy

Daytona Beach is a good example of why the split matters. Within Daytona Beach city limits, houses sold for a median of $348K across 434 transactions, while condos sold for a median of $140K across 176. Holly Hill and South Daytona are outside that sample.

Port Charlotte made both lists: $275K for houses and $122K for condos. Those are community-wide results. A house on a canal with Gulf access will have its own market.

Holiday and Port Richey are also worth a look. Momentum’s November 2026 market-area figures were about $221K and $235K respectively over the preceding year. Those figures use a different area and period, so we keep them separate from the rankings.

Compare the monthly cost, too

For condos, check monthly dues, building reserves and special assessments. For houses, get an insurance quote for the actual address and check flood exposure, the roof and any seawall. A low sale price can come with a higher monthly bill.
