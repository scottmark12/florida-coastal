The Best Florida Beach Towns for an Active Outdoor Life (2026)

Surf, trails, bike paths, courts and a race on the calendar, all close to home. Thirty towns, with 30A on top.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 3, 20266 min read

Every beach town says it's outdoorsy. We wanted the ones where the trail, the paddle and the bike path start close to your front door. We score every one of the 167 towns on Florida's coast the same way, from things we can count and check, never reviews or ads.

To qualify, a town has to rank in the top 60 of 167 for the outdoors: surf, paddling, trails and bike paths close to home. Then we rank them on the outdoors (45%) plus, per resident, public pickleball courts, public tennis courts, public golf, bike paths and organized races (10% each) and gyms and studios (5%). Thirty towns carry our Active Outdoors style, and these are the 10 that rank highest. Every score below shows where the town ranks out of all 167 (here's how we score).

The most active towns for the money are on the Atlantic

Typical prices on our list run from about $278K in Fort Pierce to about $1.49M in Key Biscayne. Fort Pierce, Cape Canaveral and Titusville give you the most for the money. Barrier islands and the Keys cost more, and so does insuring them. Our town pages use a statewide average of about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). The six bills nobody puts on the listing walks through the rest.

Before you choose a town

  • Time the airport drive you'll actually use, both ways, on a weekday.
  • Pick your sport first. The Keys for the reef, the Nature Coast for springs and manatees, the Panhandle for empty sand, the Atlantic for surf. Gulf, Atlantic or Panhandle? goes deeper.
  • Check the flood zone and elevation. The water that makes these towns great is what brings the storms. Read our insurance guide and the hurricane season checklist.
  • Check the launch rules for the river, spring or park you plan to use most.

Choose the morning you want. Then make peace with the drive to the airport.
