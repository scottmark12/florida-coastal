The Best Florida Beach Towns for Healthy Living (2026)

Towns where a healthy day is the easy default: walk to the grocer, get outside, see a doctor close by and keep evenings calm. Fernandina Beach comes out on top.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 3, 20266 min read

Healthy habits stick when they're the easy option. These are the towns where a walk to coffee, time outside and friendly faces are part of a normal day. We score every one of the 167 towns on Florida's coast the same way, from things we can count and check, never reviews or ads.

For Healthy Living we leave out the party towns, the ones that rank high for both nightlife and bars per resident, plus towns without a real outdoors. Then we rank the rest on walkability (25%), good food close by, from grocers and farmers markets against fast food (20%), parks and paths (15%), a hospital and doctors nearby (15%), community (15%) and peace and quiet (10%). Twenty three towns carry our Healthy Living style, and these are the 10 that rank highest. Every score below shows where the town ranks out of all 167 (here's how we score).

Before you choose a town

  • Walk it in August. A walking life looks different in the heat. August tells you more than February.
  • Map your daily loop: coffee, groceries, a trail and somewhere to see people. If it all needs a car, the score won't help you.
  • Price the whole year. Insurance and flood coverage add a real bill. The six bills nobody puts on the listing breaks it down.
  • Check the flood zone and elevation. Ian, Helene and Milton all left marks between Sarasota and Marco Island. See our insurance guide and the hurricane season checklist.
