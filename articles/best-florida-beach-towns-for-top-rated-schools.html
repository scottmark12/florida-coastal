The Florida Beach Towns With the Best Public Schools (2026)

Twenty four beach towns in the seven Florida school districts where nearly every public school earns an A or B from the state.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 18, 20263 min read

If schools are part of the move, start with the district. Florida grades every public school A through F each summer, and it grades every county school district too. Most coastal districts earned an A this year, so an A alone doesn't narrow things down much. We set a higher bar.

Our Best Schools style goes to towns whose district earned an A for the 2025 to 2026 school year and where at least 95% of graded schools earned an A or B. Seven districts clear it, covering 24 of the 167 towns we score (here's how we score).

Check the schools for the address

A district grade is an average across every school in the county. Each home is zoned for specific schools, and zones change, so look up the grades for the exact address with the Florida Department of Education and the county district before you buy.

Questions

Which Florida coastal county has the best public schools?

By the state's 2025 to 2026 grades, Walton, Flagler and Nassau each earned an A with every graded school at A or B. St. Johns earned an A with 98% of its schools at A or B.

Do all these towns have the same schools?

No. This list uses the grade for the whole county district, so every town in a county shares it. Each home is zoned for specific schools, so check the grades for the exact address.

Where do Florida school grades come from?

The Florida Department of Education grades every public school and district A through F each summer, mostly on test scores, learning gains and graduation rates.
