Florida's Springs: The Other Florida Water

Crystal clear, cool all year and just inland from the coast. Why springs belong on your list.

Outdoors·By Florida Coastal·Oct 5, 2026·4 min read

Florida is famous for beaches, but just inland are hundreds of freshwater springs with water so clear it looks unreal. Many stay in the low 70s all year, which makes them a favorite on hot summer days.

Why it matters for where you live

If you love paddling, snorkeling or tubing, living within an hour of a spring run adds a whole new weekend option. North and central Florida, including the Nature Coast near Cedar Key, have some of the best.

Tips

  • Go early. Popular springs fill up fast on summer weekends.
  • Check park rules. Some limit swimming, tubing or group size to protect the water.
  • Look for manatees in winter, when many head to warmer spring water.
