The Best Florida Beach Towns for Quiet Luxury (2026)

Expensive, calm and close to a great dinner, with a beach worth the price. Ranked with our own scores, with Captiva and Palm Beach on top.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 3, 20266 min read

Most luxury lists are about the price tag. Ours is about the evening: a great dinner, a quiet street and a beach worth the price. We score every one of the 167 towns on Florida's coast the same way, from things we can count and check, never reviews or ads.

For Sophisticated Laid Back we kept the towns where the typical home is $600K or more, then ranked them on dining, which counts double, plus beaches, peace and quiet, how high end they are and how little nightlife they have. Twenty nine towns make our Sophisticated Laid Back list, and these are the 10 that rank highest. Every score below shows where the town ranks out of all 167 (here's how we score).

Before you choose a town

  • Visit in the month you'd live there. Most of these towns are busy in winter and very quiet in summer. August tells you more than February.
  • Time the drive to your favorite dinner. If it's across a bridge, try it on a Friday in season.
  • Check the flood zone and elevation, ask for the insurance history and get quotes early. Start with our insurance guide and the hurricane season checklist.
  • Torn between two? Read Destin vs 30A and Naples vs Fort Myers.
