The Best Florida Beach Towns for Remote Work (2026)

Pretty, walkable towns with a real community, within about an hour of an airport. We ranked the whole coast with our own scores, and 30A comes out on top.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 3, 20265 min read

Most remote work lists are big cities with a coworking space. Ours is about where you'd actually want to live. We score every one of the 167 towns on Florida's coast the same way, from things we can count and check, never reviews or ads.

For Remote Work we left out the big cities, kept the towns within about an hour of an airport, and ranked them on five things that count equally: walkability, community, beaches, charm and how many residents run their own business, from the Census. Thirty one towns make our list, and these are the top twelve. Every score below shows where the town ranks out of all 167 (here's how we score).

The rest of our Remote Work list

Thirteen through twenty five, in order: Anna Maria Island, Key West, St. Pete Beach, Sanibel Island, Stuart, Clearwater Beach, Treasure Island, St. Augustine Beach, Atlantic Beach, Tarpon Springs, Boca Raton, Lake Worth Beach, Jacksonville Beach. Also on the list: Siesta Key, Gulfport, Sarasota, Wilton Manors, Bradenton Beach and Fort Myers Beach. Fort Myers Beach and Sanibel are still rebuilding from Hurricane Ian, so visit before you commit.

Before you choose a town

  • Time the airport. Every town here is within about an hour of one. If you fly every week, look at the drive on each town above and price a Monday morning flight.
  • Check the internet at the address. Starlink works almost anywhere on the coast, but many condo buildings and HOAs won't let you mount a dish. If you're on video all day, look for fiber.
  • Find your Wednesday. Pick a run club, jam or art walk and go every week. Finding your people has more.
  • Price the whole year. Taxes reset when you buy, flood coverage is separate and condo dues are rising. The six bills nobody puts on the listing, our insurance guide and the hurricane checklist cover it.

Everywhere has wifi now. Pick the town you'd want to walk around at lunch.
