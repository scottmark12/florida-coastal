How to Test Drive a Beach Town Before You Move

A vacation shows you the best week of the year. Here's how to see the other fifty one.

Moving Tips·By Florida Coastal·Oct 4, 2026·4 min read

Falling for a town on vacation is easy. Living there on a random Tuesday in August is the real test. Here's how to find out if a town fits before you buy.

  • Visit off season. Go when the crowds are gone, or when they're at their peak, depending on which you'd find harder to live with.
  • Rent for a month. A monthly rental tells you more than a dozen weekend trips.
  • Do your normal errands. Grocery store, pharmacy, hardware store, the gym. That's your real life.
  • Drive it at rush hour. Check the drive to the airport and the nearest major hospital.
  • Go where locals go. The farmers market, a run club, a Sunday service, a trivia night.
  • Check the flood map and get an insurance quote before you get attached to a house.
