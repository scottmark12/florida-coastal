The Best Florida Beach Towns for Top Rated Beaches, and What They Cost to Live In

The beaches on this year's Dr. Beach and Tripadvisor lists, and the towns next to them. None of Florida's 26 best beach towns is easy to afford.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 3, 20266 min read

About an hour before sunset on Sundays, people start drumming on Siesta Beach. They've done it since 1996. The sand under them is almost pure quartz and stays cool underfoot, and in 2025 Tripadvisor called it the best beach in America.

It's a wonderful place to spend a Sunday. Living next to it is a different question, and it comes down mostly to money.

We live on 30A, a few minutes from Grayton Beach, which Dr. Beach named the best in the country in 2020. We love it. We also know what it costs. So when we scored 167 towns along the Florida coast, we looked hard at what a great beach does to everything else.

No great beach comes cheap

26 towns get a 5 for beaches in our scores. Not one of them gets a 4 or better for affordability. Among the towns with the weakest beaches, 72% do.

The awards make it worse. A beach on a national list brings crowds and bridge traffic along with it, and the prices follow. That's why plenty of people live a town over and drive to the famous sand on a Saturday. Skip the famous beach town has five pairs worth comparing.

The ten we'd look at first

Twenty seven towns carry our Best Beaches style. We ranked them on our beach score, then checked each against the two lists people quote most: Dr. Beach's annual top ten and Tripadvisor's Best of the Best Beaches for 2026.

Clearwater Beach is the one the lists agree on. Tripadvisor's 2026 awards put it second in the country and 17th in the world. The sand is wide and soft, Beach Walk runs to Pier 60, and there's a free sunset celebration with street performers there every evening, weather permitting. Sand Key Park adds 95 acres over the pass, and Caladesi Island, third on Dr. Beach's 2026 list, is a short trip north. It's also one of the busiest beaches in Florida, the causeway backs up on sunny weekends, and condos come with flood insurance and association fees.

Panama City Beach has St. Andrews State Park, second on Dr. Beach's 2026 top ten and eighth on Tripadvisor's. At about $485K it's also the lowest price in our top ten. The park has jetties to snorkel and a shuttle out to undeveloped Shell Island. Spring and summer bring heavy traffic, and condo fees plus wind and flood insurance run high.

Siesta Key was sixth in 2026 after topping the list the year before, and it ranked 28th on the World's 50 Best Beaches, the only US beach on it. The lot fills early, both bridges back up in winter and spring, and homes run about $809K.

Naples has Delnor Wiggins Pass State Park just north of town, sixth on Dr. Beach's 2026 list, and beach access at the end of nearly every downtown avenue. Clam Pass has a mangrove boardwalk with a free tram. Parking is limited, the Naples Pier is being rebuilt until about mid 2027, and Ian pushed surge into town in 2022.

St. George Island is the quiet one. Dr. Beach named its state park the best beach in America in 2023: nine miles of undeveloped sand on an island with no high rises, where the county caps buildings at 47 feet. It's about $1.1M, there's one bridge, and Tallahassee's airport is an hour and 40 minutes away.

30A is home, so we'll keep it short. Grayton Beach was Dr. Beach's number one in 2020, and it's one stop on 26 miles of sugar white sand with fifteen coastal dune lakes behind it and the Timpoochee Trail linking Seaside, Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach by bike. Santa Rosa Beach runs about $915K, and the two lane road crawls in spring and summer. Destin vs 30A compares the two.

St. Pete Beach runs from Upham Beach past the pink Don CeSar to the old fishing village at the south end, with Fort De Soto just down the Bayway and a Sunday market on Corey Avenue. Helene hit the island in 2024, and flood insurance is a real line in the budget.

Destin is the look the Emerald Coast is named for. Henderson Beach State Park has 30 foot dunes with nothing built behind them, though day visits have needed a reservation since May 2026. US 98 is the one road through town and it backs up from spring break through summer.

Anna Maria Island is Old Florida on a small island, with no high rises and a free trolley that runs the length of it. You ride it to breakfast and watch the sun drop into the Gulf at night. Winters and weekends are busy, homes run about $1.15M, and the island floods in storms.

Navarre Beach is the quiet stretch of the Emerald Coast, between Pensacola Beach and Destin. The fishing pier runs 1,545 feet into the Gulf and is billed as the longest on it. There's one causeway on and off, and it's busy April through October.

Also worth a look: Dunedin sits next to both Caladesi Island and Honeymoon Island, which Tripadvisor ranked tenth in 2026. So do Captiva Island, Sanibel Island, Pensacola Beach and New Smyrna Beach.

The lists rate visits, not lives

Dr. Beach is Stephen Leatherman, who rates public beaches on 50 criteria. Tripadvisor's awards come from a year of traveler reviews. Neither one asks what it's like on a Tuesday in August with a mortgage, which is why we pair them with our own scores.

Before you buy near a famous beach

  • Price the insurance first. Nearly every town on this list sits on a barrier island. Get wind and flood quotes for the exact address before an offer. Our insurance guide covers what to ask.
  • Count the bridges. One bridge on and off changes errands, evacuations and spring break.
  • Visit in season. Drive the causeway on a sunny Saturday in March.
  • Look one town over. The beach is the same. The parking and price often aren't.
  • Read the hurricane season checklist before you sign.

Pick the beach you want to visit every week. Then live where that's easy.
