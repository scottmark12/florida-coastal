Sarasota vs St. Petersburg: Two Arts Towns on Two Different Bays

Both have a walkable downtown, serious museums and a full calendar. The beach, the nights and the price are where they split.

Town vs Town·By Florida Coastal·Oct 6, 2026·3 min read

These two come up together all the time, and on paper they look alike. Each is a small Gulf coast city with a downtown you can walk, more arts than its size should allow and beaches a short drive away. The short version: Sarasota is closer to the sand and quieter at night. St. Petersburg is bigger, later and closer to a major airport.

Price

Redfin put the median sale price in Sarasota at about $520,000 over the three months ending August 2026. In St. Petersburg it was near $430,000 in summer 2026. In both, homes close to downtown and the water run far higher, and both have neighborhoods a few miles inland that cost much less.

The arts

Sarasota is the performing arts town. The Opera House on Pineapple Avenue anchors a downtown with a ballet company, an orchestra and several professional theaters, and The Ringling up the bayfront covers 66 acres with an art museum, a circus museum and a mansion.

St. Petersburg is the museum and mural town. The Dalí, the Museum of Fine Arts, the Chihuly Collection and the James Museum are all downtown, the Mahaffey and the Palladium book shows, and the SHINE Mural Festival keeps adding to the walls.

The beach

Sarasota wins this one. Lido Beach is about ten minutes over the Ringling Bridge, and Siesta Beach is about 20. Bridge traffic adds time in winter.

St. Petersburg faces Tampa Bay, so the beaches in town are small bay beaches. The Gulf sand at St. Pete Beach, Treasure Island and Fort De Soto is a 20 to 30 minute drive, and the SunRunner bus runs from downtown out to the beaches.

Nights out

Sarasota is decent, not wild. Main Street and Lemon Avenue have bars and taprooms with live music on weekends, and the city throws free Fresh Fridays block parties.

St. Petersburg stays up. Jannus Live has put on open air concerts in a downtown courtyard for more than 40 years, bars and breweries run for blocks along Central Avenue, and last call is 3am.

Getting around and getting out

Both downtowns are compact and both have a long trail. The Legacy Trail runs 18.5 paved miles from Sarasota to Venice, and the Pinellas Trail starts in downtown St. Petersburg and heads north for dozens of miles.

For flights, Sarasota Bradenton (SRQ) is about 15 minutes from downtown Sarasota. From St. Petersburg, Tampa International is about 25 to 30 minutes, with far more nonstop routes.

What to watch

  • Sarasota took storm surge from Helene and Milton in 2024, and US 41 and the Ringling Bridge back up in winter. Summer is noticeably calmer.
  • St. Petersburg floods in low lying neighborhoods, so flood insurance is a real cost there. Downtown is busy all year.

How to choose

  • Lean Sarasota if you want the beach close, theater and opera on a weeknight, and a town that slows down in summer.
  • Lean St. Petersburg if you want more going on later, a bigger airport nearby and a little more house for the money.

Still torn? Look at the smaller towns near each: Venice and Siesta Key by Sarasota, Gulfport and Dunedin by St. Petersburg.
