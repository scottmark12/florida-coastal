The Most Walkable Florida Beach Towns (2026)

Twenty seven towns where coffee, dinner and the water fit inside a short walk, ranked strictly by our walkability score. South Beach comes out on top.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 3, 20266 min read

We score every one of the 167 towns on Florida's coast the same way, from things we can count and check, never reviews or ads.

For Walkable we ranked every town by our walkability score: how many daily needs sit within a short walk of the center of town. Twenty seven towns make our Walkable list, and these are the 10 that rank highest. Every score below shows where the town ranks out of all 167 (here's how we score).

Before you choose a town

  • Walk to the grocery store from the exact address, at 2 in the afternoon in August. You'll live there in August.
  • Price the center, not the town. The blocks closest to downtown cost more than the town median.
  • Look at condos if a house in the walkable core is out of reach, and read the fees and reserves. The six bills nobody puts on the listing helps.
  • Check the flood zone. Old towns sit close to the water. See our insurance guide and the hurricane season checklist.
