The Best Florida Beach Towns for Island Life (2026)

Real island towns, from the Keys to Cape San Blas, ranked on their beaches, life on the water and charm. Captiva comes out on top.

By Mark and Betsy ScottNov 3, 20266 min read

Plenty of towns sit near the water. These are the ones where you cross a bridge to get home. We score every one of the 167 towns on Florida's coast the same way, from things we can count and check, never reviews or ads.

For Island Life we kept the towns on a real island, from the Keys and the Gulf barrier islands to Amelia Island and Cape San Blas, then ranked them equally on beaches, life on the water and charm. Twenty two towns make our Island Life list, and these are the 10 that rank highest. Every score below shows where the town ranks out of all 167 (here's how we score).

Before you buy on an island

  • Learn the way off. Several of these islands have one causeway or one bridge. Drive it at rush hour and know the evacuation route.
  • Check the elevation and flood zone of the exact home, and what the last storm did.
  • Ask about condo reserves and assessments if you're buying a unit.
  • Pick your sunset. Gulf islands face the sunset, Atlantic islands the sunrise. Gulf, Atlantic or Panhandle? lays out the differences.
  • Read the hurricane season checklist.
