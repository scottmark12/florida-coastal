About Anna Maria Island

Anna Maria Island is seven miles of beach split into three small cities: Anna Maria at the quiet north end, Holmes Beach in the middle and Bradenton Beach to the south. There are no high rises, a free trolley runs the length of the island, and most people get around by bike or golf cart.

It's expensive, much of the housing is vacation rentals, and the 2024 storms hit the island hard. Bridge traffic to the mainland gets heavy in season.

Dining and artsTop 44%

Good seafood and casual spots along Pine Avenue, Gulf Drive and Bridge Street, a handful of galleries, and community theater from the Island Players, who opened their 77th season in 2025. It is a relaxed, early beach town scene.

Active outdoorsBottom 46%

Outdoor life is the daily routine here. Beach walks out to Bean Point, bike rides the length of the island and kayaking the bay side mangroves. Robinson Preserve, with miles of trails, is a few minutes over the Manatee Avenue bridge.

Peace and quietBottom 47%

Quiet in the ways that count, with no high rises, low speed limits and dark, calm streets at night. The catch is winter and spring, when cars back up at the bridges and beach parking fills by mid morning.

NightlifeBottom 36%

Evenings are sunset, dinner and maybe a band at a beach bar. Karaoke at the Freckled Fin runs until 11:30 on Mondays, which is about as late as the island goes. Bridge Street at the south end is the liveliest stop.
