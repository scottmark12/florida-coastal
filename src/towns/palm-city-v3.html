About Palm City

Palm City sits across the St. Lucie River from Stuart. It feels green and spread out, with gated golf communities, canal homes, horse country on bigger lots and plenty of youth sports. Families like the schools and the parks, and retirees like the golf and the quiet. Downtown Stuart is one bridge away.

Traffic on the bridges has grown, and you'll drive for almost everything. River water quality can suffer after Lake Okeechobee releases, and hurricanes are a factor here like anywhere on the coast. West Palm Beach airport is about 40 minutes by the Turnpike or Interstate 95.

Common questions

Is Palm City walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. Not a walking town. Neighborhoods have sidewalks and paths in places, but shopping and restaurants sit in plazas along the main roads.

Does Palm City have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No beach in town. Stuart Beach on Hutchinson Island is about 20 to 25 minutes east, through Stuart.

Is Palm City affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $597K median sale (Resideline, Oct 2026). Mid range for Martin County, with everything from older villas to large riverfront homes. Published medians vary a lot depending on the source, so check current numbers with a local agent.

What is the nightlife like in Palm City?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Dinner at a neighborhood favorite in the plazas off Martin Downs, maybe a drink at a tavern after. On a nice night you might watch the sunset from the river at Leighton Park first.

How busy does Palm City get?

It is quiet most of the year. Calm residential streets, with traffic on the main roads and the bridges at rush hour. The farm areas to the west feel more rural and spread out.

Is Palm City good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The St. Lucie River and its canals run through town, and many homes have docks. Leighton Park has boat ramps and a pier, and the inlet is a run downriver.

Which airport is closest to Palm City?

Palm Beach is about 40 minutes south on the Turnpike or Interstate 95. Orlando is under two hours up the Turnpike.

What should I know before moving to Palm City?

The main things to plan for: River water quality after Lake Okeechobee releases, hurricane exposure, growing traffic on the bridges.
