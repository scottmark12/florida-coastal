About Palm Harbor

Palm Harbor feels established and green, with live oaks, golf courses and neighborhoods that have been here for decades. The old downtown sits right on the Pinellas Trail, so a lot of daily life happens on a bike. To the west, Ozona and Crystal Beach are small, old Florida villages on St. Joseph Sound, with docks, tin roofs and boats. Families come for the schools and parks, and retirees come for the golf, the trail and the calm.

The trade offs: the waterfront pockets in Ozona and Crystal Beach are in flood zones, so check elevation and insurance first. US 19 runs through the middle of town and is slow and busy. The Gulf beaches are a short drive away, not in town. Prices are higher than in Pasco to the north, and many neighborhoods have HOAs.

Common questions

Is Palm Harbor walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Better than most of the area. Old downtown is small and walkable, and the Pinellas Trail lets you bike to Dunedin and Tarpon Springs. Outside that you drive.

Does Palm Harbor have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. No big beach in town, but Honeymoon Island State Park is about ten minutes away with a long natural beach, and Caladesi Island is a ferry ride from there. Pop Stansell Park has a small pier for sunsets.

Is Palm Harbor affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $485K median across Palm Harbor communities (Momentum, Jun 2026). Middle of the road for Pinellas. The overall median sits in the mid $300Ks because of condos, while houses near the water or in golf communities run much higher.

What is the nightlife like in Palm Harbor?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A beer at a brewery off the trail, dinner on the patio downtown, then a walk out to the Pop Stansell pier for the last light. Weekends pick up a bit, but it stays relaxed.

How busy does Palm Harbor get?

It is quiet most of the year. Calm, tree lined and residential, especially west of Alt 19. The busy part is the US 19 corridor on the east side.

Is Palm Harbor good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Good. Ozona and Crystal Beach sit right on St. Joseph Sound with marinas and docks, and Lake Tarpon on the east side is a big freshwater lake for bass fishing and skiing.

Which airport is closest to Palm Harbor?

Tampa International (TPA) is about 30 minutes across the Courtney Campbell or via the Veterans. It is a major airport with lots of nonstops.

What should I know before moving to Palm Harbor?

The main things to plan for: Waterfront pockets like Ozona and Crystal Beach are in flood zones; US 19 traffic; beaches are a short drive, not in town.
