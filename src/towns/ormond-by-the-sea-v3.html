About Ormond by the Sea

Ormond by the Sea is a quiet beach community on the barrier island north of Ormond Beach. Small streets of beach cottages run between the ocean and the Halifax River, with condos along A1A and homes with docks on the river side. People fish, kayak, walk the beach and ride bikes, and a lot of neighbors are retirees who have been here for years. Head north and the houses give way to state parks and dunes.

The trade offs: A1A has washed out in past storms, though a new buried seawall was finished around early 2026. Hurricane and flood exposure are part of living on the island. There are few shops on the peninsula, so most errands mean a drive over the bridge. Daytona event weeks bring extra traffic.

A week here

Most morningsWalk or bike A1A north toward North Peninsula State Park, where the houses stop and it's just dunes.
Late afternoonFish or paddle off the dock on the Halifax River while the water's calm.
SaturdayA morning at Bicentennial Park on the river side, where neighbors walk their dogs and swap fishing reports.
Saturday nightLagerheads Bar and Grill on A1A. Grouper Reuben, mussels, cold beer and live music several nights a week.
SundayPaddle the Tomoka River at Tomoka State Park, just across the Halifax.
