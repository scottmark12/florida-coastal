About Edgewater

Edgewater sits on the Indian River Lagoon just south of New Smyrna Beach. Life here is about the water without the beach town prices: people fish off the docks, paddle the mangrove trails, walk the riverfront and boat down to Mosquito Lagoon. Most homes are single family houses on big, simple lots, and the town draws retirees, working families and people who want a garage for the boat. The beach in New Smyrna is a short drive over the bridge.

The trade offs: Florida Shores, the biggest neighborhood, has flooded again and again, from Ian in 2022 and Milton in 2024 to a rain and king tide flood in October 2025. The city paused new development while it works on stormwater. There's no beach in town, and shopping and dining are thinner than in New Smyrna. Daytona's airport is about 30 minutes away and Orlando is about an hour.

A week here

Most morningsCoffee, then a walk along the river walk while the mullet jump and the pelicans work the lagoon.
Late afternoonPut the kayak in at Menard May Park and paddle the mangrove trails along the Indian River.
SaturdayRun the boat down to Mosquito Lagoon with a couple of friends and fish the flats.

Common questions

Is Edgewater walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. The River Walk along Riverside Drive is lovely for a stroll or bike ride. The rest of the city is spread out, so you drive for groceries and errands.

Does Edgewater have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No beach in Edgewater itself. New Smyrna Beach is about 15 minutes away, and the quieter Canaveral National Seashore is a bit farther south.

Is Edgewater affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $259K across the neighborhoods Momentum tracks (Momentum, Jun 2026). One of the more affordable spots in this part of Volusia. Redfin listed a median asking price around $318K in Sept 2026, though flood risk and insurance matter a lot in some neighborhoods.

What is the nightlife like in Edgewater?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Sunset on the River Walk, a simple seafood dinner, and an early night. When friends want more, you all drive up to Flagler Avenue in New Smyrna.

How busy does Edgewater get?

It is quiet most of the year. Calm most of the time. U.S. 1 is the busy spine, but the neighborhoods off it are quiet and residential.

Is Edgewater good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. A real strength. The Indian River Lagoon is right in town, with launches at Menard May and Kennedy parks, marked paddling trails and local guides for redfish, snook and trout.

Which airport is closest to Edgewater?

Daytona Beach (DAB) is about 30 minutes away with a few airlines. Orlando (MCO) is about an hour and has far more direct flights.

What should I know before moving to Edgewater?

The main things to plan for: Repeated neighborhood flooding in Florida Shores (Ian in 2022, Milton in 2024, a rain and king tide flood in Oct 2025), a development pause tied to stormwater, no beach in town.
