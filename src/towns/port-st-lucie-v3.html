About Port St. Lucie

Port St. Lucie is one of the fastest growing cities in Florida. Most of it is newer single family neighborhoods, planned communities like Tradition and St. Lucie West, and a lot of golf. It draws families, remote workers and retirees who want a newer house for less than the coast charges. The Mets hold spring training here every March, and the beaches on Hutchinson Island are about 20 minutes east.

The trade offs: fast growth means traffic, construction and crowded main roads, and some areas still feel unfinished. It's inland, so you'll drive to the beach. Many newer communities carry HOA dues, and Palm Beach airport is about 50 minutes away.

Where it shines

AffordabilityTop 32%

One of the more affordable cities near the southeast coast. Newer houses here cost noticeably less than similar ones in Martin or Palm Beach County, and being inland helps on flood insurance. Check HOA and district fees in the newer areas.

NightlifeBottom 46%

Mostly restaurants, sports bars and breweries spread across shopping plazas, with a band here and there on weekends. There is no one nightlife street, and most places are quiet by 10 or 11. Stuart and Fort Pierce have more going on downtown.

Where it falls short

BeachesBottom 26%

No beach in the city. The nearest sand is on Hutchinson Island, about 20 to 30 minutes east depending on where you start, by way of Jensen Beach or Fort Pierce. The beaches there are public and uncrowded, so it works as a weekend habit.

Coastal charmBottom 20%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Airport accessBottom 20%

The Palm Beach airport is about 50 minutes to an hour south on I-95 or the Turnpike, and Orlando (MCO) is under two hours north. Both are doable, neither is quick.

Peace and quietBottom 19%

Neighborhood streets are quiet and mostly residential. The catch is traffic. The city has grown fast, and Port St. Lucie Boulevard, U.S. 1 and the roads around Tradition and St. Lucie West back up at rush hour and stay busy all year.

Active outdoorsBottom 19%

Decent and close to home. The North Fork has a boardwalk and a paddling trail, the city keeps a botanical garden on the river, and a large state preserve of marsh and flatwoods runs along the east side. Golf courses are all over.

WalkabilityBottom 18%

Built for cars. Homes, shops and restaurants are spread over a very large area with no traditional downtown, and almost every errand is a drive. A few planned centers like Tradition are walkable once you park there.

Dining and artsBottom 12%

Plenty of places to eat, but mostly chains and plaza restaurants with some good independents mixed in. Arts are thin. For a historic theater, galleries or a restaurant row, you go to Stuart or Fort Pierce.

Meet peopleBottom 8%

A big, spread out city with a fair amount of organized activity if you look for it, like city rec leagues, golf leagues, community events in Tradition and Mets spring training games every February and March. You have to drive to all of it.

On the waterBottom 8%

The North Fork of the St. Lucie River winds through the east side, a quiet, shaded stretch for kayaks and small boats, with a few city parks and ramps along it. Many canals are freshwater drainage, not boating water, and the ocean is a long run by river.

Common questions

Is Port St. Lucie walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. Built for cars. Homes, shops and restaurants are spread over a very large area with no traditional downtown, and almost every errand is a drive. A few planned centers like Tradition are walkable once you park there.

Does Port St. Lucie have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No beach in the city. The nearest sand is on Hutchinson Island, about 20 to 30 minutes east depending on where you start, by way of Jensen Beach or Fort Pierce. The beaches there are public and uncrowded, so it works as a weekend habit.

Is Port St. Lucie affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $395K median sale over the past year (Momentum, Nov 2026). One of the more affordable cities near the southeast coast. What is the nightlife like in Port St. Lucie?

How busy does Port St. Lucie get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Neighborhood streets are quiet and mostly residential. The catch is traffic. The city has grown fast, and Port St. Lucie Boulevard, U.S. 1 and the roads around Tradition and St. Lucie West back up at rush hour and stay busy all year.
