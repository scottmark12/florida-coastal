About Hudson

Hudson is a laid back Pasco County town where a lot of life happens on the canals. Many homes near the coast have a dock out back and a short run to the Gulf, and the little strip by Hudson Beach has bars and seafood spots where people know each other. Inland, quieter neighborhoods have more affordable houses. It draws retirees, boaters and families who work around Tampa.

The honest part: waterfront and canal homes flooded badly in Helene in 2024, so flood zones, elevation and insurance come first. US 19 is the main road and the traffic can be heavy. The beach here is small, and if you want a big white sand beach you'll drive south.

Common questions

Is Hudson walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Mostly car dependent. You can walk the little Hudson Beach strip of bars, the inn and the ice cream shop, but groceries and errands are along US 19.

Does Hudson have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. Small. Hudson Beach at Robert J. Strickland Memorial Park is a short local strip, and SunWest Park has a man made white sand beach. It is fine for a swim, but it is not a classic Gulf beach.

Is Hudson affordable?

Yes. It is one of the more affordable spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $274K median sale (Broker One, Nov 2026). One of the more affordable coastal towns near Tampa, with a typical value in the high $200Ks. Canal homes cost more, and flood insurance is the wildcard after Helene.

What is the nightlife like in Hudson?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Sunset on the water at Sam's or the Inn on the Gulf, with a grouper sandwich and a cold drink. Weekends sometimes bring live music on the deck. Most of the strip quiets down by 9.

How busy does Hudson get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Calm on the canals and side streets, busy on US 19. Winter brings seasonal residents and summer weekends fill SunWest Park, but most neighborhoods are peaceful.

Is Hudson good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Lots of canal homes with docks lead out to the Gulf through the Hudson channel, and the flats and offshore fishing are close. SunWest Park adds paddling and an aqua park.

Which airport is closest to Hudson?

Tampa (TPA) is about 50 minutes south via the Suncoast Parkway, and St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) is about 45 minutes. TPA has lots of nonstops.

What should I know before moving to Hudson?

The main things to plan for: Waterfront and canal homes flooded badly in Helene (2024), heavy traffic on US 19. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
