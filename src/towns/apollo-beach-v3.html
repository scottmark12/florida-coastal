About Apollo Beach

Apollo Beach is a south shore community on Tampa Bay, built around miles of canals. A lot of homes have a dock and a boat lift, and plenty of people take the boat to dinner instead of the car. Newer master planned neighborhoods sit on the east side of US 41, so you get a mix of retirees, boaters and young families.

The trade offs are real. Canal homes sit low and storm surge is the big worry, so flood insurance and elevation matter here. Commuting to Tampa means I 75 or US 41, and both get backed up at rush hour. There is a small bay beach, but the Gulf beaches are about an hour away.

Where it falls short

Peace and quietBottom 32%

The canal streets are calm, and town sees few visitors outside manatee season. The noise is commuter traffic. US 41 and the I-75 ramps back up at rush hour, and the area keeps adding homes.

BeachesBottom 22%

Despite the name, the beach is the weak spot. There is a small strip of bay sand at the county nature preserve at the end of Surfside Boulevard, good for a sunset and not for swimming. Gulf beaches are about an hour away.

Meet peopleBottom 17%

A modest but steady calendar. The Manatee Viewing Center is a daily gathering spot from November to mid April, boating is the common ground on the canals, and the SouthShore Chamber lists festivals and parades across Apollo Beach and Ruskin.

WalkabilityBottom 13%

Coastal charmBottom 9%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Active outdoorsBottom 8%

The Manatee Viewing Center is free from November 1 to April 15, with a 900 foot tidal walk, a 50 foot tower and a coastal trail. E.G. Simmons Conservation Park and Little Manatee River State Park are a short drive south.

Dining and artsBottom 7%

A few waterfront seafood spots and casual places in the plazas along US 41. There are no theaters or galleries to speak of in town. The Firehouse Cultural Center in Ruskin, about 10 minutes south, has plays, comedy and jazz.

AffordabilityTop 47%

Mid range for the Florida coast. Homes in the newer inland subdivisions cost far less than canal front houses with a boat lift, and waterfront owners should budget for flood and wind insurance.

NightlifeBottom 42%

Evenings here are dockside. Circles Waterfront at Lands End Marina serves until 9 most nights and 10 on Fridays and Saturdays, and that is about as late as town goes. For a real night out you drive to Tampa.
