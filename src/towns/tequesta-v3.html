About Tequesta

Tequesta is a small village at the top of Palm Beach County. Life here is about the water: boats tied up behind houses, paddling on the Loxahatchee, and the beach just across the bridge on Jupiter Island. It's mostly retirees, boaters and families who want a calm place close to Jupiter's restaurants and shops.

The trade offs: there's not much commerce in the village itself, so you'll drive to Jupiter for a lot of things. US 1 gets crowded in season. Oceanfront condos on Beach Road carry real hurricane exposure, and condo fees and insurance there can add up. The airport is about 30 minutes away.

Where it shines

Active outdoorsTop 28%

Hard to beat. Jonathan Dickinson State Park is ten minutes north, Blowing Rocks Preserve is on Jupiter Island, and the Loxahatchee is one of the prettiest paddles in South Florida.

On the waterTop 27%

This is a boater's town. The Loxahatchee River, the Intracoastal and the Jupiter Inlet are right there, and on weekends half the village seems to be anchored at the sandbar just past the lighthouse.

Where it falls short

AffordabilityBottom 27%

Not cheap. Typical values sit around the mid $600Ks, waterfront homes run far higher, and oceanfront condo owners should budget for wind insurance and building assessments.

NightlifeBottom 21%

Pretty quiet after dark. Tequesta Brewing and the new smokehouse next door are the main hangout, and for a real night out people head ten minutes south to Jupiter.
