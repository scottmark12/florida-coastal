About Merritt Island

Merritt Island sits between the Indian River and the Banana River, and a lot of life here happens on the water. Many homes are on canals with their own docks, and people fish, boat and paddle right from the backyard. It's a settled, family friendly place with big oak trees, and the north end is wild refuge land next to the space center.

The trade offs: much of the island is low lying, and some areas flood in heavy rain and storm surge, so check flood zones and get insurance quotes early. It's car dependent, and Orlando's airport is about 45 minutes away. Brevard gets hurricanes, and the lagoon's water quality has had rough years.

Where it shines

On the waterTop 26%

This is the draw. Many homes sit on canals with docks, the Indian River is on one side and the Banana River on the other, and boats can reach the ocean through the Port Canaveral locks. Kayak launches and ramps are all over the island.

Active outdoorsTop 35%

Among the best on the Space Coast. Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge covers the north end with marsh, scrub and birding drives, and the Kennedy Space Center visitor complex is next door. You need a refuge pass for Black Point Wildlife Drive and some boat ramps.

Where it falls short

NightlifeBottom 30%

Quiet. There are a few waterfront bars and grills and some local wine and coffee spots, and that is mostly it. For a real night out people go to Cocoa Village, Cocoa Beach or Port Canaveral, all 10 to 20 minutes away.

Coastal charmBottom 20%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Common questions

Is Merritt Island walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. Not walkable. It is an unincorporated area built around the car, with long blocks and errands spread along the main roads. People walk and bike in their own neighborhoods and on the refuge roads.

Does Merritt Island have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No ocean beach on the island. Cocoa Beach and Cape Canaveral are about 15 to 20 minutes east over the causeway, and Playalinda Beach in the national seashore is a longer drive north through the refuge.
