About Hobe Sound

Hobe Sound is a quiet village between Stuart and Jupiter. Much of the land around it is protected, from Jonathan Dickinson State Park to the Hobe Sound National Wildlife Refuge and Blowing Rocks Preserve. Life here is about the outdoors: beach walks, hiking, paddling and golf. Across the Intracoastal sits Jupiter Island, one of the most private enclaves in Florida.

Shopping is limited, so bigger errands mean Stuart or Jupiter. Federal Highway gets heavy in season. Hurricanes are a factor, and insurance in Martin County is high. West Palm Beach airport is about 35 minutes away.

Common questions

Is Hobe Sound walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. The little downtown along Bridge Road and Old Dixie has a few shops and murals within a short walk. Most daily life still means a short drive.

Does Hobe Sound have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Hobe Sound Beach on Jupiter Island is clean and uncrowded with a lifeguard tower, and Blowing Rocks Preserve has limestone shelves where waves shoot up through the rock. Both are a few minutes over the bridge.

Is Hobe Sound affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $515K median sale (Redfin, Sep 2026). Mid range for this stretch of coast, with a recent median near $515K. Homes on Jupiter Island across the water are in a different price world.

What is the nightlife like in Hobe Sound?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A beach sunset off Bridge Road, then a casual dinner and maybe live music at Harry's or a local brewery. Most nights are early and easy.

How busy does Hobe Sound get?

It is quiet most of the year. Calm and leafy. Federal Highway is the busy line through town, but the neighborhoods, the island and the refuge are very quiet.

Is Hobe Sound good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The Indian River Lagoon, the Loxahatchee River and Jupiter Inlet are all close. Boaters, anglers, kayakers and kiteboarders all have room here.

Which airport is closest to Hobe Sound?

Palm Beach is about 35 minutes south and the closest real airport. Orlando is a little over two hours.

What should I know before moving to Hobe Sound?

The main things to plan for: Hurricane exposure, limited shopping, heavy traffic on Federal Highway in season. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
