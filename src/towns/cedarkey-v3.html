About Cedar Key

Cedar Key is a small island town at the end of State Road 24, about an hour from Gainesville. Life is slow and close to the water. People fish off the pier, paddle around the little islands, farm clams in the shallows and walk everywhere downtown. The crowd is a mix of old fishing families, artists and retirees who wanted quiet over convenience.

Be honest with yourself about the storms. Recent hurricanes hit Cedar Key hard, and the town is still rebuilding, with a few restaurants and businesses still missing. The grocery store was destroyed and the nearest full grocery is about 40 minutes away in Chiefland. Check flood zones and building elevation on every home, and expect a long drive for doctors and big errands.

Where it falls short

A short list. 83 West on Dock Street, clam chowder at Tony's and the Cedar Keyhole Artist Co-Op for browsing. The town is still down a few restaurants since Helene, so the open ones get busy.

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. A small patch of sand at City Park is the town beach. It works for wading and sunsets, but the water is shallow and marshy and this is not a long white sand shoreline.
