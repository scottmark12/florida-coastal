About Shalimar

Shalimar is a small town on Choctawhatchee Bay just north of Fort Walton Beach. The streets are shady, a lot of homes back up to a bayou, and boats are part of daily life. Neighbors are a mix of retirees, Eglin families and people who've lived here for years. It feels quiet and settled, with Fort Walton Beach and the airport both close by.

The trade offs: Eglin Parkway traffic at rush hour, jet noise from Eglin, and storm surge risk on low bayfront lots. There's very little shopping or dining in town itself, and the Gulf beaches are a drive over the bridges. Homes don't come up often, so good ones move.

Dining and artsBottom 27%

Limited in town. A handful of casual spots sit along Eglin Parkway, and Fort Walton Beach, Niceville and Destin cover everything else within 10 to 25 minutes.

Meet peopleBottom 15%

Small town social. The town is under a thousand people, so you get to know neighbors on the water, at the golf club and at the volunteer run library. Bigger clubs and events are in Fort Walton Beach and Niceville.

Active outdoorsBottom 6%

Golf by the bay at Shalimar Pointe, sailing and paddling on the bayous, and easy reach to Okaloosa Island and Eglin's big tracts of open land.

NightlifeBottom 7%

Barely any. Shalimar is mostly homes, and evenings are dinner on the dock or a quick drive to Fort Walton Beach, about ten minutes south, for bars and live music.
