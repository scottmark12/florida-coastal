About Indialantic

Indialantic is a small beach town on the barrier island across from Melbourne. People walk to the ocean, surf before work and know the folks at the surf shops by name. Fifth Avenue has the coffee, the shops and the restaurants, and you can bike almost everywhere. It suits retirees who want an easy beach life and families who want a small town feel close to the city.

The trade offs are real. This is a barrier island, so hurricanes and storm surge matter, and flood zones vary street by street. Homes near the ocean cost a lot more than on the mainland. The airport is close, about 15 minutes, and Melbourne has the hospitals and big shopping.
