About Indian Rocks Beach

Indian Rocks Beach is a small, low key town on the Pinellas barrier islands. It's mostly cottages, older beach houses and small condos, with public beach access at the end of nearly every street. People walk to the beach, ride bikes along Gulf Boulevard and know the folks at the coffee spots by name. It's a mix of retirees, second home owners and families who wanted a slower beach town.

Helene's surge flooded much of the island in 2024 and some homes are still being rebuilt or raised. Flood insurance and elevation matter a lot here. Prices per square foot are high for small, older homes, and Tampa's airport is about 40 minutes away in good traffic.

Common questions

Is Indian Rocks Beach walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. The island is only a few blocks wide, with public beach accesses at the street ends. Kolb Park, the Beach Art Center and a few restaurants are walkable, but groceries and errands mean a car or the beach trolley.

Does Indian Rocks Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Public accesses at the street ends, plus a county beach park at 1700 Gulf Boulevard with paid parking. The sand is soft and white, and it is far less crowded than Clearwater Beach up the road.
