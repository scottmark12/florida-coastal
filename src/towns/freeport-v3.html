About Freeport

Freeport sits on the north side of Choctawhatchee Bay in Walton County. It's a place for families and younger retirees who want a newer house with a yard, room for the boat and a lower price than the beach side. Weekends mean the bay, the bayous and quick trips down US 331 to the Gulf.

The town is growing fast, so expect new construction, more rooftops each year and summer traffic on US 331. The Gulf beaches are 25 to 30 minutes away, not around the corner. Some low spots near the bayous can flood, so check the flood zone on any home you like.

BeachesBottom 28%

Airport accessBottom 29%

Common questions

Is Freeport walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. Car dependent. Planned communities like Hammock Bay have miles of trails and sidewalks, but errands, dining and the beach all mean driving.

Does Freeport have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No beach in town. The 30A beaches are about 25 to 30 minutes south over the bay bridge on 331, which is easy in winter and slow on summer weekends.

Is Freeport affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $385K median sale (Momentum, Nov 2026). What is the nightlife like in Freeport?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. A casual dinner close to home, maybe a pub with a game on, and an early night. When friends come to town you drive down 331 for sunset on the beach. Weekends are more about the boat, the ballfields or the back porch.

How busy does Freeport get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Quieter than the beach, but busy in its own way. Construction is everywhere and US 331 traffic no longer thins out midday. Off the highway the neighborhoods are calm.

Is Freeport good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. Good for the bay. LaGrange Bayou and Choctawhatchee Bay have launches at Grady Brown Park and Eden Gardens, and the Choctawhatchee River is close for fishing and paddling. Gulf boating means a trip around to Destin.
