About Cortez

Cortez is a small village on Sarasota Bay, just before the bridge to Anna Maria Island. Fish houses, crab traps and working boats still line the water, and many families here go back generations. The streets are quiet, neighbors know each other, and most homes are modest cottages, older houses and canal homes. Bradenton's shops and doctors are a short drive east.

Almost the whole village is low and close to the water. Redfin rates flood risk here as extreme, and storm surge is a real concern, so check flood zones and insurance before you fall for a cottage. The Cortez Bridge is set to be replaced, with work running to about 2029, so expect delays on the way to the island. It's quiet most of the year, but the fishing festival weekend brings big crowds.

A week here

Most morningsCoffee on the docks, watching the fishing boats head out past the regulars mending gear.
Late afternoonWalk the trail through the FISH Preserve, the protected bay land on the east side of the village.
SaturdayRun the boat across Sarasota Bay to the beaches of Anna Maria Island, just over the bridge.
Saturday nightStar Fish Company on the docks. Fresh local seafood at outdoor tables right beside the boats, from a fish house that's been here since the early 1920s. Locals keep going because it's simple and the fish is right off the water.
SundayA slow walk past the old schoolhouse that's now the Florida Maritime Museum, stopping to talk with neighbors on their porches.

Where it falls short

BeachesBottom 20%

No beach in the village. Cortez Beach and Coquina Beach are right across the bridge in Bradenton Beach, about five minutes by car when traffic is light.

NightlifeBottom 18%

Nights end early. Swordfish Grill & Tiki Bar has local musicians on its deck over the bay but closes at 9, and the village is dark soon after. For anything later, Bridge Street in Bradenton Beach is a few minutes over the bridge.

Dining and artsBottom 18%

Seafood straight off the boats at a few dockside fish houses, among them Swordfish Grill & Tiki Bar, open daily from 11am to 9pm. That is most of it. The Florida Maritime Museum was still closed for storm repairs at last report.

Meet peopleBottom 35%

Small village, short list. The Cortez Commercial Fishing Festival comes every February, a nautical flea market sets up in March, and there is a spring community picnic and a Veterans Day tribute from the village historical society.

Active outdoorsBottom 33%

The 98 acre FISH Preserve wraps the east side of the village in mangroves and is being restored after 2024 storm damage. Sarasota Bay is right there for kayaks, and the Gulf beaches are just over the bridge.
