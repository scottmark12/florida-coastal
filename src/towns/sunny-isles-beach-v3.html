About Sunny Isles Beach

Sunny Isles Beach is a narrow city of high rise condos on the ocean between Bal Harbour and Golden Beach. Life happens on the beach path, in the pools and lobbies of the towers, and along Collins Avenue. It draws buyers from around the world, with a large Russian speaking community and many Latin American and Canadian owners. A few blocks of single family homes sit at the north end in Golden Shores.

The trade offs: Collins Avenue traffic can crawl, and parking takes planning. Condo fees are high, and older buildings may face big assessments under Florida's newer condo safety rules. The whole city is on a barrier island, so storms and flooding are real concerns, and insurance in Miami Dade is some of the costliest in the state.

Where it shines

Dining and artsTop 9%

Collins Avenue packs in restaurants at street level and inside the hotel towers, from quick counters to white tablecloth rooms, and you can eat over the water at Sandbar at Newport Pier. The city adds its Art Uncorked exhibitions and readings.

Meet peopleTop 10%

Most of what is on is run by the city. Bingo every Wednesday at Gateway Center, the Art Uncorked series, movie nights at Gateway Park and volunteer beach cleanups at the pier. Clubs and leagues are thin beyond that.

Airport accessTop 17%

Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about 25 to 30 minutes north and Miami (MIA) about 35 to 40 minutes south. Both depend on how Collins Avenue and the causeways are moving.

WalkabilityTop 23%

The city is one long strip, about two miles of Collins Avenue with the beach on one side. You can walk to Newport Fishing Pier, a park and a plaza for groceries, but Collins is wide and errands farther out need a car or bus.

Where it falls short

On the waterBottom 26%

Newport Fishing Pier is free to walk and charges a fee to fish. There is no public marina in town, so boaters use Bill Bird Marina at Haulover Park just south, with 152 wet slips, a boat ramp and a charter fishing fleet.

Active outdoorsBottom 15%

Parks are small and tidy. Gateway Park has five acres with a stage and butterfly garden, Heritage Park has a dog park and open field, and Town Center Park has a fitness trail. Real trails start at Oleta River State Park across the bridge.

Peace and quietBottom 12%

Collins Avenue carries nearly everything, so it backs up, and there is usually a tower going up somewhere along it. Winter is the busy season. The beach side of the buildings is far calmer than the street side.

AffordabilityBottom 11%

Nearly all housing is in oceanfront and bayfront towers, priced near the top of the Florida coast. Monthly condo fees are high, and older buildings can carry special assessments.

Coastal charmBottom 4%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Common questions

Is Sunny Isles Beach walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. The city is one long strip, about two miles of Collins Avenue with the beach on one side. You can walk to Newport Fishing Pier, a park and a plaza for groceries, but Collins is wide and errands farther out need a car or bus.

Does Sunny Isles Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Two miles of public beach run behind the towers, reached by access paths between the buildings. The sand is wide and the pier breaks up the view. In winter the high rises put the beach in shade by mid afternoon.

Is Sunny Isles Beach affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $2.06M median recorded sale over the past 12 months (Josh Stein Realtor, Oct 2026). Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A night out here is dinner on Collins Avenue and a walk out on Newport Fishing Pier, where Sandbar serves until 10pm. Some evenings there is a concert or a movie at Gateway Park. For anything later you drive to Gulfstream Park or South Beach.

How busy does Sunny Isles Beach get?

It gets busy, especially in season. Collins Avenue carries nearly everything, so it backs up, and there is usually a tower going up somewhere along it. Winter is the busy season. The beach side of the buildings is far calmer than the street side.

Is Sunny Isles Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. Newport Fishing Pier is free to walk and charges a fee to fish. There is no public marina in town, so boaters use Bill Bird Marina at Haulover Park just south, with 152 wet slips, a boat ramp and a charter fishing fleet.
