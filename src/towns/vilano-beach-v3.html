About Vilano Beach

Vilano Beach sits on the north side of the St. Augustine Inlet, with the ocean on one side and the Tolomato River on the other. There's a retro little main street, a fishing pier on the river, and numbered streets of beach houses running up the coast. People fish, surf, paddle and ride bikes over the bridge into old St. Augustine for dinner. It feels like a beach neighborhood more than a town.

The trade offs: it's a barrier island, so hurricanes, flooding and beach erosion after big storms are part of life here. In season, the bridge into St. Augustine backs up. Prices have climbed, and Jacksonville's airport is about 50 minutes away.

Where it shines

On the waterTop 17%

This is a big reason people pick it. You have the ocean, the inlet and the Tolomato River all within a few blocks, a pier and floating dock on the river side, and marinas in St. Augustine minutes away.

Active outdoorsTop 17%

Lots to do outside. The GTM Research Reserve to the north has trails, dunes and marsh, and paddling the river and inlet is right out the door.

Dining and artsTop 17%

A handful of casual spots along Vilano Road plus a few newer places, including a Spanish restaurant at the Hyatt Place. For real variety you cross the bridge into St. Augustine, which has one of the best food scenes on this coast.

Peace and quietTop 24%

Mostly calm. Summer weekends bring beach traffic and the hotels by the bridge fill up, but the streets off Vilano Road are sleepy most of the year.

Where it falls short

Meet peopleBottom 34%

It has a tight community feel for a place this small. Pier events and fishing tournaments, like the Old School Kingfish Shootout, bring people out, and a lot of social life happens in St. Augustine across the bridge.

AffordabilityBottom 23%

Pricier than inland St. Johns County. Supply is small, a mix of older beach cottages, condos and newer elevated homes, and barrier island wind and flood insurance add a lot to the monthly cost.

Airport accessBottom 19%

Jacksonville (JAX) is about 50 minutes north, and it reaches the main hubs. Daytona Beach (DAB) is a bit over an hour south.

Common questions

Is Vilano Beach walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Better than most beach areas here. The little main street, Publix, the pier and the beach sit close together along Vilano Road, so a lot of daily life is walkable or a short bike ride.

Does Vilano Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. A wide, sandy Atlantic beach that the county and the Army Corps renourished in 2024 after years of storm erosion. Vilano Beachfront Park is the main public access.

Is Vilano Beach affordable?
