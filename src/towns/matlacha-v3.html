About Matlacha

Matlacha is a small strip of land on Pine Island Road between Cape Coral and Pine Island, with brightly painted cottages, galleries, fish markets and docks on both sides. People here fish, paddle, crab and keep a boat out back. It draws artists, retirees and longtime fishing families, and it feels more like a village than a suburb. On the second Friday of the month, the art walk keeps shops open late and the street fills with neighbors.

The trade offs are real. Matlacha sits low on the water. It took heavy damage from Hurricane Ian in 2022 and more flooding in 2024, so check the flood zone, the elevation and the roof on any home. The one road through town backs up on busy winter weekends. Groceries, doctors and most errands mean a drive into Cape Coral, and the airport is on the far side of it.

A week here

Most morningsCoffee, then a line in the water off the Matlacha bridge with the same handful of regulars.
Late afternoonPaddle the mangrove islands of Matlacha Pass while the water's calm and the birds come in.
SaturdayRun the boat up Pine Island Sound to Cayo Costa for a swim and a long lunch on the sand.
Saturday nightBlue Dog Bar and Grill on Pine Island Road. Seafood from the island's own fishermen, cold drinks, and crabbers and shrimpers at the bar. That's why locals keep coming back.
SundayA slow drive up Pine Island to the pier in Bokeelia, then mangoes from a roadside stand on the way home.

Where it falls short

NightlifeBottom 34%

Modest and early. Miceli's has live music on the water, and the Blue Dog and Yucatan Waterfront pour drinks over the canal. Bert's Bar, the old late spot, was lost to Ian and has not come back.

Common questions

Is Matlacha walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. The village is one strip along Pine Island Road, well under a mile, and you can walk it end to end past the galleries, cafes and the bridge. For groceries and most errands you drive to the mainland.

Does Matlacha have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No beach here. This is mangroves and flats, not sand. The nearest Gulf beaches are a drive to Sanibel or Fort Myers Beach, or a boat ride out to Cayo Costa.

Is Matlacha affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $494K median sale (Momentum, Nov 2026), based on a small number of sales. What is the nightlife like in Matlacha?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A night out is dinner on a dock. You eat at Miceli's or the Blue Dog, listen to whoever is playing by the water, and watch the bridge lights come on. Most of the village is quiet by 9 or 10.

How busy does Matlacha get?

It is quiet most of the year. Nights are still, with water on both sides. By day Pine Island Road is the only way on or off Pine Island and it runs right through the village, so winter traffic can stack up at the drawbridge.

Is Matlacha good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. Water is the whole point. Matlacha Pass is on both sides, the community park has a boat ramp and fishing pier, people fish from the bridge, and two outfitters rent kayaks for the Great Calusa Blueway.
