About Daytona Beach Shores

Daytona Beach Shores is a small city that is almost all condos, lined up along A1A between Daytona Beach and Ponce Inlet. Life is simple: a walk on the hard sand in the morning, a pool afternoon, a fish off the Sunglow Pier. Many owners are retirees and snowbirds, and it stays calmer than Daytona proper even when the city next door is busy.

The trade offs come with the buildings. Older condo towers face new state inspection and reserve rules, which can mean special assessments, so read the association's finances before you buy. The whole strip sits right on the ocean, so storms, surge and flood insurance are part of the deal, and A1A gets backed up during big Daytona event weeks.

Common questions

Is Daytona Beach Shores walkable?

Yes, a very good one. The beach is the whole point. It is the same wide, hard packed sand as Daytona with more room to spread out, public walkovers and oceanfront parks along A1A, and some stretches where you can still drive on.
