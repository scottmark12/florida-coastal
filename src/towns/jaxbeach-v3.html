About Jacksonville Beach

Jacksonville Beach is the liveliest of the beach towns east of Jacksonville. Mornings start with surfers by the pier and joggers on the hard sand, and evenings fill up with patios, live music and families walking downtown. It's a mix of young professionals, Navy families, retirees and people who commute into the city, and it feels like a real town year round, not just a resort.

The trade offs are crowds and noise near downtown on summer weekends, and hurricane exposure on a barrier island. Homes close to the ocean or the Intracoastal can sit in flood zones, so check insurance before you fall for a house. The airport is about 40 minutes away, and the bridges get busy at rush hour.

Where it shines

NightlifeTop 7%

The blocks around the pier are the late night center of the beaches, with bars, patios and live bands a short walk from each other. The city adds Seawalk Sessions, live music at the oceanfront Seawalk Pavilion.

Meet peopleTop 13%

Lots of ways to plug in. Players by the Sea is in its 61st season and runs on volunteers and open auditions, the Beaches Museum hosts Boardwalk Talks and chapel concerts, and the Beaches Green Market shows up on the Saturday calendar.

WalkabilityTop 14%

From the center you can walk to the beach, the pier, the Seawalk Pavilion, the Beaches Museum on Beach Boulevard and Players by the Sea on 6th Street. The streets are flat, and a beach cruiser handles most errands.

Active outdoorsTop 18%

The outdoors here starts with the ocean, from surfing to beach biking to volleyball on the sand. The city also runs a public course, the Jax Beach Golf Club, and the trails, lake and campground at Hanna Park are a short drive north.

Where it falls short

AffordabilityBottom 35%

Mid range for a Florida beach town. Oceanfront condos and new builds near the sand cost the most, and older houses west of 3rd Street bring the price down. Budget for wind and flood coverage this close to the water.

On the waterBottom 24%

The fishing pier puts you out over the surf without a boat. On the west side of town the Intracoastal has marinas and boat access near the Beach Boulevard bridge, and from there it is a run north to the inlet at Mayport.

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Peace and quietBottom 11%

Common questions

Is Jacksonville Beach walkable?

Yes, for the most part. From the center you can walk to the beach, the pier, the Seawalk Pavilion, the Beaches Museum on Beach Boulevard and Players by the Sea on 6th Street. The streets are flat, and a beach cruiser handles most errands.

Does Jacksonville Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Wide, flat, firm sand with public access at the end of nearly every street. Surfers work the breaks by the pier, lifeguards cover the main stretch in season, and at low tide you can ride a bike on the beach for miles.
