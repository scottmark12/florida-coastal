About Naples

Naples is clean, calm and well kept, with miles of soft beaches, sunsets over the Gulf, and Fifth Avenue and Third Street for dinner and a walk. Golf, boating and the beach fill most days. It draws a lot of retirees and second home owners.

It's expensive near the water, and insurance has gone up after recent storms. Winters are busy with seasonal residents and summers are hot and quiet. The Naples Pier is being rebuilt and is expected to reopen around mid 2027.

Where it shines

BeachesTop 3%

Among the best on the Florida coast. Downtown has beach access at the end of nearly every avenue, and north of it Delnor-Wiggins Pass State Park ranked sixth on Dr. Beach's 2026 national list. Vanderbilt Beach has its own parking garage, and Clam Pass has a mangrove boardwalk with a free tram. The Naples Pier is closed for a rebuild and is expected back around mid 2027.

Meet peopleTop 3%

Gulf Coast Runners puts on group runs and races, including a Thanksgiving 5K at Cambier Park. The Third Street South Farmers Market is a Saturday morning habit, and the Botanical Garden takes volunteers.

Dining and artsTop 4%

Among the best on the Gulf coast. Fifth Avenue South and Third Street South are lined with restaurants and galleries, Gulfshore Playhouse stages professional theater at its Baker Theatre, and Art in the Park has run since 1957.

On the waterTop 6%

Naples Bay opens to the Gulf at Gordon Pass. The City Dock at Crayton Cove has slips, fuel and charter boats, and public ramps sit at Naples Landing and Bayview Park. Watch the strong tidal current in the pass.

Coastal charmTop 11%

A charming town with a real old center, local shops and pretty streets.

WalkabilityTop 15%

Old Naples is a true walking district. Fifth Avenue South runs to the beach, Third Street South and Cambier Park are a few blocks off, and Crayton Cove and the City Dock sit on the bay side. Beyond downtown you need a car.

NightlifeTop 18%

Decent, though not late. Patio bars along Fifth Avenue South and Third Street South have music most evenings, and Evening on Fifth puts bands up and down the sidewalks. Downtown thins out by 11, with a few spots around town open until 1.

Active outdoorsTop 21%

The Gordon River Greenway has boardwalks and paved paths through mangroves a few minutes from downtown, next to Baker Park. Naples Botanical Garden is here too, and the Rookery Bay reserve starts just south of town.

Where it falls short

Peace and quietBottom 36%

Calm on a summer evening and crowded from January through April, when Fifth Avenue parking fills and US 41 crawls. The side streets of Old Naples stay hushed even then.

AffordabilityBottom 23%

Old Naples and anything near the beach rank with the priciest addresses in Florida. Condos and homes east of US 41 cost far less, though still above the state median, and flood insurance is a factor near the bay and Gulf.

About average for 1 more: airport access
Airport accessTop 49%

Southwest Florida International (RSW) is about 40 minutes north on I-75. Naples Airport in town handles private and charter flights only. Fort Lauderdale and Miami are roughly two hours east across Alligator Alley.
