About Lauderdale by the Sea

Lauderdale by the Sea is a small town squeezed between Fort Lauderdale and Pompano Beach. Buildings are kept low, so it feels more like an old beach village than the towers around it. Everything centers on the square where Commercial Boulevard meets the ocean and the fishing pier. People walk to the beach, to dinner and to the pier, and the coral reef sits close enough to shore that you can snorkel it straight from the sand. Many residents are retirees and snowbirds, plus some young families.

The trade offs: the whole town sits right on the ocean, so storm exposure is high and flood insurance matters. A lot of the housing is older condos, which can mean special assessments for repairs. Winter brings visitors and tight parking around the square.

Where it shines

WalkabilityTop 5%

The whole village works on foot. From Anglin's Square you walk to the beach, the restaurants, the shops, El Prado Park and the Sunday market in minutes, and Circuit-By-The-Sea rides cover the rest.

BeachesTop 12%

The coral reef starts about 100 yards off the sand, so you can swim out with a mask and fins. The state named this Florida's Beach Diving Capital, and the SS Copenhagen wreck lies offshore as an underwater preserve.

Meet peopleTop 17%

A decent small town calendar. A Sunday farmers market at El Prado Park from December into May, classes at Jarvis Hall, Friday Night Music in season, beach yoga, and BugFest each July for divers chasing lobster.

Airport accessTop 18%

FLL is about 25 minutes south on US 1 or I-95. The Palm Beach airport is about 50 minutes north, and Miami (MIA) is close to an hour.

Dining and artsTop 21%

A good cluster for a small place. Aruba Beach Cafe and 101 Ocean sit by the sand at the end of Commercial Boulevard, with more spots around Anglin's Square, and Taste of the Beach is the annual food event. Theaters and museums are in Fort Lauderdale.

NightlifeTop 24%

Small but steady. The beach bars around Anglin's Square have live music most nights, and the town runs a Friday Night Music series in season. It winds down far earlier than Fort Lauderdale, which is the point.

Where it falls short

AffordabilityBottom 27%

Beach village prices. Houses near the ocean run high, and oceanfront condos carry wind insurance and building fees on top. Older low rise condos a few blocks back are the more reachable way in.

Active outdoorsBottom 23%

The outdoors here is the water. Shore snorkeling and diving on the reef, long beach walks, yoga on the sand in season and a Sunday market at El Prado Park. Trails and big parks are a short drive south.

On the waterBottom 5%

A snorkel and dive town more than a boating one. There is no marina in the village, and Anglin's Fishing Pier has been closed since storm damage in 2022 with no reopening expected before 2028. Boaters keep boats in Pompano Beach or Fort Lauderdale.
