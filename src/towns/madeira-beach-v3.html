About Madeira Beach

Locals call it Mad Beach. Life here centers on John's Pass, where charter boats, the boardwalk and seafood spots line the channel between the Gulf and Boca Ciega Bay. On the Gulf side it's mostly condos along the sand, and on the bay side you'll find canal homes with docks. Retirees, boaters and anglers make up a big share of the year round crowd.

Helene's surge flooded much of the island in 2024, and some buildings are still being repaired or raised. Flood insurance matters a lot here. Winter and spring bring visitor traffic on Gulf Boulevard and around John's Pass, and condo fees on the Gulf can run high.

Where it falls short

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Active outdoorsBottom 26%

NightlifeTop 26%

John's Pass Village is where the evening happens, with bars and seafood decks along the boardwalk and live music by the water. Away from the Pass the town is quiet after dinner.

Airport accessTop 26%

About 30 minutes to St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) and roughly 40 to 45 to Tampa (TPA). Bridge and beach traffic can add time on weekends.

Dining and artsTop 36%

Fresh Gulf seafood is the draw, with grouper sandwiches and dockside decks at John's Pass. The John's Pass Seafood Festival marks its 45th year in October 2026. Galleries and theater are thin.

Meet peopleTop 45%

A modest calendar. The Wednesday Market runs November through May, the Trash Pirates do a monthly beach cleanup at Archibald Park, and the Old Salt Fishing Foundation is run entirely by volunteers.

AffordabilityBottom 51%

Often a step below neighbors like Indian Rocks Beach on price, with lots of condos to choose from. Flood insurance is the wildcard, and Helene's surge in 2024 makes elevation worth asking about.

Peace and quietBottom 48%

Common questions

Is Madeira Beach walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. At John's Pass you can walk from the boardwalk shops to the sand across Gulf Boulevard. The rest of town stretches about two and a half miles, so most trips use a car or the Suncoast Beach Trolley.

Does Madeira Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. About two and a half miles of white sand, with public parking at Archibald Memorial Beach Park. The park's 1934 Snack Shack has been closed since Helene, and the city expects it back in 2027.

Is Madeira Beach affordable?

What is the nightlife like in Madeira Beach?

How busy does Madeira Beach get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. John's Pass bills itself as the top visitor attraction in Pinellas County, so the south end is busy all year and the drawbridge stops traffic. The residential fingers on the bay side stay quiet.
