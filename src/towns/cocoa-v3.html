About Cocoa Beach

Cocoa Beach is the East Coast's classic surf town. The beach is wide and flat, the pier has been a hangout for generations, and when there's a launch you can watch the rocket climb off the Cape from the sand. It's casual, friendly and close to the Space Coast's big employers.

It's mostly condos and older beach houses on a narrow island, with hurricane exposure and some flooding on the river side. Summer weekends get busy with day trippers from Orlando.

Where it shines

Active outdoorsTop 4%

Surfing is the daily sport. Lori Wilson Park gives you dunes and open beach, the Banana River has mangrove islands to paddle, and the Cocoa Beach Air Show brings jets over the sand in the warm months.

NightlifeTop 11%

Beach bars more than clubs. The tiki bar on the Westgate Cocoa Beach Pier, Coconuts on the Beach and Salty Sisters keep the music going, and Friday Fest turns downtown into a street party the third Friday of each month.

Meet peopleTop 17%

Cocoa Beach Main Street keeps the calendar moving with Friday Fest every month, Sip & Stroll evenings and a fall songwriter festival. Surf schools take any level, and Surfin' Santas fills downtown on December 24.

Coastal charmBottom 30%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Common questions

Is Cocoa Beach walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Downtown around Minutemen Causeway and Brevard Avenue is compact and a short walk to the sand. The pier and Ron Jon Surf Shop are a couple of miles north along A1A, so a bike helps.

Does Cocoa Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Wide, flat sand and gentle surf that has taught a lot of people to stand up on a board. Lori Wilson Park has free parking, and the Westgate Cocoa Beach Pier is the landmark. Launches go up just to the north.

Is Cocoa Beach affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $450K median sale (Resideline, Sep 2026). Middle of the road for a Florida beach town. Older condos keep the entry price reachable, while houses on the canals and the ocean run much higher. Check condo fees and insurance closely.

What is the nightlife like in Cocoa Beach?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Sunset with a drink at the tiki bar on the pier, then a band at Coconuts on the Beach or Salty Sisters. On the third Friday of the month, Friday Fest fills downtown with live music and artisans.

How busy does Cocoa Beach get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Depends on the day. Weekends, launch days and spring bring heavy traffic on A1A and the causeways, and the pier area stays busy. The residential streets south of downtown are much calmer.

Is Cocoa Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The Banana River side is for paddling. You can rent kayaks at The Riverfront and head out among the mangrove islands. For the ocean, Port Canaveral and its inlet are about 15 minutes north.

Which airport is closest to Cocoa Beach?

Orlando (MCO) is about 50 minutes west on the Beachline, with flights nearly everywhere. Melbourne (MLB) is about 40 minutes south and much smaller.
