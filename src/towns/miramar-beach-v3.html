About Miramar Beach

Miramar Beach is the stretch of Gulf front between Destin and the 30A towns. Days here run on the beach, golf, the bay side at Sandestin and plenty of places to eat and shop. You'll find full time locals, retirees, second home owners and a lot of condo renters, so it never feels sleepy.

The trade offs are real. US 98 backs up in summer and spring break, and beach parking fills early. Condo HOA dues and insurance can add up fast, and Gulf front buildings take the brunt of hurricanes. If you want quiet streets and low monthly costs, look closely before you buy.

Plenty going on but a lot of it is visitors. Locals find each other at the Saturday farmers market at Grand Boulevard, the Thursday Bands on the Boulevard concerts in early summer and through golf, tennis and pickleball at the resorts.

Active outdoorsTop 45%

Mostly beach and water, plus golf and tennis at Sandestin. For trails and wilder land, Topsail Hill and Grayton Beach state parks are a short drive east along 30A.

Dining and artsBottom 41%

Lots of choice. Pompano Joe's sits right on the sand, Grand Boulevard and Baytowne Wharf add sit down restaurants and cocktail bars, and Destin's seafood spots are a short drive.

Common questions

Is Miramar Beach walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Better than most of the area if you live near Scenic Gulf Drive, Sandestin or Grand Boulevard, where you can walk or bike to the beach, shops and restaurants. Farther from the water it is a driving place.

Does Miramar Beach have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. Top tier. Fine white sand and clear green water, with the Miramar Beach Regional Access and Miramar Park giving public parking and access. Lots fill fast on summer weekends, so go early.

Is Miramar Beach affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $735K median sale (Resideline, Oct 2026). Expensive. The median sale is in the high $700s, a lot of the stock is condos and resort homes with HOA fees, and Gulf front insurance adds up. There is also a large supply of listings right now, which gives buyers some room.

What is the nightlife like in Miramar Beach?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Sunset on the sand at Pompano Joe's, then a stroll through Baytowne Wharf for live music and a nightcap by the lagoon. In summer there are fireworks and concerts. In winter it is a quieter dinner out with locals.

How busy does Miramar Beach get?

It gets busy, especially in season. Not very quiet from spring break through Labor Day. US 98 backs up and the beach accesses fill early. Winter is noticeably calmer, and some streets behind the dunes stay peaceful all year.

Is Miramar Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Gulf on one side, Choctawhatchee Bay on the other. Sandestin has a bayside marina, Destin's harbor and charter fleet are next door, and there is a snorkeling reef just off Miramar Park.

Which airport is closest to Miramar Beach?

Destin Fort Walton Beach (VPS) is about 30 minutes west over the Mid Bay Bridge, which has a toll. Northwest Florida Beaches (ECP) near Panama City Beach is about 45 minutes east.

What should I know before moving to Miramar Beach?

The main things to plan for: Heavy US 98 traffic in season, beach parking fills early, condo HOA and insurance costs, Gulf front hurricane exposure.
