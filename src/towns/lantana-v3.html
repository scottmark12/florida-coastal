About Lantana

Lantana is a low key town between Lake Worth Beach and Boynton Beach. Life here is about boats, fishing and a small public beach across the bridge on the barrier island. Retirees, working families and boaters all call it home, and most houses are older and modest, with a handful of big estates on Hypoluxo Island.

Homes near the Intracoastal face storm surge, so check flood zones and insurance early. Much of the housing is older and may need updating. US 1 and I 95 traffic can be heavy, especially in winter when the snowbirds are back.

Common questions

Is Lantana walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Limited. The area around Ocean Avenue and the Intracoastal is pleasant to stroll, but most daily errands mean a short drive.

Does Lantana have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. Lantana's public beach is a small stretch of about 750 feet with a boardwalk, picnic tables and a volleyball court. In 2025 the town reviewed concept plans to upgrade it.

Is Lantana affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $423K median sale (Horizon Palm Realty, Nov 2026). One of the more affordable towns this close to the ocean. Median sales were near $350K in 2026, mostly older homes and condos, while waterfront and Hypoluxo Island homes cost far more.

What is the nightlife like in Lantana?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A typical evening is dinner on the water at the Old Key Lime House or a casual spot on Ocean Avenue. Locals bring the boat or walk down to watch the sunset over the Intracoastal.

How busy does Lantana get?

It is quiet most of the year. Mostly calm residential streets. The noise is the main roads and Interstate 95 on the west side, plus some weekend boat traffic.

Is Lantana good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Good water access. Sportsman's Park has a boat ramp on the Intracoastal, and both the Boynton and Palm Beach inlets are an easy run by boat.

Which airport is closest to Lantana?

West Palm Beach (DJT) is about 15 minutes north on Interstate 95. The small Lantana airport on the west side serves private planes.

What should I know before moving to Lantana?

The main things to plan for: Storm surge along the Intracoastal, older housing stock, and US 1 and Interstate 95 traffic.
