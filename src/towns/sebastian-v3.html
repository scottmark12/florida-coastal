About Sebastian

Sebastian is a small, easygoing fishing town on the Indian River Lagoon. Life here is about the water: fishing, paddling out to the spoil islands, surfing and fishing the inlet, and watching pelicans at the refuge just up the river. It has a real Old Florida feel, with riverfront bars, a lot of retirees and families, and no high rises.

The trade offs: Sebastian is quiet and has few big city services, so you'll head to Vero Beach or Melbourne for major shopping and specialty care. It's exposed to hurricanes, the lagoon has had water quality problems, and the closest real airports are 25 to 35 minutes away.

Mostly casual seafood and tiki bars with a river view, plus craft shows and small festivals in Riverview Park. For theater, museums and a longer restaurant list, Vero Beach is about half an hour south.

Common questions

Is Sebastian walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. The riverfront is the walkable part. You can stroll Indian River Drive between Riverview Park, the piers and a handful of restaurants. For groceries and nearly everything else you will drive.

Does Sebastian have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. No ocean beach in town. You drive 20 to 25 minutes across the lagoon to the barrier island, where Sebastian Inlet State Park has miles of sand and well known surf. Bridge work in 2026 has limited access on the south side.

Is Sebastian affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $399K median sale (Redfin via Stacker, Jan to Mar 2026). One of the better values on this stretch of coast. Prices sit below Vero Beach and well below the barrier island towns. Riverfront homes cost more and need flood insurance.

What is the nightlife like in Sebastian?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Dinner on the river, then music outside at Capt Hiram's Sandbar or a band at Earl's Hideaway. Pareidolia Brewing and Mash Monkeys cover the craft beer. Expect a low key night that ends early compared with a city.

How busy does Sebastian get?

It is quiet most of the year. A slow river town. Indian River Drive gets busy on festival weekends and US 1 carries the through traffic, but most streets stay calm year round, with a modest bump in winter.

Is Sebastian good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. This is what the town is for. The city's Yacht Club ramp on Indian River Drive has a double ramp and a fishing pier, there is another ramp at Main Street, and Sebastian Inlet and the Atlantic are a short run across the lagoon.

Which airport is closest to Sebastian?

About 35 minutes to Melbourne (MLB) and about 25 to Vero Beach's small airport, which now has a few airline routes. Orlando (MCO) is roughly an hour and a half.

What should I know before moving to Sebastian?

The main things to plan for: Hurricane exposure, few big city services. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
