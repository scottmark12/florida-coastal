About Bradenton Beach

Bradenton Beach is the south end of Anna Maria Island. Historic Bridge Street runs from the Gulf to the old pier on the bay, lined with casual seafood spots and shops. A free trolley runs up and down the island, and Coquina Beach has some of the island's longest stretch of white sand. Across the bridge, Cortez is still a working fishing village.

The island floods, and Helene hit Bradenton Beach hard in 2024. Check elevation, flood zone and storm history for any home. Cortez Bridge replacement work is planned through about 2029, which means traffic. Winters and weekends get busy. Sarasota Bradenton airport is about 30 minutes away.

NightlifeTop 31%

Bridge Street is the island's liveliest block after dark. The Drift In marked 100 years in 2025, open air bars put on live music, and a few times a year the street closes to cars for a party. It is a few blocks, not a district.

Dining and artsTop 35%

Seafood and casual restaurants on Bridge Street and along Gulf Drive, including the Anna Maria Oyster Bar out on the pier, plus a few shops and galleries. For theater you head up the island, and for museums into Bradenton.

Airport accessTop 44%

Sarasota Bradenton (SRQ) is about 30 minutes, more when the bridges back up. Tampa International (TPA) is about an hour and 15 minutes north over the Sunshine Skyway.

Meet peopleBottom 51%

A small town with a short list. The Bradenton Runners Club meets at Coquina Beach on Saturday mornings, the Bridge Street merchants put on Sundown Get Down street parties, and Turtle Watch volunteers patrol the beach in nesting season.

Peace and quietBottom 50%

Calm on a weekday in fall, crowded from February through April. Gulf Drive is the only road through, so beach traffic crawls on sunny weekends, and work on a new Cortez Bridge is set to start in November 2026 and run into 2029.

Active outdoorsBottom 44%

Coquina Beach has a paved path along the Gulf, and the bay side across the road has mangrove shoreline to explore by kayak or paddleboard. Robinson Preserve, with longer trails, is about 20 minutes away on the mainland.
