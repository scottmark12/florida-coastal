About Dania Beach

Dania Beach is the low key neighbor to Fort Lauderdale and Hollywood. People come for the fishing pier, the quiet beach and John U. Lloyd Beach State Park, and for a price that is easier than most of Broward's coast. You'll find older single family homes, condos and townhomes, plus a long run of antique shops downtown.

The trade offs are real. Planes from FLL fly right over parts of town, so listen before you buy. Many streets sit low and flood in heavy rain and king tides, and winters bring more traffic. Insurance in Broward County is costly, so get quotes early.

NightlifeTop 36%

Modest. The Casino at Dania Beach books tribute bands and comedy, the Improv at Dania Pointe has stand up, and Quarterdeck pours drinks out on the pier. For a real late night you drive ten minutes to Fort Lauderdale.

AffordabilityTop 38%

Middle of the pack for the southeast coast. Prices tend to run below the Fort Lauderdale beach neighborhoods, with plenty of condos and older houses. Much of the city sits low, so price flood insurance before you fall for a place.

On the waterTop 45%

Strong for a small city. The state park has two boat ramps that take trailered boats up to 36 feet, minutes from Port Everglades Inlet. The city marina sits by the pier, and the Dania Cutoff Canal is lined with boatyards.

Dining and artsTop 48%

Dania Pointe has about two dozen restaurants, mostly well known names, plus the Improv comedy club. Jaxson's Ice Cream Parlor has been on Federal Highway since 1956. Galleries and theaters are a short drive north or south.

Meet peopleBottom 50%

A short list. Yoga and Zumba on The Lawn at Dania Pointe on Wednesday evenings, guided paddles on Whiskey Creek and a few seasonal markets and fun runs. Most clubs and leagues are next door in Fort Lauderdale or Hollywood.

BeachesBottom 49%

Dania Beach Ocean Park is a simple city beach with tiki huts, parking and a 974 foot fishing pier. Just north, Mizell-Eula Johnson State Park adds more than two miles of undeveloped sand. Low key, not a headline beach.

Coastal charmBottom 42%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Common questions

Is Dania Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. The city is spread out between US 1, the airport and the beach, so most errands take a car. Dania Pointe is walkable once you park, and the pier, the beach and the city marina sit together at the ocean end of town.

Does Dania Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. Dania Beach Ocean Park is a simple city beach with tiki huts, parking and a 974 foot fishing pier. Just north, Mizell-Eula Johnson State Park adds more than two miles of undeveloped sand. Low key, not a headline beach.
