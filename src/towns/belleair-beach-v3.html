About Belleair Beach

Belleair Beach is almost all homes, with no hotels lining the sand and very little commercial strip. The Gulf is on one side of Gulf Boulevard, and canal streets with docks and boat lifts run off the Intracoastal on the other. People here walk the beach in the morning, take the boat out on weekends and know their neighbors. It suits retirees, second home owners and families who want a calm, residential beach town.

The trade offs are serious. This is a low barrier island with storm surge risk, and Helene and Milton hit hard in 2024. You will still see demolitions, rebuilding and elevated new homes, and most homes sit in FEMA A or V flood zones. Flood and wind insurance are expensive, and property taxes reset high on a new purchase. Groceries, doctors and the hospital are over the causeway on the mainland.

Most morningsCoffee, then a walk or ride over the Belleair Causeway for the view of the bay before the traffic picks up.
Late afternoonBike north to Sand Key Park and walk the wide beach at the tip of the island.
