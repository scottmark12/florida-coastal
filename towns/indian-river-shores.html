About Indian River Shores

Indian River Shores is a small town on the barrier island just north of Vero Beach. There's no downtown, just low rise oceanfront condos, private clubs and gated neighborhoods running from the beach to the lagoon. Life revolves around golf, tennis, the beach and the club calendar, and most residents are retirees and winter residents. Vero's shops and restaurants are a short drive south.

It's a barrier island, so hurricane and flood exposure are real, and beach erosion means dune repair work comes and goes. Insurance is high, and club dues and condo assessments add up. Summers are very quiet. The nearest airport with real airline service is Melbourne, about 40 minutes.

Common questions

Is Indian River Shores walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. You walk the beach and bike the shoulder of the island road, but daily errands mean a short drive. A few shops sit along the main road, and Vero's oceanside village is the nearest place to stroll.

Does Indian River Shores have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Long, uncrowded stretches of sand with very little development showing from the water. Much of the oceanfront sits behind clubs and condos, and county beach parks nearby give the public a way in.

Is Indian River Shores affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $1.42M median sale (Redfin, Sept 2026). One of the most expensive places on this coast, with a typical value around $1.4M. Add barrier island insurance, club dues in many communities and HOA fees before you budget.

What is the nightlife like in Indian River Shores?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Sunset over the lagoon, then dinner with friends at the club or a short drive to Ocean Drive. By nine the island road is quiet and the loudest thing is the surf.

How busy does Indian River Shores get?

It is one of the quietest places on the Florida coast. About as quiet as the coast gets. Building heights are low, the island road is calm, and summer feels almost empty once the winter residents leave.

Is Indian River Shores good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The Indian River Lagoon on the west side is good for paddling, fishing and docks in the communities that have them. Sebastian Inlet to the north and Fort Pierce Inlet to the south are the routes offshore.

Which airport is closest to Indian River Shores?

Melbourne is about 40 minutes north and handles most trips with a connection. Vero Beach's own airport is close but has only a few flights, and Orlando is around an hour and a half.

What should I know before moving to Indian River Shores?
