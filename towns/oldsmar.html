Oldsmar

A small, tidy city at the top of Old Tampa Bay, with bayfront parks and the fastest airport run in Pinellas.

The scorecard

Tap any score for the local's take.

Nightlife2

Quiet. A few bars, a brewery or two and casual grills, plus live racing at Tampa Bay Downs right next door in winter and spring. Most people go into Tampa or Westchase for a night out.

Meet people3

Small enough that the city's events bring everyone out, like Oldsmar Days and Nights each April at R.E. Olds Park and the Upper Tampa Bay Oktoberfest. Youth sports and the Upper Tampa Bay Chamber are big here too.

Peace and quiet4

Calm and suburban, with bayfront streets that are really peaceful. Tampa Road and Hillsborough Avenue carry heavy commuter traffic at rush hour.

Walkability2

Mostly a drive. The small downtown and bayfront parks are pleasant on foot or bike, but errands are in plazas along the main roads.

On the water3

Bay water, not Gulf water. R.E. Olds Park has a 400 foot fishing pier and Mobbly Bayou has a canoe launch, but upper Old Tampa Bay is shallow, so it suits paddling and fishing more than big boats.

Beaches1

No beach to speak of. The shoreline is bay and mangrove, and the nearest Gulf beaches like Clearwater Beach or Honeymoon Island are 30 to 40 minutes away.

Dining and arts2

Mostly casual, family owned spots and chains, with more choice in Westchase and Tampa nearby.

Outdoors and wellness3

Better than you would think. R.E. Olds Park, Mobbly Bayou Preserve and the bayfront trails give you sunsets, birding and paddling, and Upper Tampa Bay Park is right across the county line.

Affordability3

Mid priced for Tampa Bay, with typical values in the $360Ks. Newer townhomes and older ranch homes mix in, and bayfront streets cost more.

Airport access5

Tampa International (TPA) is about 15 to 20 minutes away, which is about as good as it gets in the region.

Common questions about Oldsmar

Is Oldsmar walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Mostly a drive. The small downtown and bayfront parks are pleasant on foot or bike, but errands are in plazas along the main roads.

Does Oldsmar have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No beach to speak of. The shoreline is bay and mangrove, and the nearest Gulf beaches like Clearwater Beach or Honeymoon Island are 30 to 40 minutes away.

Is Oldsmar affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $361K typical home value (Zillow, Jan 2026). Mid priced for Tampa Bay, with typical values in the $360Ks. Newer townhomes and older ranch homes mix in, and bayfront streets cost more.

What is the nightlife like in Oldsmar?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Dinner somewhere casual in town, then a walk on the R.E. Olds pier as the sun drops over the bay. On race days, an afternoon at Tampa Bay Downs is the local outing.

How busy does Oldsmar get?

It is quiet most of the year. Calm and suburban, with bayfront streets that are really peaceful. Tampa Road and Hillsborough Avenue carry heavy commuter traffic at rush hour.

Is Oldsmar good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. Bay water, not Gulf water. R.E. Olds Park has a 400 foot fishing pier and Mobbly Bayou has a canoe launch, but upper Old Tampa Bay is shallow, so it suits paddling and fishing more than big boats.

Which airport is closest to Oldsmar?

Tampa International (TPA) is about 15 to 20 minutes away, which is about as good as it gets in the region.

What should I know before moving to Oldsmar?

The main things to plan for: Low areas along upper Old Tampa Bay flood in surges; Tampa Road and Hillsborough Avenue traffic; no Gulf beach.
