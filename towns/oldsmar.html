About Oldsmar

Oldsmar is a quiet, orderly little city where Pinellas meets Hillsborough. It has big bayfront parks, nature preserves full of wading birds, and a lot of established neighborhoods with good sized yards. Families like the parks and ball fields, and frequent travelers love that Tampa International is about 15 to 20 minutes away. It feels like a suburb with a small town calendar of spring festivals and parades.

The trade offs: the low areas along upper Old Tampa Bay flood in surges, so check the flood zone before you buy. Tampa Road and Hillsborough Avenue get heavy at rush hour. There is no Gulf beach here; the nearest ones are about 30 minutes away. Pinellas property taxes are high on a new purchase, and more than half the homes for sale have an HOA.

Common questions

Is Oldsmar walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Mostly a drive. The small downtown and bayfront parks are pleasant on foot or bike, but errands are in plazas along the main roads.

Does Oldsmar have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No beach to speak of. The shoreline is bay and mangrove, and the nearest Gulf beaches like Clearwater Beach or Honeymoon Island are 30 to 40 minutes away.

Is Oldsmar affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $399K median sale (Momentum, Nov 2026). Mid priced for Tampa Bay, with typical values in the $360Ks. Newer townhomes and older ranch homes mix in, and bayfront streets cost more.

What is the nightlife like in Oldsmar?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Dinner somewhere casual in town, then a walk on the R.E. Olds pier as the sun drops over the bay. On race days, an afternoon at Tampa Bay Downs is the local outing.

How busy does Oldsmar get?

It is quiet most of the year. Calm and suburban, with bayfront streets that are really peaceful. Tampa Road and Hillsborough Avenue carry heavy commuter traffic at rush hour.

Is Oldsmar good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. Bay water, not Gulf water. R.E. Olds Park has a 400 foot fishing pier and Mobbly Bayou has a canoe launch, but upper Old Tampa Bay is shallow, so it suits paddling and fishing more than big boats.

Which airport is closest to Oldsmar?

Tampa International (TPA) is about 15 to 20 minutes away, which is about as good as it gets in the region.

What should I know before moving to Oldsmar?

The main things to plan for: Low areas along upper Old Tampa Bay flood in surges; Tampa Road and Hillsborough Avenue traffic; no Gulf beach.
