About Hallandale Beach

Hallandale Beach is mostly condos. Towers line A1A on the ocean, older buildings run along Hallandale Beach Boulevard, and Golden Isles holds the single family homes on the Intracoastal. Many residents are retirees and part time snowbirds, with a strong mix of Latin American and Canadian buyers. Gulfstream Park brings racing, shops and restaurants to the west side of the beach.

The trade offs: traffic on A1A and US 1 is heavy in season, and low streets flood in storms and king tides. Older condo buildings can carry big fees and special assessments since Florida's new condo safety rules, so read the association's budget and reserve study before you buy.

Where it shines

Airport accessTop 4%

Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about 15 to 20 minutes north on I-95 or US 1. Miami (MIA) is a workable backup about 35 to 40 minutes south.

NightlifeTop 16%

Gulfstream Park is the night out, with a casino open 365 days a year and more than 20 restaurants, bars and nightspots around the track. Away from it the city is mostly condo towers and goes quiet early.

Dining and artsTop 20%

Most of the dining is at Gulfstream Park, where the village mixes sit down restaurants and casual spots with track side rooms like Ten Palms. Beyond that it is plaza restaurants along the boulevards. Theaters and galleries are a drive.

Where it falls short

On the waterBottom 30%

The Intracoastal runs along the east side of town, with docks behind many of the condo buildings and canal front homes to the west. There is no inlet here, so ocean runs go north to Port Everglades or south to Haulover Inlet.

Active outdoorsBottom 11%

Green space is limited to city parks with courts, fields and a pool, plus the two beach parks. For trails and paddling you drive ten to fifteen minutes to the mangrove park at West Lake in Hollywood or to Oleta River State Park.

Peace and quietBottom 10%

Hallandale Beach Boulevard and US 1 are slow at rush hour, and big race days and the winter season add more cars. The beach is short and backed by towers, so it rarely feels empty.

Coastal charmBottom 3%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Common questions

Is Hallandale Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Not much is close together. The towers sit along A1A, the stores are on wide boulevards, and the village at Gulfstream Park is walkable only once you are there. A free city minibus covers some of the gaps.

Does Hallandale Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. The city's oceanfront is short, with public beach parks at the north and south ends and a water tower painted like a beach ball marking the way in. The sand is good. Parking is limited and fills on weekends.

Is Hallandale Beach affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $245K median sale, mostly condos (Horizon Palm Realty, Jun 2026). Middle of the pack for this stretch of coast. Older condo towers list well below Sunny Isles Beach next door, but monthly fees, reserve funding and special assessments can change the math. Read the building's budget first.

What is the nightlife like in Hallandale Beach?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. A night out here mostly means Gulfstream Park. Dinner in the village, a few races if the horses are running, then the casino or one of the bars. If you want a beach bar and a live band, the Hollywood Broadwalk is ten minutes north.

How busy does Hallandale Beach get?

It gets busy, especially in season. Hallandale Beach Boulevard and US 1 are slow at rush hour, and big race days and the winter season add more cars. The beach is short and backed by towers, so it rarely feels empty.

Is Hallandale Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The Intracoastal runs along the east side of town, with docks behind many of the condo buildings and canal front homes to the west. There is no inlet here, so ocean runs go north to Port Everglades or south to Haulover Inlet.

Which airport is closest to Hallandale Beach?

Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about 15 to 20 minutes north on I-95 or US 1. Miami (MIA) is a workable backup about 35 to 40 minutes south.

What should I know before moving to Hallandale Beach?

The main things to plan for: Traffic, flooding, condo fees and assessments. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
