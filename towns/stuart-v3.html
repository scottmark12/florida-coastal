About Stuart

Stuart is a boating and fishing town where the St. Lucie River meets the Indian River Lagoon, a short run to the inlet and the ocean. Downtown is small and walkable, with a riverwalk, a historic theater, local shops and restaurants. People here fish, boat, golf and spend Sunday mornings at the market in Flagler Park. It's popular with retirees and families who want a polished small town feel.

The trade offs: the river's water quality can suffer badly after Lake Okeechobee releases. Stuart is hurricane exposed and insurance in Martin County is among the higher ones on this coast. Winters bring traffic and crowds, and waterfront homes get expensive fast.

Where it shines

Water on three sides. The St. Lucie River wraps around downtown, the Indian River Lagoon is to the east, and the St. Lucie Inlet puts you offshore fast for the sailfish the town is known for. Marinas are all over. Watch water quality after lake releases.

Coastal charmTop 4%

Full of charm: a historic downtown, local everything, no towers, and streets you want to walk.

Active outdoorsTop 14%

The Riverwalk is the daily walk, and the Sunday Green Market sets up at Memorial Park. Bigger preserves and trails are a 15 to 20 minute drive. Most of the outdoor life here happens on a boat or a paddleboard.

Dining and artsTop 14%

A strong restaurant row for a town this size, mostly independent places packed into a few downtown blocks, plus working studios, galleries and antique shops. A historic theater downtown books concerts and shows through the year.

WalkabilityTop 15%

Downtown is compact. From the Riverwalk you can reach restaurants, shops, galleries, the music stage and Memorial Park on foot in a few minutes. It is a small area, though, and the beach is a drive over two bridges.

Meet peopleTop 20%

Lots of standing dates. Rock'n Riverwalk puts a band on the Riverwalk stage on Sunday afternoons, the Stuart Green Market runs Sunday mornings, Terra Fermata has open jams early in the week, and Stuart Main Street runs strolls and street festivals.

Where it falls short

Airport accessBottom 29%

No commercial airport nearby. The Palm Beach airport is about 45 minutes south on I-95, and Fort Lauderdale (FLL) and Orlando (MCO) are each a longer haul. Fine for occasional trips, tiring if you fly every week.

Common questions

Is Stuart walkable?

Yes, for the most part. Downtown is compact. From the Riverwalk you can reach restaurants, shops, galleries, the music stage and Memorial Park on foot in a few minutes. It is a small area, though, and the beach is a drive over two bridges.

Does Stuart have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. The beaches are on Hutchinson Island, about 10 to 15 minutes from downtown, with public parking and room to spread out. St. Lucie Inlet Preserve State Park, reached by boat, is about as empty as a Florida beach gets.

Is Stuart affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $580K median single family sale, 2026 to March (BeachesMLS via Treasure Coast Living). Middle of the pack for the Florida coast. Prices generally run below Jupiter and the rest of Palm Beach County, with older houses and condos near downtown and newer neighborhoods to the west. Waterfront and island addresses cost much more.

What is the nightlife like in Stuart?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Dinner downtown, a walk along the river, then a show at Terra Fermata, which has live music every night of the week. A few bars nearby keep going a bit later. On Sundays the music starts early with Rock'n Riverwalk at 1pm.

How busy does Stuart get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Downtown is small and gets busy on weekends and in winter, when parking can take a few laps. U.S. 1 and the bridges carry steady traffic, and trains run right through the middle of town. Neighborhoods off the main roads are calm.

Is Stuart good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. Water on three sides. The St. Lucie River wraps around downtown, the Indian River Lagoon is to the east, and the St. Lucie Inlet puts you offshore fast for the sailfish the town is known for. Marinas are all over. Watch water quality after lake releases.

Which airport is closest to Stuart?

No commercial airport nearby. The Palm Beach airport is about 45 minutes south on I-95, and Fort Lauderdale (FLL) and Orlando (MCO) are each a longer haul. Fine for occasional trips, tiring if you fly every week.

What should I know before moving to Stuart?

The main things to plan for: River water quality after Lake Okeechobee releases, hurricane exposure. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
