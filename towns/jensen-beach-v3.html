About Jensen Beach

Jensen Beach is a small, easygoing town on the Indian River, with a tiny old downtown, colorful shops and a Caribbean feel. Across the causeway, Hutchinson Island has uncrowded beaches where sea turtles nest in summer. People here fish, paddle, bike Indian River Drive and spend a lot of evenings outside.

The trade offs: the lagoon has had water quality problems, especially after Lake Okeechobee releases. Hutchinson Island and the riverfront take the brunt of hurricanes, and insurance in Martin County is on the high side. Palm Beach airport is about 50 minutes away.

Active outdoorsTop 30%

The lagoon and the beach are the main outdoors. A large county park on the river has a fishing pier and open lawn, and a state preserve of marsh and pine flatwoods sits just north of town for hiking and paddling.

WalkabilityTop 35%

Downtown is a few colorful blocks you can cover on foot in ten minutes, with the lagoon at the end of the street. Beyond that you need a bike or a car. The beach is a few miles away across the causeway.

Dining and artsTop 37%

A decent run of independent restaurants downtown and along the river, heavy on seafood and island flavors, with a few galleries mixed in. Theater and bigger shows are 10 to 15 minutes away in Stuart.

Peace and quietTop 38%

One of the calmer towns on this stretch of coast. There is no interstate nearby and no high rise strip, and the beaches rarely fill. Downtown gets busy on event nights and winter weekends, then goes back to quiet.

BeachesTop 38%

Jensen Sea Turtle Beach on Hutchinson Island is wide, lifeguarded and easy, with volleyball courts, pavilions, showers and a beach cafe open every day. The buildings nearby are low and it rarely feels crowded.

AffordabilityTop 42%

Mid range for the Florida coast. Houses and condos generally cost less than in Jupiter or the Palm Beaches, with older cottages near downtown and condos on the island. Island and riverfront places carry higher insurance.

Meet peopleTop 46%

The town is small enough that you keep running into the same faces downtown, at the beach volleyball courts and on the lagoon. Street events and festivals fill the cooler months, and sailing and paddling groups use the river.

On the waterTop 48%

Common questions

Is Jensen Beach walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Downtown is a few colorful blocks you can cover on foot in ten minutes, with the lagoon at the end of the street. Beyond that you need a bike or a car. The beach is a few miles away across the causeway.

Does Jensen Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Jensen Sea Turtle Beach on Hutchinson Island is wide, lifeguarded and easy, with volleyball courts, pavilions, showers and a beach cafe open every day. The buildings nearby are low and it rarely feels crowded.

Is Jensen Beach affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $475K median sale (Redfin, Sep 2026). Mid range for the Florida coast. Houses and condos generally cost less than in Jupiter or the Palm Beaches, with older cottages near downtown and condos on the island. Island and riverfront places carry higher insurance.

What is the nightlife like in Jensen Beach?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. A night out is dinner and a band on a patio downtown, all within two or three blocks, or a drink on the causeway watching the lagoon go dark. Street event nights bring the biggest crowds. It is easygoing and done at a reasonable hour.

How busy does Jensen Beach get?

It is quiet most of the year. One of the calmer towns on this stretch of coast. There is no interstate nearby and no high rise strip, and the beaches rarely fill. Downtown gets busy on event nights and winter weekends, then goes back to quiet.

Is Jensen Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The Indian River Lagoon is at the edge of downtown, with causeway parks, fishing spots and places to launch a kayak or small boat. The St. Lucie Inlet is a run south for ocean access. Keep an eye on lagoon water quality reports in summer.

Which airport is closest to Jensen Beach?

No commercial airport close by. The Palm Beach airport is about 50 minutes south, and Orlando (MCO) is around two hours north. Plan on a real drive at each end of a trip.
