About Delray Beach

Delray Beach is built around Atlantic Avenue, a long stretch of restaurants, shops and music that runs from the old downtown right to the beach. People bike to dinner, know the bartenders and still get a quiet stretch of sand in the morning.

It has gotten pricey, especially east of the Intracoastal, and downtown is busy on weekends. Farther west you'll find big gated communities and golf clubs at lower prices.

Where it shines

WalkabilityTop 2%

Atlantic Avenue runs from downtown straight to the sand, with the murals and galleries of Pineapple Grove one street over. The free Freebee shuttle covers everything from I-95 to the beach.

Meet peopleTop 2%

Easy to plug in. Run club on Wednesday nights and Saturday mornings, leagues and clinics at the Tennis Center downtown, an art walk the first Friday of every month and a Saturday GreenMarket from late October through May.

NightlifeTop 5%

Atlantic Avenue stays up. Tin Roof has live music every night, more stages sit a few blocks east, and the late spots run until 2am on weekends.

Dining and artsTop 5%

Restaurant after restaurant on the Avenue, live shows at Arts Garage, the Cornell Art Museum at Old School Square, and the Delray Affair street festival every April.

BeachesTop 5%

A lifeguarded city beach at the end of Atlantic Avenue. In 2026 it earned the international Blue Flag award for the fourth year in a row, one of only three beaches in the country to have it.

Coastal charmTop 6%

Full of charm: a historic downtown, local everything, no towers, and streets you want to walk.

Where it falls short

AffordabilityBottom 19%

Houses near the beach and downtown run well above the Florida median. Condos and townhomes farther from the Avenue bring the price of entry way down.

Peace and quietBottom 8%

Downtown is busy all year, on foot and by car, and parking on the Avenue is metered. A block or two off Atlantic the parking is free and the streets are calmer.

On the waterBottom 3%

The Intracoastal runs through town, with a 24 slip city marina and public ramps at Knowles Park and Mangrove Park. For the ocean you run north to Boynton Inlet or south to Boca.

About average for 2 more: airport access and active outdoors
Airport accessTop 35%

About half an hour to the Palm Beach airport and about 45 minutes to Fort Lauderdale (FLL). Tri-Rail and Amtrak also stop in town, west of downtown.

Active outdoorsTop 47%

Better for a stroll than a hike. Wakodahatchee Wetlands has a free boardwalk known for its birds, and the Morikami has Japanese gardens to wander. Long bike trails are a drive away.

Common questions

Is Delray Beach walkable?

Yes. It is one of the most walkable towns on the Florida coast. Atlantic Avenue runs from downtown straight to the sand, with the murals and galleries of Pineapple Grove one street over. The free Freebee shuttle covers everything from I-95 to the beach.

Does Delray Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. A lifeguarded city beach at the end of Atlantic Avenue. In 2026 it earned the international Blue Flag award for the fourth year in a row, one of only three beaches in the country to have it.

Is Delray Beach affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $546K median across Delray's communities (Momentum, Jun 2026). Houses near the beach and downtown run well above the Florida median. Condos and townhomes farther from the Avenue bring the price of entry way down.

What is the nightlife like in Delray Beach?

Really good for a beach town. A night out here is a walk, not a drive. Dinner on Atlantic Avenue, a show at Arts Garage or live music at Tin Roof, and the late spots stay open until 2 on weekends.

How busy does Delray Beach get?

It gets busy, especially in season. Downtown is busy all year, on foot and by car, and parking on the Avenue is metered. A block or two off Atlantic the parking is free and the streets are calmer.

Is Delray Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The Intracoastal runs through town, with a 24 slip city marina and public ramps at Knowles Park and Mangrove Park. For the ocean you run north to Boynton Inlet or south to Boca.

Which airport is closest to Delray Beach?

About half an hour to the Palm Beach airport and about 45 minutes to Fort Lauderdale (FLL). Tri-Rail and Amtrak also stop in town, west of downtown.

What should I know before moving to Delray Beach?

The main things to plan for: Traffic, higher insurance. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
