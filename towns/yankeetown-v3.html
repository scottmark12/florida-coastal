About Yankeetown

Yankeetown is a small, shady town along the lower Withlacoochee River. Live oaks hang over the roads, homes sit along the river and its creeks, and the road west ends at a big nature preserve on the Gulf. People here fish, boat, paddle and walk the preserve trails. It is quiet most of the year, with a busier weekend around the November seafood festival and in fishing season. Neighbors tend to be retirees, anglers and families who have been here a long time.

The whole town sits in the flood zone. Helene in 2024 pushed a surge of about 12 feet into nearly all of Yankeetown, and many homes flooded. Few owners carry flood insurance, so check the flood history and elevation of any home before you get attached. Groceries are a short drive to Inglis, but bigger shopping and hospitals are farther, and Gainesville's airport is about 1 hour 15 minutes away. Sale prices swing a lot because only a few homes sell each year.
