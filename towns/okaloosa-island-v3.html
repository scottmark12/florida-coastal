About Okaloosa Island

Okaloosa Island sits across the Brooks Bridge from Fort Walton Beach. It's mostly condos, a few beach houses and parks, with the Gulf on one side and the sound on the other. Locals walk the beach early, fish the pier, take the grandkids to the Gulfarium and drive east along the undeveloped stretch toward Destin. A lot of owners are part timers and retirees who split the year.

The trade offs: this is a barrier island with real hurricane exposure, and flood insurance matters here. The Brooks Bridge is under construction until about 2029, and spring and summer bring heavy crowds. Most property is condos, so dues and building insurance can be steep. Ask about the land lease and the condo association's finances on anything you consider.

NightlifeTop 27%

Beach bar nightlife. The Boardwalk next to the pier has restaurants and bars, and Al's Beach Club has live music nightly once it reopens in March. New Year's Eve brings free fireworks over the beach. It quiets down a lot in winter.

Active outdoorsTop 28%

Beach life plus wildlife. The Gulfarium, open since 1955, has dolphin and manatee habitats and a sea turtle rescue center, and the sound side is good for paddling. Eglin land nearby keeps a lot of the coast natural.

Dining and artsTop 39%

Mostly beachy and casual. The Boardwalk has seafood, tacos and a burger bar, and a few waterfront spots dot the island. For more choice, downtown Fort Walton Beach is five minutes over the bridge and Destin is about 15 minutes east.

WalkabilityTop 44%

Walkable along the main strip. The pier, Boardwalk, Gulfarium, condos and beach accesses sit close together, so you can go days without driving. For groceries and errands you cross the bridge.

Airport accessTop 49%

Destin Fort Walton Beach (VPS) is about 20 to 25 minutes away and added JetBlue nonstops to Boston and New York in 2026. Pensacola (PNS) is about an hour and 15 minutes.

Meet peopleBottom 44%

Most people here are visitors or part time owners, so community takes effort. Regulars find each other on the pier, at the Boardwalk and through volunteering, and Fort Walton Beach across the bridge has the clubs, festivals and leagues.

On the waterBottom 39%

Water on both sides. The Gulf pier stretches 1,262 feet out, and on the sound side the county boat basin was rebuilt with slips, a fishing pier and a kayak launch. East Pass and Destin's charter fleets are a short drive.

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. This is the reason to live here. Soft white sand, clear green water and public beach accesses all along the island, with undeveloped Eglin land toward Destin that keeps a long stretch wide open.
