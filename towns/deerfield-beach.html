About Deerfield Beach

Deerfield Beach is at the north end of Broward County, next to Boca Raton. The beachfront has a fishing pier, a beach walk and nine lifeguard stands along the sand.

The median sale is around $330K, but that includes many inland condos, and houses run higher. Fort Lauderdale's airport is about 30 minutes away and Palm Beach's about 35, and Broward Health North is in town.

Common questions

Is Deerfield Beach walkable?

Partly. The blocks around the pier are walkable, with restaurants and the beach walk. Most errands elsewhere need a car.

Does Deerfield Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a good one. A lifeguarded beach with nine lifeguard stands, the fishing pier and a walk along the sand lined with restaurants.

Is Deerfield Beach affordable?

Yes, for a beach town in South Florida. Home prices: About $330K median sale, all home types (Broker One, June 2026). Houses run higher; many sales are inland condos. Beach condos often sell for about $400K to $620K, and canal homes in The Cove run higher. Broward insurance is high.

What is the nightlife like in Deerfield Beach?

Some, around the pier. Drinks by the pier at JB's or Oceans 234, sometimes with live music. Boca Raton and Fort Lauderdale are close for more.

How busy does Deerfield Beach get?

Busy on winter weekends. Calm side streets near the beach, but A1A and Hillsboro Boulevard get busy, especially on winter weekends.

Is Deerfield Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with some limits. The beach and pier on the ocean, the Intracoastal and canals in The Cove, and Hillsboro Inlet a few miles south for boats.

Which airport is closest to Deerfield Beach?

Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about 30 minutes and Palm Beach (PBI) about 35.

What should I know before moving to Deerfield Beach?

The main things to plan for: Flood zones vary, high insurance. Statewide about $10,384 a year for $250K (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address.
