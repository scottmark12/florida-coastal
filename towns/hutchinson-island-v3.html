About Hutchinson Island

Hutchinson Island is the long, narrow barrier island off Fort Pierce, Jensen Beach and Stuart. Most of it is low rise condos, parks and public beaches with free parking, with a few gated neighborhoods near the ends. People come for the beach, fishing and quiet. Many owners are retirees and snowbirds, and the island empties out in summer, when sea turtles nest on the beaches.

It's a barrier island, so hurricanes and flooding are the big risks. Bathtub Beach at the south end keeps losing sand and needs repair. Most homes are condos, so ask about insurance, reserves and special assessments. Groceries, doctors and hospitals are back over the bridges on the mainland.

Where it shines

Active outdoorsTop 15%

Sea turtles nest along the whole island in summer. The Florida Oceanographic Coastal Center has trails and a big game fish lagoon, and the St. Lucie Inlet Preserve sits just across the water at the south tip.

BeachesTop 17%

This is the draw. Bathtub Beach has a calm reef lagoon for wading and snorkeling, Stuart Beach and Jensen Beach Park have lifeguards and showers, and plenty of spots in between are nearly empty on a weekday.

Where it falls short

Airport accessBottom 26%

Palm Beach is about 50 minutes from the Stuart end and longer from the Fort Pierce end. Orlando is around two hours, and Treasure Coast airports have only limited service.

Meet peopleBottom 18%

Fewer organized groups on the island itself. People meet at the beach parks, on morning walks and at museum events, and the clubs and markets are a short drive over the causeways.

Dining and artsBottom 17%

A few resort restaurants, a beach cafe and the occasional fish shack. Real choice is ten minutes away in downtown Stuart or Jensen Beach.

Coastal charmBottom 11%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Common questions

Is Hutchinson Island walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. You walk the beach, not to errands. Most condos sit along the main island road with little within walking distance, and groceries are back across the river.

Does Hutchinson Island have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. This is the draw. Bathtub Beach has a calm reef lagoon for wading and snorkeling, Stuart Beach and Jensen Beach Park have lifeguards and showers, and plenty of spots in between are nearly empty on a weekday.

Is Hutchinson Island affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $400K to $490K median sale on the St. Mostly condos, with a mix of older buildings and newer oceanfront projects. Recent medians on the south island ran in the low $500Ks, but insurance, condo reserves and assessments can change the math a lot.

What is the nightlife like in Hutchinson Island?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Sunset from the beach or a lagoon side dock, then dinner at Drift or a quick ride over to Jensen or Stuart. The island goes dark early, and on summer nights you might see a turtle walk.

How busy does Hutchinson Island get?

It is quiet most of the year. Quiet most of the year. Buildings are capped at four stories on the Martin County side, so it never feels like a high rise strip. Winter weekends and spring break bring more cars to the beach lots.

Is Hutchinson Island good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Ocean on one side and the Indian River Lagoon on the other. There is a marina at the resort near the south end, and St. Lucie Inlet and Fort Pierce Inlet are close for boats heading offshore.

Which airport is closest to Hutchinson Island?

Palm Beach is about 50 minutes from the Stuart end and longer from the Fort Pierce end. Orlando is around two hours, and Treasure Coast airports have only limited service.

What should I know before moving to Hutchinson Island?

The main things to plan for: Barrier island hurricane and flood exposure, beach erosion at Bathtub Beach, condo insurance and assessments.
