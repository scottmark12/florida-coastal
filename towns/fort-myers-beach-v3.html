About Fort Myers Beach

Fort Myers Beach is on Estero Island, a narrow seven mile strip between the Gulf and Estero Bay. The sand is wide and soft, the water is gentle for wading, and the shrimp boats still work out of the back bay. Locals bike Estero Boulevard, fish, shell and watch the sun go down. It's a mix of retirees, snowbirds, longtime islanders and families.

Hurricane Ian hit the island hard in 2022, and a lot is still being rebuilt. Expect construction, new elevated homes next to empty lots, and high flood insurance. There's one main road, and winter and spring traffic on and off the island can be slow. The airport is about 40 minutes away.

A week here

Most morningsWalk the sand at low tide out toward Bowditch Point at the north tip of the island.
Late afternoonPaddle the mangrove trails of Estero Bay on the back side of the island.
SaturdayBike Estero Boulevard down to Lovers Key State Park for a swim and a picnic.
Saturday nightDixie Fish Company under the Matanzas Pass bridge. Whole fried fish, grouper and live music most nights, with boats tied up at the dock. It started as a fish market for the shrimp fleet, and it still feels like it.
SundaySunset on the beach near Little Estero Island, where neighbors bring chairs and stay until the light's gone.

Where it shines

On the waterTop 12%

Estero Bay is behind you and the Gulf in front. Shrimp boats tie up across Matanzas Pass on San Carlos Island, there is a marina at Pink Shell, and Lovers Key has a boat ramp. The pier is being rebuilt, due in 2027.

BeachesTop 15%

Seven miles of wide, flat, soft sand with shallow water, plus two more undeveloped miles at Lovers Key State Park. Public accesses line Estero Boulevard. Two county beach parks have parking closed for repairs.

NightlifeTop 24%

Still a beach bar town, just a smaller one. Live music plays at the beach bars around Times Square, at Margaritaville and along Old San Carlos Boulevard. There are fewer rooms than before Ian, and some lots are still empty.

Where it falls short

Pricey. New elevated homes and condos set the market, most of the old ground level cottages are gone, and flood and wind insurance are among the highest costs you will face on this coast.

Thin for now, and mostly event driven. The Bayside Park concert series, the Lions Club Shrimp Festival each March and the sand sculpting championship each November are the big gatherings, with Mound House for volunteering.

Coastal charmBottom 48%

A little character in spots, but mostly modern.

Active outdoorsBottom 46%

Lovers Key State Park is the standout, with the Black Island Trail, paddling and two miles of beach. Mound House runs kayak tours on Estero Bay. On the island itself, green space is limited.

Dining and artsBottom 42%

A shorter list than before the storm. The dependable tables are the seafood docks at Fisherman's Wharf, like Dixie Fish Company and Doc Ford's, and the restaurants at Margaritaville. No theaters to speak of.

Common questions

Is Fort Myers Beach walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. The north end works on foot. From Times Square you can reach the beach, the bay and Old San Carlos Boulevard in minutes. The island is seven miles long though, so farther south you bike, drive or ride LeeTran.

Does Fort Myers Beach have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. Seven miles of wide, flat, soft sand with shallow water, plus two more undeveloped miles at Lovers Key State Park. Public accesses line Estero Boulevard. Two county beach parks have parking closed for repairs.

Is Fort Myers Beach affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $492K median sale (Redfin, Sep 2026), down from a year earlier. Pricey. New elevated homes and condos set the market, most of the old ground level cottages are gone, and flood and wind insurance are among the highest costs you will face on this coast.

What is the nightlife like in Fort Myers Beach?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Start with sunset on the sand near Times Square, then live music at a beach bar or at Margaritaville. Dinner might be on the docks at Fisherman's Wharf. It is a lively few blocks surrounded by a town still rebuilding.

How busy does Fort Myers Beach get?

It gets busy, especially in season. Not a quiet island right now. Estero Boulevard is the only road, winter traffic backs up over the bridge, and construction runs on weekdays up and down the beach. Summer and the south end are calmer.

Is Fort Myers Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Estero Bay is behind you and the Gulf in front. Shrimp boats tie up across Matanzas Pass on San Carlos Island, there is a marina at Pink Shell, and Lovers Key has a boat ramp. The pier is being rebuilt, due in 2027.

Which airport is closest to Fort Myers Beach?

Southwest Florida (RSW) is about 35 to 45 minutes away. In winter, getting off the island over the bridge can add a good chunk to that, so leave early.

What should I know before moving to Fort Myers Beach?

The main things to plan for: Still rebuilding after Hurricane Ian in 2022. Check flood zones and construction nearby. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
