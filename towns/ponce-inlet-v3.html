About Ponce Inlet

Ponce Inlet sits at the southern end of the Daytona peninsula, with the tall red lighthouse, a working fishing harbor and a wide beach that wraps around the inlet. It's mostly residential, with condos along the ocean and quiet streets of houses on the river side. People here fish, boat, surf and walk the beach, and a lot of them are retirees and families who wanted a small town without leaving the coast.

The trade offs: it's a barrier island town with one road in and out, so hurricanes and storm surge are a real concern. The currents near the inlet are strong, so swim with care. Summer weekends and Daytona event weeks bring crowds to the beach and the restaurants. For big shopping and most errands you'll drive into Port Orange or Daytona.

BeachesTop 20%

Common questions

Is Ponce Inlet walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. You can walk or bike between the lighthouse, the harbor restaurants and Lighthouse Point Park. Groceries and most errands mean a drive north.
