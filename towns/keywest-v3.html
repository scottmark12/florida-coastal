About Key West

Key West is a small island town with wood frame houses, tin roofs, bikes everywhere and a sunset celebration every night at Mallory Square. Old Town is walkable and full of music, art and restaurants. People here tend to be easygoing and a little offbeat, and nobody minds if you show up to dinner in flip flops.

It's also the most expensive place in Florida to insure a home, lots are small, and the only way in by car is a long drive down the Overseas Highway, which matters when a storm is coming. If you love island life, though, there's nowhere else like it in the country.

Where it shines

Meet peopleTop 2%

On an island this small you run into people constantly. Southernmost Runners meet three times a week, the Community Sailing Center teaches and races, and sooner or later everyone ends up at the Mallory Square Sunset Celebration.

WalkabilityTop 3%

The whole island is about 4 miles long and a mile wide. Most trips in Old Town happen by bike or on foot, and a car can sit for days.

On the waterTop 3%

The water is the neighborhood. Garrison Bight has 245 slips and Charter Boat Row, the Historic Seaport lines the harbor, and the reef is about 7 miles out.

NightlifeTop 5%

Music starts before lunch. Sloppy Joe's has bands from mid morning until 2am, the bar pours until 4, and there's a stage on nearly every block of Duval Street.

Dining and artsTop 5%

A nonprofit cinema with three screens, a playhouse that has run since 1940, free galleries at The Studios of Key West, and a festival most months, from Hemingway Days to Fantasy Fest.

Coastal charmTop 6%

Full of charm: a historic downtown, local everything, no towers, and streets you want to walk.

Where it falls short

Airport accessBottom 33%

Key West's own airport (EYW) is minutes away, with six airlines and many seasonal routes. Miami is about a four hour drive.

AffordabilityBottom 6%

One of the most expensive towns in Florida, with small lots and high insurance on top.

Peace and quietBottom 3%

This is not the quiet Key. Cruise ships and festival weeks keep Old Town busy all year, and spring is the fullest.

Common questions

Is Key West walkable?

Yes. It is one of the most walkable towns on the Florida coast. The whole island is about 4 miles long and a mile wide. Most trips in Old Town happen by bike or on foot, and a car can sit for days.

Does Key West have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. The honest answer: the beaches are small. The reef blocks the waves that build wide sandy beaches, so Smathers is imported sand and Fort Zachary Taylor is rocky. Bring water shoes and a snorkel.

Is Key West affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $1.05M median (Redfin, Aug 2026). One of the most expensive towns in Florida, with small lots and high insurance on top.

What is the nightlife like in Key West?

Really good for a beach town. A night out starts at Mallory Square, where the Sunset Celebration happens every single evening. From there it's a walk or a bike ride to dinner, then live music on and around Duval Street for as long as you want. Bars can pour until 4am.

How busy does Key West get?

It is busy most of the year. This is not the quiet Key. Cruise ships and festival weeks keep Old Town busy all year, and spring is the fullest.

Is Key West good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. The water is the neighborhood. Garrison Bight has 245 slips and Charter Boat Row, the Historic Seaport lines the harbor, and the reef is about 7 miles out.

Which airport is closest to Key West?

Key West's own airport (EYW) is minutes away, with six airlines and many seasonal routes. Miami is about a four hour drive.

What should I know before moving to Key West?

The main things to plan for: Long evacuation route, high insurance, small lots. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
