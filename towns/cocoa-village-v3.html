About Cocoa Village

Cocoa Village is the old downtown of Cocoa, right on the Indian River. It has brick streets, big oaks, a historic playhouse, pubs and little shops, and it feels like a real small town. People who live nearby walk to dinner, catch a show, and spend weekends at festivals and concerts in the riverfront park.

The trade offs: there isn't much housing right in the village, so most people live in the older neighborhoods around it or a short drive away. Home condition and prices change block to block. The riverfront can flood, Brevard gets hurricanes, and festival weekends bring crowds and parking hassles.

Common questions

Is Cocoa Village walkable?

Yes, for the most part. Very walkable inside the village. Shops, restaurants, the playhouse and the riverfront are a few blocks apart. Outside that core, Cocoa is a driving city.

Does Cocoa Village have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No beach in town. Cocoa Beach is about 20 minutes east across the causeways, so most people do beach days and come back to the village for dinner.

Is Cocoa Village affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $290K typical home value for the city of Cocoa (Zillow, Jan 2026). One of the more affordable places to live near the beach. The city of Cocoa has a typical value around $290K, while restored homes near the village and on the river cost much more.

What is the nightlife like in Cocoa Village?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Dinner on a patio on Brevard Avenue, then a pint at a pub or the brewery's beer garden. On a concert night you walk down to Riverfront Park and listen by the river. It gets busy on fair weekends and is quieter on weeknights.

How busy does Cocoa Village get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Middle of the road. Weekday mornings are calm and the streets around the village are leafy, but festival weekends and concert nights bring crowds, parking hunts and noise near the river.

Is Cocoa Village good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. Right on the Indian River. There is a public boat ramp by the causeway, paddling and fishing along the river, and boats can work their way out through the lagoon. It is calm river water, not open ocean.

Which airport is closest to Cocoa Village?

Orlando (MCO) is about 40 minutes west on the Beachline, so it is one of the closer Space Coast spots to a big airport. Melbourne (MLB) is about 30 minutes south.

What should I know before moving to Cocoa Village?
