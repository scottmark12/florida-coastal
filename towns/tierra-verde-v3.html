About Tierra Verde

Tierra Verde is a set of small islands between St. Pete Beach and Fort De Soto, built around canals and docks. Most houses have a boat out back, and plenty of residents spend more weekends on the water than on land. Fort De Soto's beaches and bike trail are a few minutes away, and Pass-a-Grille is just over the bridge. It is quiet on weekdays and lively on summer weekends, popular with boaters, retirees and families who love the water.

The trade offs: these are low barrier islands with major storm surge exposure, and Helene and Milton flooded much of Tierra Verde in 2024. Elevation, construction year and flood insurance matter a great deal. There is one road in and out over the Pinellas Bayway, with tolls, and beach traffic backs up on spring and summer weekends. Property taxes are high on a new purchase.

A week here

Most morningsCoffee on the dock, then a bike ride down the paved trail into Fort De Soto Park.
Late afternoonTake the boat across to Shell Key for an hour on the sand before the tide turns.
SaturdayRun the boat out to Egmont Key with neighbors, swim off the beach and look for gopher tortoises.
Saturday nightThe Island Grille and Raw Bar by the Tierra Verde bridge. Chargrilled oysters, steel drums or acoustic music, and boats tied up at the dock.
SundayOver the bridge to Pass-a-Grille for a slow walk on the beach and sunset with half of south Pinellas.

Common questions

Is Tierra Verde walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Not very walkable. The islands are spread out, though the bike path along the Bayway to Fort De Soto is a great ride.

Does Tierra Verde have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. World class beaches next door at Fort De Soto, with North Beach and East Beach reopened after the 2024 storms, plus Shell Key by kayak or ferry and Pass-a-Grille across the channel.

Is Tierra Verde affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $640K median sale (Momentum, Nov 2026). Expensive. The recent median sale is around $840K with condos pulling it down, and waterfront houses with docks run well over $1M. Flood and wind insurance plus the Bayway tolls add up.

What is the nightlife like in Tierra Verde?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Sunset from the boat or a waterfront deck, then dinner on the islands or a short drive to Pass-a-Grille. It is a sun up, sun down kind of rhythm.

How busy does Tierra Verde get?

It is quiet most of the year. Quiet and residential most of the time. Weekends bring beach traffic through on the way to Fort De Soto.

Is Tierra Verde good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. As good as it gets. Deep canals, private docks, marinas, and a short run to Egmont Key, Shell Key, Passage Key and the Gulf, with great fishing in the passes.

Which airport is closest to Tierra Verde?

St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) and Tampa International (TPA) are both about 35 to 40 minutes away, so you get choice but not convenience.

What should I know before moving to Tierra Verde?

The main things to plan for: Low barrier islands with major surge exposure, flooded in Helene and Milton in 2024; one road in and out via the Pinellas Bayway with tolls.
