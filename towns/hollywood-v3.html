About Hollywood

Hollywood is a full size city that runs from the ocean west past I 95. Life on the east side centers on the Broadwalk, a wide brick path along the sand where people walk, run and bike every morning. Downtown has murals, small restaurants and a big park at Young Circle. Neighborhoods are a mix of 1920s and mid century homes, condo towers on the beach and family streets inland, with retirees, young families and Canadians in winter all living side by side.

The trade offs: some low streets, especially near the Intracoastal and in Hollywood Lakes, flood in heavy rain and king tides, so check the flood zone before you fall for a house. Traffic on US 1, Hollywood Boulevard and A1A gets heavy in season. Beach condos can carry high fees and special assessments.

Where it shines

Airport accessTop 3%

Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about 10 to 15 minutes from most of the city. Miami (MIA) is about 35 to 45 minutes south if you need more international flights.

WalkabilityTop 6%

The brick Broadwalk gives you nearly 2.5 miles of car free walking and biking between the cafes and the sand. Downtown around Young Circle is walkable too, and the Sun Shuttle links the two for $2 a ride.

NightlifeTop 8%

Free live music plays at the Hollywood Beach Theatre on the Broadwalk Thursday through Sunday from 7 to 9pm, with room to dance. After that it is beach bars or the downtown bars near Hollywood Boulevard. Lively, but not a late night town.

BeachesTop 10%

A long public beach runs beside the Broadwalk, with accessible entry points at streets like Johnson, Tyler and Harrison. Travel + Leisure has named the Broadwalk one of America's best beach boardwalks.

On the waterTop 20%

The Intracoastal runs right behind the beach, with a city marina and restaurants you can reach by boat, and the mangrove lakes on the north side are good for paddling. The nearest inlet is Port Everglades, a short run north.

Meet peopleTop 23%

Plenty of standing dates. Dancing at the Beach Theatre shows, weekends at Yellow Green Farmers Market, movie nights and festivals at ArtsPark at Young Circle, volunteer Beach Sweeps and an October half marathon on the Broadwalk.

Dining and artsTop 23%

Casual places line the Broadwalk, and downtown has more restaurants, the Downtown Hollywood Mural Project and festivals at ArtsPark. Dine Out Hollywood brings prix fixe menus in October. Fine dining and theater are thinner.

Where it falls short

Peace and quietBottom 5%

The Broadwalk is packed on weekends and through the winter season, and beach parking fills early. A1A and Hollywood Boulevard move slowly at peak times. Weekday mornings on the sand are the calm window.

Common questions

Is Hollywood walkable?

Yes, for the most part. The brick Broadwalk gives you nearly 2.5 miles of car free walking and biking between the cafes and the sand. Downtown around Young Circle is walkable too, and the Sun Shuttle links the two for $2 a ride.

Does Hollywood have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. A long public beach runs beside the Broadwalk, with accessible entry points at streets like Johnson, Tyler and Harrison. Travel + Leisure has named the Broadwalk one of America's best beach boardwalks.

Is Hollywood affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $478K median sale over the last 12 months (Horizon Palm Realty, Nov 2026). Middle of the range for southeast Florida. Beach condos and houses east of US 1 cost more, and neighborhoods farther west bring prices down. Flood zones near the Intracoastal push insurance up.

What is the nightlife like in Hollywood?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. A night out starts on the Broadwalk with dinner by the sand and the free 7pm show at the Beach Theatre, Thursday through Sunday. From there it is a beach bar or a ride on the Sun Shuttle to the downtown bars. Big touring concerts play at the casino resort west of town.

How busy does Hollywood get?

It gets busy, especially in season. The Broadwalk is packed on weekends and through the winter season, and beach parking fills early. A1A and Hollywood Boulevard move slowly at peak times. Weekday mornings on the sand are the calm window.

Is Hollywood good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The Intracoastal runs right behind the beach, with a city marina and restaurants you can reach by boat, and the mangrove lakes on the north side are good for paddling. The nearest inlet is Port Everglades, a short run north.

Which airport is closest to Hollywood?

Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about 10 to 15 minutes from most of the city. Miami (MIA) is about 35 to 45 minutes south if you need more international flights.

What should I know before moving to Hollywood?

The main things to plan for: Flooding in low areas, traffic. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
