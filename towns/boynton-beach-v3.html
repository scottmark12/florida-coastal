About Boynton Beach

Boynton Beach stretches from the Intracoastal all the way out to the edge of the Everglades. Near the water, it's about boats, diving off the inlet and a growing downtown on Ocean Avenue. Out west, you'll find gated 55 and over communities, golf and wetlands with long boardwalks. It's a city where retirees and working families live side by side.

Homes near the water face hurricane and surge risk. Interstate 95 and Federal Highway get crowded, especially in winter. The beach is across the bridge, and the city started charging for beach parking in October 2025. Prices vary a lot from one community to the next, so compare HOA dues and rules closely.

A week here

Late afternoonA slow loop on the Green Cay Wetlands boardwalk, looking for herons and the odd gator.

Common questions

Is Boynton Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Mostly a driving city. The downtown blocks around Ocean Avenue and the marina are walkable, and the Tri Rail station helps, but most neighborhoods need a car.

Does Boynton Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Oceanfront Park, just across the bridge, is a good public beach with a boardwalk and cabana rentals. Since October 2025 parking there costs $3.50 an hour unless you buy a city permit.

Is Boynton Beach affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $400K median sale over the past year (Horizon Palm Realty, Nov 2026). One of the more affordable coastal cities in the county. The September 2026 median sale was about $337K, pulled down by large condo communities out west; homes near the water cost much more.

What is the nightlife like in Boynton Beach?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A typical evening is dinner on the water by the marina, maybe live music on a deck, and a walk back past the boats. On festival weekends Centennial Park fills up with food and bands.

How busy does Boynton Beach get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Depends on the neighborhood. East side streets near the water are calm, while the big corridors and Interstate 95 are busy, especially in winter.

Is Boynton Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. This is where Boynton shines. The inlet leads straight to the reef, and the harbor marina runs dive, fishing and boat rental charters every day.

Which airport is closest to Boynton Beach?

West Palm Beach (DJT) is about 20 to 25 minutes north on Interstate 95, and Fort Lauderdale is about 40 minutes south for more nonstops.

What should I know before moving to Boynton Beach?

The main things to plan for: Hurricane exposure near the water, Interstate 95 and Federal Highway traffic, and new beach parking fees since October 2025.
