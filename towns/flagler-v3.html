About Flagler Beach

Flagler Beach is a small beach town with no high rises along its main stretch. A1A runs beside the beach, with little shops, surf spots and casual places to eat on the other side of the road. People surf, fish, ride bikes and walk the beach, and the town feels the same in March as it does in October. Retirees, surfers and young families all live here, and Palm Coast is close by for big stores.

The trade offs: beach erosion is a constant fight, and hurricanes have chewed up A1A and the pier more than once. A new pier is on the way. Redfin rates flood risk here as severe for about half of homes. Daytona Beach is the closest airport, and big hospitals are a drive away.

Where it shines

Active outdoorsTop 8%

Gamble Rogers has maritime hammock trails, paddling on the Intracoastal and camping, though its beachside campground and day use lot were closed for improvements as of mid 2026. Otherwise the outdoors here is surf and sand.

Coastal charmTop 16%

A charming town with a real old center, local shops and pretty streets.

Where it falls short

On the waterBottom 27%

The Intracoastal runs behind town, and Gamble Rogers Memorial State Recreation Area has a boat ramp and a kayak launch on it. The old pier is gone. An 800 foot replacement is under construction and expected to open in early 2027.

Airport accessBottom 18%

Daytona Beach (DAB) is about 35 minutes south with a short list of routes. Jacksonville (JAX) is about an hour and 15 minutes north, and Orlando (MCO) is roughly an hour and a half.

Common questions

Is Flagler Beach walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. The center is compact. From the foot of the pier you can walk to restaurants, shops and beach walkovers along A1A in a few blocks, and a bike covers the rest of town. Bigger shopping is across the bridge on SR 100.

Does Flagler Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. Coarse, golden brown coquina sand right across A1A, with free parking spots along the road and dogs allowed north of North 10th Street and south of South 10th. It is narrower than the Daytona beaches, and erosion is an ongoing fight.

Is Flagler Beach affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $450K median sale (Redfin, Aug 2026). Prices run below most Florida beach towns, especially a few blocks back from A1A. It is a barrier island, so wind and flood insurance are part of the math, and oceanfront property carries erosion risk.

What is the nightlife like in Flagler Beach?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A big night here is sunset on a deck over A1A, a band in an open air bar, and a walk back along the ocean. Things wind down early, and anything later means a drive to St. Augustine or Daytona Beach.

How busy does Flagler Beach get?

It is quiet most of the year. A1A is two lanes with no high rises, and most of the year the pace is slow. Sunny weekends and holidays bring day trip crowds and parking gets tight. For now, pier construction adds some noise and fencing in the middle of town.

Is Flagler Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The Intracoastal runs behind town, and Gamble Rogers Memorial State Recreation Area has a boat ramp and a kayak launch on it. The old pier is gone. An 800 foot replacement is under construction and expected to open in early 2027.

Which airport is closest to Flagler Beach?

Daytona Beach (DAB) is about 35 minutes south with a short list of routes. Jacksonville (JAX) is about an hour and 15 minutes north, and Orlando (MCO) is roughly an hour and a half.

What should I know before moving to Flagler Beach?

The main things to plan for: Beach erosion, hurricane exposure. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
