The Panhandle

Florida’s whitest sand and clearest green water, with real seasons. Much of it runs on Central Time and feels more small town South than South Florida. Summers are busy, winters are cool and quiet, and outside Destin and 30A it costs less than most of the coast.

Pensacola and the Emerald Coast

The western Panhandle runs from historic Pensacola east to Panama City Beach. In between are Destin's harbor and the small beach towns of 30A, all on sugar white sand and clear green water.

The Forgotten Coast

East of Panama City the high rises stop. Port St. Joe, Apalachicola and Carrabelle are small working waterfront towns on quiet bays, St. George Island has the long empty beaches, and the coast runs on east past St. Teresa to the river town of St. Marks, half an hour from Tallahassee.

The Gulf Coast

Calm, warm water and sunsets over the Gulf, from the springs and fishing villages of the Big Bend down to Naples. Tampa Bay and Sarasota have the most going on. The ends of the coast are quieter and wilder.
