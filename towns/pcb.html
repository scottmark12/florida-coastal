Panama City Beach

Big beaches, big energy and a lot going on after dark.

A day in Panama City Beach

You spend the day on wide white sand and the evening at a live music spot near the pier.

Sophisticated High Energy Voted Best Beaches in America Big Social Scene

The scorecard

Nightlife4

One of the loudest nights out on the Panhandle. Tootsie's reopened in May 2026 in the old Spinnaker building with three floors and two stages, Schooners and Sharky's have bands by the sand, and Margaritaville at Pier Park has music daily.

Meet people4

Lots of ways in. Thursday night concerts at Aaron Bessant Park all summer, free pickleball courts at the Lyndell Center, ranger led hikes at St. Andrews State Park and big events like Ironman Florida each November.

Peace and quiet1

Not quiet. Spring and summer bring heavy traffic along the beach roads, and concerts, car shows and motorcycle rallies keep the calendar full well into fall. January is about as calm as it gets.

Walkability2

Pier Park is the one place you can park once and walk to shops, restaurants, the city pier and the sand. Gayle's Trails give you paved miles for a bike. Everything else is spread along the beach road, so plan to drive.

On the water4

Grand Lagoon is the boating center, with Capt. Anderson's, Lighthouse, Pirate's Cove and Treasure Island marinas. Bay Point has 180 slips, St. Andrews State Park has a public ramp, and two long piers reach into the Gulf.

Beaches5

Wide, soft white sand for miles, with public accesses all along the beach road plus the Russell-Fields city pier and the M.B. Miller county pier. St. Andrews State Park adds jetties to snorkel and a shuttle to undeveloped Shell Island.

Dining and arts3

Mostly seafood houses and beach bars, with the dockside restaurants at Capt. Anderson's Marina a long time favorite. Festivals carry the culture side, like UNwineD in March and the Seabreeze Jazz Festival in April.

Outdoors and wellness4

Conservation Park has 24 miles of unpaved trails and a mile of boardwalks across 2,900 acres, and St. Andrews State Park has nature trails and a campground. Outside those two, most of the beach is built up.

Affordability3

Middle of the pack for the Florida coast. There is a deep supply of condos at a wide range of prices, plus houses on the bay side. Budget for condo fees and for wind and flood insurance, which run high here.

Airport access4

Northwest Florida Beaches (ECP) is about 25 minutes north of Pier Park, a little more from the east end. It is a small, easy airport. Destin Fort Walton (VPS) is a bit over an hour west if you need another option.

Common questions about Panama City Beach

Is Panama City Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Pier Park is the one place you can park once and walk to shops, restaurants, the city pier and the sand. Gayle's Trails give you paved miles for a bike. Everything else is spread along the beach road, so plan to drive.

Does Panama City Beach have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. Wide, soft white sand for miles, with public accesses all along the beach road plus the Russell-Fields city pier and the M.B. Miller county pier. St. Andrews State Park adds jetties to snorkel and a shuttle to undeveloped Shell Island.

How busy does Panama City Beach get?

It is busy most of the year. Not quiet. Spring and summer bring heavy traffic along the beach roads, and concerts, car shows and motorcycle rallies keep the calendar full well into fall. January is about as calm as it gets.

Is Panama City Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Grand Lagoon is the boating center, with Capt. Anderson's, Lighthouse, Pirate's Cove and Treasure Island marinas. Bay Point has 180 slips, St. Andrews State Park has a public ramp, and two long piers reach into the Gulf.
