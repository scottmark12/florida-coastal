About Mary Esther

Mary Esther is a quiet residential city on Santa Rosa Sound between Fort Walton Beach and Hurlburt Field. It's mostly single family homes on shady streets, with a lot of military families, retirees and people who work nearby. Life is simple here: fishing off the sound, quick trips to the beach on Okaloosa Island and everything you need along Highway 98.

The trade offs are traffic on Highway 98, aircraft noise from Hurlburt Field in some neighborhoods, and storm surge risk on the sound side. There's no real downtown. The upside is price: it's one of the more approachable places to buy on this stretch of coast.

Where it falls short

Meet peopleBottom 31%

It is a small city, so community runs through neighborhood streets, city events and the gyms and parks people share with Fort Walton Beach. Many clubs and leagues are technically next door, but they are only minutes away.

Dining and artsBottom 30%

Mostly casual spots and chains along the highway. Fort Walton Beach next door has more local restaurants, and the mall redevelopment plans call for new restaurants and a full service grocery store.

Active outdoorsBottom 28%

You have the sound for paddling and fishing, Okaloosa Island and the national seashore within easy reach, and big stretches of protected Eglin land around the area.

NightlifeBottom 18%

Very little of its own. The old Santa Rosa Mall still has a movie theater and an adventure park, and for bars and live music most people head a few minutes east to downtown Fort Walton Beach or Okaloosa Island.

Coastal charmBottom 17%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

On the waterBottom 14%

Good for a small city. Santa Rosa Sound runs along the south edge, and a small city pier and ramp off Misty Water Lane make it easy to put in a kayak or small boat.

Common questions

Is Mary Esther walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Mostly a driving town. A few neighborhoods are pleasant for a walk or bike ride along the sound, but groceries and errands are along Highway 98 and Mary Esther Boulevard.

Does Mary Esther have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. No Gulf beach in town. Okaloosa Island is about 10 to 15 minutes east over the Brooks Bridge, and Navarre Beach is about 25 minutes west.

Is Mary Esther affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $325K median sale (Momentum, Nov 2026). One of the most affordable spots on the water around here. Zillow put the typical home around $324K in September 2026, with a mix of older ranch homes, townhomes and some sound front properties.

What is the nightlife like in Mary Esther?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. A quiet dinner at home or a casual spot on Highway 98, maybe a movie at the theater by the old mall. For live music or a bar, you are ten minutes from downtown Fort Walton Beach and the island.

How busy does Mary Esther get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Side streets near the sound are quiet, but Highway 98 runs right through and gets heavy at rush hour. Hurlburt Field is next door, so you will hear aircraft, more on some streets than others.

Is Mary Esther good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. Good for a small city. Santa Rosa Sound runs along the south edge, and a small city pier and ramp off Misty Water Lane make it easy to put in a kayak or small boat.

Which airport is closest to Mary Esther?

Destin Fort Walton Beach (VPS) is about 30 minutes away, with nonstops to major hubs plus seasonal routes. Pensacola (PNS) is about an hour west.
