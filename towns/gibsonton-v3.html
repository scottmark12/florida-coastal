About Gibsonton

Gibsonton sits where the Alafia River meets Hillsborough Bay, just south of Tampa. It used to be the winter home of carnival and circus families, and some of that quirky, small town feel is still around. Today most people live in newer subdivisions off US 41 and commute to Tampa or Brandon. Prices are some of the lowest in the Tampa area, and there's water close by for fishing and boating.

The trade offs are traffic and growth. US 41 and I 75 are busy with trucks and commuters, and new subdivisions keep going up. Low areas near the river and the bay can flood in big storms, so check the flood zone. Most newer neighborhoods have an HOA, though dues tend to be small. Gulf beaches are about an hour away.

Common questions

Is Gibsonton walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. Car dependent. Most streets lack sidewalks, and stores are spread along US 41.

Does Gibsonton have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No Gulf beach. The nearest swimming is a small bay beach at Apollo Beach about 15 minutes south, and the Gulf beaches in Pinellas are about an hour away.

Is Gibsonton affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $320K median sale over the last year (Momentum, Nov 2026). One of the more affordable places near Tampa. The median sale price in the 33534 ZIP was about $415K over the past year, with new construction and older riverside homes both in the mix.

What is the nightlife like in Gibsonton?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Evenings are low key: dinner on US 41, a drink at a neighborhood bar, or a drive into Riverview or Brandon. On the river, people fish from their docks until dark.

How busy does Gibsonton get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Mixed. Older river streets are sleepy, but US 41, I 75 and new subdivisions bring steady traffic and construction noise.

Is Gibsonton good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. Good for a small boat. The Alafia River and Bullfrog Creek lead out to Hillsborough Bay, there is a small free launch at Bullfrog Creek, and some older homes sit right on the river.

Which airport is closest to Gibsonton?

Tampa International (TPA) is about 30 minutes away by I 75 and the Selmon Expressway, longer at rush hour.

What should I know before moving to Gibsonton?

The main things to plan for: Low areas near the Alafia River and Hillsborough Bay flood in big storms; heavy truck and commuter traffic on US 41 and I 75; fast growth.
