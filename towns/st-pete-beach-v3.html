About St. Pete Beach

St. Pete Beach is a barrier island town on the Gulf, with soft white sand along its whole length. At the south end, Pass-a-Grille keeps an old Florida feel with cottages, small shops and a beach you can walk to from almost anywhere. The middle of the island has condos, resorts and the pink Don CeSar, and the bay side is lined with canal homes and docks. Retirees, second home owners and families who love the beach all live here.

This is a low barrier island. Helene and Milton flooded much of it in 2024, and some homes and businesses are still rebuilding. Nearly every property carries flood risk, so insurance and elevation matter a lot. Winter and spring bring crowds and traffic on Gulf Boulevard, and the airport is about 35 minutes away.

Where it shines

On the waterTop 6%

Water on three sides. Blind Pass and Pass-a-Grille Channel run to the Gulf, canal homes line Boca Ciega Bay, and Shell Key and Fort De Soto are a short boat or paddle trip from the south end.

BeachesTop 6%

Miles of wide white sand, from Upham Beach Park in the north to Pass-a-Grille in the south, with the pink Don CeSar in between. In Pass-a-Grille you park right along the sand.

Meet peopleTop 7%

Corey Avenue is the gathering spot, with a Sunday market year round, a monthly Sip and Stroll and movies at the Beach Theatre, which reopened in 2025. The Suntan Art Center runs classes and member shows.

Dining and artsTop 15%

Corey Avenue has nearly all its storefronts open again, plus the 1940s Beach Theatre. Pass-a-Grille has Paradise Grille on the sand, and the Don CeSar's restaurants are back after its 2025 reopening.

NightlifeTop 21%

Resort beach bars along Gulf Boulevard have live music, and Corey Avenue adds a Sip and Stroll with drinks and bands on the last Friday of the month. Pass-a-Grille is quiet once the sun is down.

WalkabilityTop 23%

Two walkable pockets, Corey Avenue at the north end and 8th Avenue in Pass-a-Grille at the south. In between is a long stretch of Gulf Boulevard, covered by free Freebee rides and the SunRunner bus to St. Pete.

Where it falls short

Active outdoorsBottom 32%

Fort De Soto Park is just down the Bayway, with trails, kayaking and miles of shoreline. In town it's long beach walks, sunsets at Upham Beach and the Corey Avenue market on Sundays.

AffordabilityBottom 30%

Among the more expensive towns on this coast. Houses near the sand and in Pass-a-Grille run high, older condos are the lower rung, and flood insurance is a real line in the budget.

Peace and quietBottom 15%

Common questions

Is St. Pete Beach walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Two walkable pockets, Corey Avenue at the north end and 8th Avenue in Pass-a-Grille at the south. In between is a long stretch of Gulf Boulevard, covered by free Freebee rides and the SunRunner bus to St. Pete.

Does St. Pete Beach have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. Miles of wide white sand, from Upham Beach Park in the north to Pass-a-Grille in the south, with the pink Don CeSar in between. In Pass-a-Grille you park right along the sand.

Is St. Pete Beach affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $577K median sale (Redfin, Sep 2026). Among the more expensive towns on this coast. Houses near the sand and in Pass-a-Grille run high, older condos are the lower rung, and flood insurance is a real line in the budget.

What is the nightlife like in St. Pete Beach?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Start with the sunset, from Pass-a-Grille or a beach bar deck. Then dinner on Corey Avenue or along Gulf Boulevard and a band at one of the resort bars. For a later night the SunRunner goes to downtown St. Pete.

How busy does St. Pete Beach get?

It gets busy, especially in season. A resort town with resort traffic. Gulf Boulevard and the bridges slow down through winter and spring, parking in Pass-a-Grille is tight, and the big hotels keep the beach busy most of the year.

Is St. Pete Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Water on three sides. Blind Pass and Pass-a-Grille Channel run to the Gulf, canal homes line Boca Ciega Bay, and Shell Key and Fort De Soto are a short boat or paddle trip from the south end.

Which airport is closest to St. Pete Beach?

Tampa (TPA) is about 35 to 40 minutes by the Bayway and I-275, and St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) is about the same. Add time on spring weekends.

What should I know before moving to St. Pete Beach?

The main things to plan for: Barrier island flooding. Hit by Helene in 2024. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
