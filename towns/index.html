The Panhandle

Pensacola and the Emerald Coast

The western Panhandle runs from historic Pensacola east to Panama City Beach. In between are Destin's harbor and the small beach towns of 30A, all on sugar white sand and clear green water.

18 towns · Perdido Key, Pensacola, Gulf Breeze and moreView area

The Forgotten Coast

East of Panama City the high rises stop. Port St. Joe, Apalachicola and Carrabelle are small working waterfront towns on quiet bays, St. George Island has the long empty beaches, and the coast runs on east past St. Teresa to the river town of St. Marks, half an hour from Tallahassee.

7 towns · Port St. Joe, Mexico Beach, Apalachicola and moreView area
The Gulf Coast

The Nature Coast and Big Bend

Where the Panhandle bends into the peninsula, the coast turns to marsh and there is very little development. Life here is springs, rivers, scalloping and fishing, in towns like Cedar Key, Crystal River and Steinhatchee.

14 towns · Steinhatchee, Horseshoe Beach, Suwannee and moreView area

St. Pete and the Clearwater Beaches

A narrow peninsula between the Gulf and Tampa Bay that packs in a walkable waterfront city, a string of barrier island beach towns and a few small bayside downtowns. Tampa's airport is close to all of it.

22 towns · New Port Richey, Port Richey, Tarpon Springs and moreView area

Tampa and the South Shore

The east side of Tampa Bay is the big city and the canal communities south of it. You trade Gulf beaches for jobs, pro sports, a major airport and boating on the bay.

5 towns · Tampa, Apollo Beach, Gibsonton and moreView area

Sarasota, Bradenton and Venice

From the Manatee River down to Englewood, this stretch pairs an arts minded city with some of the Gulf's best known sand on Anna Maria Island, Longboat Key and Siesta Key. Venice adds a walkable island downtown.

9 towns · Bradenton, Bradenton Beach, Cortez and moreView area

Fort Myers, Sanibel and Charlotte Harbor

Charlotte Harbor, the Caloosahatchee River and the barrier islands of Sanibel and Captiva make this boating and shelling country. Much of it was hit hard by Hurricane Ian in 2022 and has been rebuilding since.

9 towns · Punta Gorda, Port Charlotte, Matlacha and moreView area

Naples, Marco Island and the Everglades

The polished southwest corner of the state, with calm Gulf beaches, golf and a walkable downtown in Naples. Past Marco Island the road ends at Everglades City and Chokoloskee, on the edge of the Ten Thousand Islands and Everglades National Park.

4 towns · Naples, Marco Island, Everglades City and moreView area
The Florida Keys

The Florida Keys

A chain of islands strung along one highway for more than 100 miles, from Key Largo to Key West. Everything here revolves around the water: diving, fishing, sailing and sunsets.

3 towns · Key Largo, Islamorada, Key WestView area
The Atlantic Coast

Miami and Fort Lauderdale

The most urban stretch of the Florida coast, from the Art Deco blocks of South Beach north through Hollywood to the canals of Fort Lauderdale. Two major airports, late nights and beaches with a city right behind them.

12 towns · South Beach, Miami, Bal Harbour and moreView area

The Palm Beaches

From Boca Raton up to Jupiter, this stretch mixes lively downtowns in Delray Beach and West Palm Beach with quiet oceanfront towns, golf communities and clear water at the inlets.

16 towns · Boca Raton, Highland Beach, Delray Beach and moreView area

The Treasure Coast

Named for the Spanish treasure fleet that wrecked here in 1715. It is a lower key run of river towns and uncrowded barrier island beaches from Stuart to Sebastian, with no high rise skyline.

12 towns · Stuart, Palm City, Sewall's Point and moreView area

The Space Coast

The coast around Cape Canaveral, where rocket launches are part of the view. Surf towns line the barrier island, and the mainland cities have some of the lowest prices near the Atlantic.

13 towns · Palm Bay, West Melbourne, Melbourne Beach and moreView area

Daytona, New Smyrna and Flagler

Wide, hard packed beaches run from New Smyrna Beach up through Daytona to Flagler Beach. Surf towns, race week crowds and some of the most affordable oceanfront in Florida share this stretch.

13 towns · New Smyrna Beach, Edgewater, Ponce Inlet and moreView area

The First Coast

Northeast Florida, from the brick streets of St. Augustine to Fernandina Beach on Amelia Island. Expect wide beaches, real seasons and the Jacksonville beach towns in between.
