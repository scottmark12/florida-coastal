The Panhandle

Florida’s whitest sand and clearest green water, with real seasons. It feels more small town South than South Florida, and outside Destin and 30A it costs less than most of the coast.

From the Scotts · the family behind this guide

Visit in January before you buy. The Panhandle has a real winter, and the Gulf gets too cold to swim for a few months. Our quiet pick is Port St. Joe.

The Gulf Coast

Calm, warm water and sunsets over the Gulf, from the springs and fishing villages of the Big Bend down to Naples. Tampa Bay and Sarasota have the most going on. The ends of the coast are quieter and wilder.

From the Scotts · the family behind this guide

The Gulf is calm, and that same shallow water lets storm surge pile up. Check the flood score on any Gulf town before you fall for it.

The Florida Keys

A chain of islands on one road. Boats, reefs and a slower clock, with the highest prices and the most exposure to tides and storms on our list.

From the Scotts · the family behind this guide

Rent for a whole summer before you buy. Island life in February and island life in August are two different things, and storm season is long.

The Atlantic Coast

Surf, sunrises and the biggest cities, from the Jacksonville beaches down to Miami. Busier and pricier toward the south, quieter and easier on the wallet around the Space Coast and the First Coast.

From the Scotts · the family behind this guide

Want the beach without the big city? Start with the Space Coast and the Treasure Coast. They have some of the lowest flood scores on our list.
