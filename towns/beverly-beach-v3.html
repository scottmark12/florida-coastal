About Beverly Beach

Beverly Beach is one of the smallest towns in Florida, only a few blocks wide, with A1A and the beach on one side and the Intracoastal on the other. You'll find a 55 plus manufactured home community, beach cottages, canal homes with boat slips and an RV resort. People come here to fish, boat, walk the beach and be left alone, with Flagler Beach a couple of minutes down the road for groceries and a night out.

The trade offs are real. The beach and dunes here have been eroding, the seawall at the RV resort has collapsed, and A1A takes the brunt of big storms. Beach renourishment construction is planned to start in November 2026, so expect trucks and work zones for a while. There are almost no shops in town, and the closest airport is Daytona Beach, about 40 minutes away.

A week here

Most morningsBike south on A1A to Flagler Beach and back, with the ocean beside you the whole way.
Late afternoonTake the kayak or the boat out of the canals onto the Intracoastal while the water's calm.
SaturdayChairs and a cooler on the beach across A1A, with the same neighbors from down the street.
Saturday nightFinn's Beachside Pub, just down A1A in Flagler Beach. Live music up on the rooftop and a crowd that keeps coming back.
SundayDrive up A1A to Washington Oaks Gardens State Park for a slow walk along the rocky beach.

Common questions

Is Beverly Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. You can walk to the beach and around the little neighborhoods, but there are no shops beyond the RV resort's camp store. Groceries and errands mean a short drive.

Does Beverly Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. The beach is right there, but storms have eaten away at the dunes, and the RV resort's seawall collapsed in 2026. Flagler County approved a beach and dune restoration that runs through here, with work expected to start in November 2026.

Is Beverly Beach affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $975K median sale, from only 3 sales (Redfin, Sept 2026). Mixed. What is the nightlife like in Beverly Beach?
