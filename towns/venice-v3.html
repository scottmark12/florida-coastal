About Venice

Venice is a planned 1920s town with palm lined avenues, Mediterranean style buildings and a small downtown you can walk to the beach from. A lot of people here are retirees, and the days run on beach walks, bike rides on the Legacy Trail, golf and long lunches. Newer neighborhoods spread east across the mainland, including Wellen Park, where the Braves hold spring training.

Storm surge is a real risk on the island and near the water, so check flood zones closely. Winters get busy with seasonal residents and traffic on US 41. The Sarasota airport is about 35 to 40 minutes away, and prices on the island run well above the mainland.

Coastal charmTop 10%

A charming town with a real old center, local shops and pretty streets.

On the waterTop 19%

The Intracoastal wraps the east side of the island and the jetties open to the Gulf at the north end. You can fish from the Venice Fishing Pier or the South Jetty at Humphris Park. Slips and ramps are limited.

Meet peopleTop 21%

Easy to join in. The farmers market by City Hall runs every Saturday all year, Venice Theatre has a full season and workshops, there is yoga on Venice Beach morning and evening, and downtown hosts festivals through the cooler months.

Active outdoorsTop 23%

Beach walks, shark tooth hunting and the Venetian Waterway Park trail along the Intracoastal. The Legacy Trail runs 18.5 paved miles from the Venice Train Depot to Sarasota, and the Venice Audubon Rookery on South Tamiami Trail is open dawn to dusk.

BeachesTop 24%

Venice Beach sits at the end of Venice Avenue, with more sand south to the fishing pier and a dog beach nearby. The shoreline was renourished and the pier fully reopened in September 2025. Bring a sifter for shark teeth.

Dining and artsTop 33%

A solid small town lineup. Independent restaurants and shops along Venice Avenue, Venice Theatre's season, and art and craft festivals downtown. The theater's main Jervey stage is still being rebuilt after Ian, with a 2027 reopening goal.

AffordabilityTop 40%

It depends which side of the bridge you are on. Island homes cost far more than the mainland a mile away, and older houses near the Gulf carry high flood and wind premiums. Newer inland neighborhoods cost well under the island, and being off the Gulf usually means easier flood insurance.

Peace and quietTop 46%

Slow and shaded. Venice Avenue and the bridges get busy in winter and on festival weekends, and beach parking fills by late morning. A block or two away the streets are calm all year.

NightlifeBottom 41%

Low key. Sunset drinks at Sharky's on the Pier, free concerts at the Centennial Park gazebo on the second and fourth Fridays, and a few downtown spots with music. Most of it wraps up by 9 or 10.

Common questions

Is Venice walkable?

Yes. It is one of the most walkable towns on the Florida coast. The whole 1920s town plan is walkable. Venice Avenue runs under palms from the downtown shops to the Gulf in about a mile, with Centennial Park and the Saturday market on the way. The Venetian Waterway trail follows the Intracoastal.

Does Venice have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Venice Beach sits at the end of Venice Avenue, with more sand south to the fishing pier and a dog beach nearby. The shoreline was renourished and the pier fully reopened in September 2025. Bring a sifter for shark teeth.

Is Venice affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $385K median sale (Momentum, Nov 2026). It depends which side of the bridge you are on. Island homes cost far more than the mainland a mile away, and older houses near the Gulf carry high flood and wind premiums. Newer inland neighborhoods cost well under the island, and being off the Gulf usually means easier flood insurance.

What is the nightlife like in Venice?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Catch the sunset from the pier or the South Jetty, then have dinner on Venice Avenue. On the second and fourth Fridays there is a free concert at the Centennial Park gazebo from 7 to 9. After that the island gets quiet.

How busy does Venice get?

It is quiet most of the year. Slow and shaded. Venice Avenue and the bridges get busy in winter and on festival weekends, and beach parking fills by late morning. A block or two away the streets are calm all year.

Is Venice good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The Intracoastal wraps the east side of the island and the jetties open to the Gulf at the north end. You can fish from the Venice Fishing Pier or the South Jetty at Humphris Park. Slips and ramps are limited.

Which airport is closest to Venice?

Sarasota Bradenton (SRQ) is about 35 to 40 minutes north. Punta Gorda (PGD), with Allegiant and Sun Country, is about 40 to 45 minutes south. Tampa is around an hour and a half.

What should I know before moving to Venice?

The main things to plan for: Storm surge on the island and near the water. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
