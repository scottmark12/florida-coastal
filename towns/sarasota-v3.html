About Sarasota

Sarasota mixes a lively downtown on the bay with some of the best beaches in the country just across the bridges on the keys. There's an opera, a symphony, the Ringling museum, great restaurants and neighborhoods full of old Florida bungalows. It draws retirees, families and plenty of people working from home.

Prices rise fast as you get closer to the water, and traffic on US 41 and the bridges gets heavy in season. The 2024 storms flooded some low areas.

Where it shines

NightlifeTop 9%

Decent, not wild. Main Street and Lemon Avenue have bars and taprooms with live music on weekends, the city throws free Fresh Fridays block parties on Main Street, and theater crowds fill the restaurants before and after shows.

Dining and artsTop 10%

The arts are the draw. The Sarasota Opera House on Pineapple Avenue anchors a downtown with a ballet company, an orchestra, several professional theaters and a major art museum up the bayfront, plus restaurants on nearly every block.

Meet peopleTop 11%

Very easy to plug in. The 99 Bottles Run Club draws 150 to 250 runners every Wednesday at 6pm, the Saturday farmers market has run since 1979, The Bay hosts free classes and events all year, and the Sailing Squadron has open membership.

WalkabilityTop 12%

Downtown is compact. From Main Street you can walk to the Opera House, the Saturday market at Lemon Avenue, the bayfront and The Bay park. The Legacy Trail starts on the edge of downtown and runs 18.5 miles to Venice.

Active outdoorsTop 21%

The Bay is a free waterfront park open from 5am to 11pm with classes and events. The Legacy Trail gives you 18.5 paved miles to Venice, Payne Park is the start of the Legacy Trail Ultra in November, and the farmers market runs every Saturday.

BeachesTop 24%

No beach downtown, but Lido Beach is about ten minutes over the Ringling Bridge. It is a wide public Gulf beach a few blocks from St. Armands Circle, with quieter sand toward the north end. Siesta Beach, number 28 on the World's 50 Best Beaches list for 2026, is about 20 minutes. Bridge traffic adds time in winter.

Where it falls short

Peace and quietBottom 9%

Busy. US 41 and the Ringling Bridge back up in winter, and downtown parking fills on show nights and market mornings. Summer is noticeably calmer, and the neighborhoods east of downtown are quieter all year.

Common questions

Is Sarasota walkable?

Yes, for the most part. Downtown is compact. From Main Street you can walk to the Opera House, the Saturday market at Lemon Avenue, the bayfront and The Bay park. The Legacy Trail starts on the edge of downtown and runs 18.5 miles to Venice.

Does Sarasota have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. No beach downtown, but Lido Beach is about ten minutes over the Ringling Bridge. It is a wide public Gulf beach a few blocks from St. Armands Circle, with quieter sand toward the north end. Siesta Beach, number 28 on the World's 50 Best Beaches list for 2026, is about 20 minutes. Bridge traffic adds time in winter.

Downtown condos and bayfront homes are expensive, but neighborhoods a few miles inland cost much less and sit outside the worst surge zones, which helps on insurance.

What is the nightlife like in Sarasota?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Dinner downtown, then a show at the Opera House or one of the theaters around it. Afterward the bars along Main Street and Lemon Avenue carry on with live music on weekends. On Fresh Fridays, Main Street itself turns into the party.

How busy does Sarasota get?

It gets busy, especially in season. Busy. US 41 and the Ringling Bridge back up in winter, and downtown parking fills on show nights and market mornings. Summer is noticeably calmer, and the neighborhoods east of downtown are quieter all year.

Is Sarasota good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Sarasota Bay sits at the foot of Main Street, with a big downtown marina, public boat ramps and passes to the Gulf a short run away. The Sarasota Sailing Squadron on City Island offers adult lessons and open membership.

Which airport is closest to Sarasota?
