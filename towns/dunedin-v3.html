About Dunedin

Dunedin is a small city on St. Joseph Sound with a busy Main Street of restaurants, breweries and shops, and the Pinellas Trail running right through downtown. Honeymoon and Caladesi Islands, two of the best beaches on the Gulf, are just up the causeway. The Blue Jays hold spring training here, and people of all ages walk, bike and stay out on the patios.

Homes near the water face storm surge, so check flood zones closely. Downtown is busy on weekends and in winter, and parking can be tight. Single family homes near downtown cost a lot more than the condos around town.

Coastal charmTop 3%

Full of charm: a historic downtown, local everything, no towers, and streets you want to walk.

BeachesTop 9%

No beach downtown, but Honeymoon Island State Park is a short drive over the Causeway with more than four miles of sand. It's also where the ferry leaves for Caladesi Island.

Active outdoorsTop 10%

The Pinellas Trail runs right through town. Honeymoon Island has the Osprey Trail through old slash pine, Caladesi has a mangrove paddling trail, and the Downtown Market covers the produce.

Meet peopleTop 18%

Easy to plug in. The Dunedin Boat Club races and holds socials, the Fine Art Center runs classes, the Downtown Market is a weekly habit and the Pinellas Trail is busy with riders and runners every morning.

Dining and artsTop 18%

A lot of good cooking in a small footprint, from The Black Pearl and The Restorative to Casa Tina. Add the Dunedin Fine Art Center, a January art festival and the Highland Games each April.

Airport accessTop 20%

Two airports within easy reach. St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) is about 25 minutes away and Tampa (TPA) is about 35, depending on traffic over the bay.

Common questions

Is Dunedin walkable?

Yes. It is one of the most walkable towns on the Florida coast. You can do most days on foot or by bike. Main Street, the marina, the Downtown Market and the brewery sit within a few blocks, the Pinellas Trail cuts through the middle and Freebee gives free rides.

Does Dunedin have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. No beach downtown, but Honeymoon Island State Park is a short drive over the Causeway with more than four miles of sand. It's also where the ferry leaves for Caladesi Island.

Is Dunedin affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $450K median (Momentum, Jun 2026). Not cheap, but not the top of the Pinellas market either. Bungalows near downtown carry a premium for the walkability. Homes and condos farther east cost less and tend to sit higher, which helps on insurance.

What is the nightlife like in Dunedin?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. You park once and walk. Dinner on Main Street, then a band at Dunedin Brewery, which has two stages and stays open until midnight on Friday and Saturday. Weeknights are mellower, with an open jam on Wednesdays.

How busy does Dunedin get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Downtown hums most evenings, and festival weekends and spring training games at TD Ballpark pack the streets. Parking is free but fills up. A few blocks off Main Street it's calm.

Is Dunedin good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Dunedin Marina sits at the foot of Main Street on St. Joseph Sound, and the Dunedin Boat Club, founded in 1929, is based there. The Causeway is the spot for kayaks, paddleboards and fishing.

Which airport is closest to Dunedin?

Two airports within easy reach. St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) is about 25 minutes away and Tampa (TPA) is about 35, depending on traffic over the bay.
