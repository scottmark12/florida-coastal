About Keaton Beach

Keaton Beach is a small cluster of canal homes on the Taylor County coast, about 20 minutes from Perry. Life runs on the water: trout and redfish on the grass flats, scallops in summer, and a boat lift behind most houses. It's mostly fishing families, weekenders and a few year round neighbors who all know each other.

The trade offs are serious. Idalia in 2023 and Helene in 2024 both came ashore here, and county leaders estimated Helene destroyed about 90% of homes. Rebuilding is slow, many lots are empty or have campers on them, and flood insurance and elevation rules drive the cost of any house. There's no grocery store or hospital in town, and Tallahassee's airport is about an hour and a half away.

A week here

Most morningsCoffee on the dock, then out across the grass flats for trout and redfish while the water's still slick.
Late afternoonA swim and a walk at Hodges Park, where neighbors trade fishing reports by the water.
SaturdayIn scallop season, a boat full of friends with masks and mesh bags on the grass beds off Keaton Beach. The rest of the year, the flats at first light.
Saturday nightSupper at Roy's, about 25 miles down the coast by the mouth of the Steinhatchee River. They're cooking the old seafood favorites from a trailer in the parking lot while the restaurant is rebuilt after Helene, and regulars keep coming.
SundayA slow ride along the marsh shoreline, then a fish fry on the porch with whatever came home in the cooler.

Where it falls short

BeachesBottom 26%

There is a small sandy beach at Hodges Park, the county's main public beach, but Helene destroyed the park and the county only approved its rebuild budget in late 2025. The Gulf here is shallow and grassy, better for wading than swimming.

Active outdoorsBottom 19%

Big open marsh, the Big Bend Seagrasses Aquatic Preserve offshore and miles of undeveloped coastline make it great for paddling, birding and fishing. Inland, the area is mostly pine forest and timberland.

Dining and artsBottom 5%

Very limited. Most people cook what they catch, and the closest real restaurant choices are in Perry or up the coast in Steinhatchee.

Meet peopleBottom 3%

It is a tight knit place where people know each other from the ramp and the canals. There are no organized clubs here, so you meet neighbors by fishing, helping on a rebuild or showing up for the summer holidays at the water.

Airport accessBottom 3%

Tallahassee (TLH) is about an hour and a half north through Perry. It is a small airport, so most trips mean a connection.

NightlifeBottom 1%

There is no nightlife to speak of. Evenings are a grill on the porch and the sunset over the marsh, and for a restaurant or a bar you drive into Perry.

WalkabilityBottom 1%

Not a walking town. You can stroll the canal streets, but every errand means getting in the truck and heading to Perry.

Common questions

Is Keaton Beach walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. Not a walking town. You can stroll the canal streets, but every errand means getting in the truck and heading to Perry.

Does Keaton Beach have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. There is a small sandy beach at Hodges Park, the county's main public beach, but Helene destroyed the park and the county only approved its rebuild budget in late 2025. The Gulf here is shallow and grassy, better for wading than swimming.

Is Keaton Beach affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: Too few sales for a town median. Land and older homes are cheap by Florida standards, and there are few sales after Helene. The real costs are building to current elevation rules, flood and wind insurance, and the risk of another storm.

What is the nightlife like in Keaton Beach?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. You clean fish at the dock, fire up the grill and watch the sun go down over the marsh with whoever is around. On summer weekends the canals get busier and neighbors drift between porches. In winter it is very still.

Is Keaton Beach good for boating and fishing?
