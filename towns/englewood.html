Englewood

A laid back Gulf town with a small old downtown and beaches on Manasota Key, known for shark teeth.

A day in Englewood

You walk Stump Pass Beach looking for shark teeth, then head to Dearborn Street for the farmers market.

The scorecard

Nightlife1

Early nights. A few waterfront bars and tiki decks have music in the late afternoon and early evening, and Lemon Bay Playhouse on Dearborn Street puts on plays, but most of town is dark by 9.

Meet people3

Enough to get started. The Thursday Market on Dearborn runs October through May, the all volunteer Englewood Sailing Association sails from Indian Mound Park with a social sail on Wednesdays, and the playhouse is in its 39th season.

Peace and quiet4

Laid back most of the year. State Road 776 and the bridges to Manasota Key slow down in winter, and the beach lots fill on sunny weekends, but there is no real rush hour and summer is very quiet.

Walkability2

Dearborn Street is a pleasant few blocks of shops, markets and the playhouse. Everything else, including the beaches on Manasota Key, the grocery stores and the boat ramps, is a drive.

On the water4

Lemon Bay runs the length of town, sheltered and good for small boats and kayaks, with Stump Pass leading out to the Gulf. Indian Mound Park has a public ramp near Dearborn Street, and the state park has a paddle launch.

Beaches4

Englewood Beach at Chadwick Park has a 900 foot boardwalk, pavilions and volleyball courts. Stump Pass Beach State Park, at the south tip of the key, is a quieter mile of sand for $3 a car. Both are good for shark teeth.

Dining and arts2

Simple and casual. Fish houses, a few spots on Dearborn Street and plays at Lemon Bay Playhouse. A restaurant week called Let's Eat Englewood adds some variety. For more choice you drive to Venice or Punta Gorda.

Outdoors and wellness4

Stump Pass Beach State Park is a narrow strip of sand and trail between the Gulf and Lemon Bay, with ranger led hikes in winter. Add kayaking on the bay, four farmers markets along Dearborn Street in season and shelling on the key.

Affordability4

Still one of the more affordable Gulf towns south of Tampa Bay. Homes inland of Lemon Bay cost well under Venice or Sarasota. Anything on Manasota Key or the bay is far pricier, and flood insurance needs a close look.

Airport access3

Punta Gorda (PGD) is about 40 minutes away, with Allegiant and Sun Country. Sarasota Bradenton (SRQ) is about an hour north, and Fort Myers (RSW) is a little over an hour south.

Common questions about Englewood

Is Englewood walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Dearborn Street is a pleasant few blocks of shops, markets and the playhouse. Everything else, including the beaches on Manasota Key, the grocery stores and the boat ramps, is a drive.

Does Englewood have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Englewood Beach at Chadwick Park has a 900 foot boardwalk, pavilions and volleyball courts. Stump Pass Beach State Park, at the south tip of the key, is a quieter mile of sand for $3 a car. Both are good for shark teeth.

Is Englewood affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: $329K median. Still one of the more affordable Gulf towns south of Tampa Bay. Homes inland of Lemon Bay cost well under Venice or Sarasota. Anything on Manasota Key or the bay is far pricier, and flood insurance needs a close look.

What is the nightlife like in Englewood?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Sunset on Manasota Key is the main event. After that it is dinner at a fish house or a play at Lemon Bay Playhouse on Dearborn Street, and most of town is dark by 9. The exception is the November weekend when powerboat races fill Englewood Beach.

How busy does Englewood get?

It is quiet most of the year. Laid back most of the year. State Road 776 and the bridges to Manasota Key slow down in winter, and the beach lots fill on sunny weekends, but there is no real rush hour and summer is very quiet.

Is Englewood good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Lemon Bay runs the length of town, sheltered and good for small boats and kayaks, with Stump Pass leading out to the Gulf. Indian Mound Park has a public ramp near Dearborn Street, and the state park has a paddle launch.

Which airport is closest to Englewood?

Punta Gorda (PGD) is about 40 minutes away, with Allegiant and Sun Country. Sarasota Bradenton (SRQ) is about an hour north, and Fort Myers (RSW) is a little over an hour south.
