About Sewall's Point

Sewall's Point is a small town on a narrow peninsula between the St. Lucie and Indian rivers. The streets are shaded and quiet, and many homes have docks on deep water. People bike the main road, keep a boat out back, and cross one bridge to the beaches on Hutchinson Island or the other to downtown Stuart.

It's expensive, and the low peninsula is exposed to hurricanes and flooding, so insurance costs are high. River water quality can suffer after Lake Okeechobee releases. There's very little shopping in town, so you'll drive to Stuart for most errands. West Palm Beach airport is about 45 minutes away.

A week here

Most morningsA walk or bike ride down South Sewall's Point Road under the oaks, waving at the same neighbors every day.
Late afternoonOver the bridge to Stuart Beach on Hutchinson Island for a swim before dinner.
SaturdayTake the boat out to snorkel Bathtub Reef Beach, or just drift on the Indian River with friends.
Saturday nightConchy Joe's on the river in Jensen Beach. Bahamian style seafood, conch fritters and live music, and the locals have been gathering there for decades.
SundayThe Sunday green market in downtown Stuart, then a long lunch on the St. Lucie.

Common questions

Is Sewall's Point walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Good for walking and biking the road that runs the length of the point, and cyclists do loops through here. For a coffee or groceries you head into Stuart.

Does Sewall's Point have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. The point itself has small river beaches, not surf. The ocean at Stuart Beach is about five minutes across the bridge to Hutchinson Island.

Is Sewall's Point affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $2.2M median sale (Redfin, Sep 2026). One of the priciest addresses on the Treasure Coast. Medians have run around $1.5M to $2.2M in a market with only a few dozen sales a year, plus high waterfront insurance.

What is the nightlife like in Sewall's Point?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Drinks on the dock as the sun drops over the river, then dinner in downtown Stuart a few minutes away. Back home it is quiet by ten.

Which airport is closest to Sewall's Point?
