About Riviera Beach

Riviera Beach is two places. The mainland is a working city with the Port of Palm Beach, a large public marina and charter boats heading out to fish. Across the bridge, Singer Island is a strip of condo towers and beaches between the ocean and Lake Worth, with Peanut Island and the inlet just south. Boaters, divers and anglers feel right at home.

The trade offs: Singer Island takes the full weather in a hurricane, and its prices and insurance are much higher than the mainland's. The mainland varies a lot block by block, so look closely at each street. Older condos on the island may have big fees or assessments, so check the association's reserves.

Where it shines

On the waterTop 10%

Hard to beat for boaters. The inlet is minutes away, the city marina has water taxis to Peanut Island, and Phil Foster Park has four boat ramps open around the clock plus a fishing pier. Near the inlet the water runs clear on an incoming tide.

Airport accessTop 10%

About 15 minutes to the Palm Beach airport by I-95. Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is a little over an hour south. Few beach addresses in Florida sit this close to a commercial airport.

Where it falls short

Coastal charmBottom 30%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Peace and quietBottom 29%

A mix. The port, Blue Heron Boulevard and Broadway carry trucks and steady traffic. Singer Island is calmer but fills up on sunny weekends and in winter, when the beach lots and the bridge get busy.

Meet peopleBottom 28%

Organized clubs are thin here, and most of what exists revolves around the water. You get talking at the marina, on the Peanut Island dock or with the divers at Phil Foster Park around high tide. For leagues and run clubs, look to West Palm Beach.

WalkabilityBottom 27%

Singer Island has a small walkable pocket where the municipal beach park, a few shops and restaurants sit together. On the mainland things are spread out along wide roads, and most trips need a car.

Active outdoorsBottom 25%

The outdoors here is mostly wet. Peanut Island Park has a snorkeling lagoon and tent camping, and Phil Foster Park has a snorkel trail by the Blue Heron Bridge that divers travel a long way for. On land, parks are small and trails are short.

Dining and artsBottom 18%

A modest lineup of casual seafood spots and waterfront decks near the marina and on Singer Island. You will not find a gallery row or a theater district. For that, West Palm Beach and Palm Beach Gardens are both about 15 minutes away.

About average for 3 more: beaches, affordability and nightlife
BeachesTop 34%

Singer Island's ocean beaches are long and less crowded than the ones farther south. Riviera Beach Municipal Beach Park has a promenade and good shelling after high tide, and Peanut Island adds a lagoon beach for snorkeling.

AffordabilityTop 47%

Two markets in one city. Mainland houses and condos are among the lower priced options near the water in Palm Beach County. Singer Island's oceanfront towers cost far more and carry barrier island insurance and condo fees.

NightlifeBottom 43%

Low key. A few waterfront bars and tiki decks around the marina and on Singer Island have music on weekends, and things wrap up early. For a real night out you drive 10 or 15 minutes south to Clematis Street.

Common questions

Is Riviera Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Singer Island has a small walkable pocket where the municipal beach park, a few shops and restaurants sit together. On the mainland things are spread out along wide roads, and most trips need a car.

Does Riviera Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Singer Island's ocean beaches are long and less crowded than the ones farther south. Riviera Beach Municipal Beach Park has a promenade and good shelling after high tide, and Peanut Island adds a lagoon beach for snorkeling.

Is Riviera Beach affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $398K median sale (Resideline, Nov 2026). Two markets in one city. Mainland houses and condos are among the lower priced options near the water in Palm Beach County. Singer Island's oceanfront towers cost far more and carry barrier island insurance and condo fees.

What is the nightlife like in Riviera Beach?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Evenings are a sunset drink on a waterfront deck by the marina or on Singer Island, maybe with a band on the weekend, and an early finish. When you want more, Clematis Street in West Palm Beach is a short drive down U.S. 1.

How busy does Riviera Beach get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. A mix. The port, Blue Heron Boulevard and Broadway carry trucks and steady traffic. Singer Island is calmer but fills up on sunny weekends and in winter, when the beach lots and the bridge get busy.

Is Riviera Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. Hard to beat for boaters. The inlet is minutes away, the city marina has water taxis to Peanut Island, and Phil Foster Park has four boat ramps open around the clock plus a fishing pier. Near the inlet the water runs clear on an incoming tide.

Which airport is closest to Riviera Beach?

About 15 minutes to the Palm Beach airport by I-95. Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is a little over an hour south. Few beach addresses in Florida sit this close to a commercial airport.
