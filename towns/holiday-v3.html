About Holiday

Holiday is a quiet, mostly residential place just north of Tarpon Springs. Neighborhoods of small block homes from the 1960s and 1970s sit inland off US 19, while to the west canal streets run out toward the Anclote River and the open Gulf. A lot of people here are retirees and working families who wanted Gulf access on a budget. Life is simple: fishing, boating out to Anclote Key, and the Pinellas Trail a few minutes south.

The trade offs are real. The low coastal neighborhoods west of US 19 flood in storm surges, so check elevation and flood history before you fall for a canal house. The Anclote fishing pier took hurricane damage. US 19 is a long run of traffic lights and strip malls, and the town has no real downtown of its own. Tampa International is about 40 minutes away.

Where it falls short

Peace and quietBottom 34%

The neighborhoods are quiet and settled. US 19 runs through the middle and is loud, busy and commercial.

NightlifeBottom 28%

Not much of it. A few neighborhood bars and grills, and that is the scene. For a night out people head next door to Tarpon Springs or up to downtown New Port Richey.

Meet peopleBottom 26%

Mostly at the parks, the boat ramps and the fishing pier. Holiday is unincorporated, so a lot of community life runs through Pasco County parks and nearby Tarpon Springs.

Active outdoorsBottom 21%

Good for a quiet coast. Anclote Gulf Park, Anclote River Park and Werner Boyce Salt Springs State Park are all nearby, and Anclote Key has trails, a lighthouse and primitive camping.

Coastal charmBottom 20%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Dining and artsBottom 14%

Mostly casual spots and chains along US 19. The Sponge Docks in Tarpon Springs, ten minutes away, are where you go for Greek food, bakeries and seafood.

BeachesBottom 13%

No real sand beach in town. Anclote Gulf Park has a boardwalk and a fishing pier with Gulf views, and the actual beaches are on Anclote Key by boat or at Fred Howard Park in Tarpon Springs, about 15 minutes away.

WalkabilityBottom 11%

Common questions

Is Holiday walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. Hard to get around on foot. It is built around US 19, so you drive for almost everything.

Does Holiday have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No real sand beach in town. Anclote Gulf Park has a boardwalk and a fishing pier with Gulf views, and the actual beaches are on Anclote Key by boat or at Fred Howard Park in Tarpon Springs, about 15 minutes away.

Is Holiday affordable?

Yes. It is one of the more affordable spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $220K median sale (Momentum, Nov 2026). One of the cheapest coastal towns in Tampa Bay, with median sales in the $220Ks to $240Ks. Older block homes and canal houses are common, and flood insurance is the cost to watch.

What is the nightlife like in Holiday?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Sunset from the boardwalk at Anclote Gulf Park, then dinner over in Tarpon Springs or something simple close to home. It is an early to bed kind of place.

How busy does Holiday get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. The neighborhoods are quiet and settled. US 19 runs through the middle and is loud, busy and commercial.

Is Holiday good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Strong. The Anclote River runs right to the Gulf, many canal homes have docks, and Anclote River Park has a boat ramp. The flats, the Anclote Key sandbars and the Tarpon Springs sponge boats are all close.

Which airport is closest to Holiday?

Tampa International (TPA) is about 40 minutes via the Suncoast Parkway or US 19. Easy to fly almost anywhere from there.

What should I know before moving to Holiday?

The main things to plan for: Low coastal neighborhoods west of US 19 flood in surges; the Anclote pier had hurricane damage; US 19 traffic and strip malls. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
