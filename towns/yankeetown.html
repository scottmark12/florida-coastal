Yankeetown

An old river town on the Withlacoochee a few miles from the Gulf, with live oaks, fish camps and a big nature preserve at the end of the road.

A day in Yankeetown

You launch a kayak at Bird Creek Park, paddle the marsh out toward the Gulf, and spend the afternoon watching birds from the tower at the Withlacoochee Gulf Preserve.

The scorecard

Tap any score for the local's take.

Nightlife1

Very little. You might catch music at the seafood festival in November, but most nights are quiet, and the closest bars and restaurants are in Inglis and Crystal River.

Meet people3

Better than you would guess for a town this size. The Inglis Yankeetown Lions Club runs the seafood festival, the preserve has volunteer and education programs, and the marina and boat ramp are social places.

Peace and quiet5

Very quiet. It is a dead end river town, so the only traffic is people who live here or came to fish and paddle.

Walkability2

You can walk or bike the shady streets along the river and ride out to the preserve. Groceries and most errands are a short drive to Inglis or about 20 minutes to Crystal River.

On the water5

Great. The Withlacoochee River runs through town to the Gulf, there is a public boat launch and a marina with fishing guides, and Bird Creek Park is a popular kayak launch to the open water.

Beaches1

No real beach. The coast here is marsh and islands, so the Gulf is something you boat or paddle to rather than lie next to.

Dining and arts2

Limited. There is little to choose from in town beyond the seafood festival each fall, so for regular meals out you drive to Inglis or Crystal River.

Outdoors and wellness5

Excellent. The Withlacoochee Gulf Preserve has a tower, boardwalk and trails, the Cross Florida Greenway starts nearby, and Crystal River's manatee springs are about 20 minutes south.

Affordability4

One of the cheaper river towns on the Gulf, with a median sold price around $254K in September 2026. Flood insurance is the big variable, since the whole town is in a FEMA flood zone.

Airport access2

Gainesville (GNV) is about an hour and 15 minutes away for short hops, and Tampa (TPA) is about an hour and 45 minutes for more direct flights.
