About Gulfport

Gulfport is a small town on Boca Ciega Bay, between St. Petersburg and the Gulf beaches. The Waterfront District has galleries, cafes, bars, a small beach and a pier, and the town is known for a friendly, quirky, welcoming crowd. People walk or bike to dinner, turn out for street events and know their neighbors by name.

Low lying streets flood, and Helene hit Gulfport hard in 2024. Some businesses took more than a year to reopen. Check flood zone, elevation and repair history on any home, and get insurance quotes early. Most homes are older and on the smaller side.

Common questions

Is Gulfport walkable?

Yes, for the most part. Beach Boulevard runs a few blocks from the shops and galleries down to the bay beach, with restaurants on Shore Boulevard along the water. It's all flat and close. The marina and Clam Bayou are a short bike ride east.

Does Gulfport have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. A small bayfront beach on Boca Ciega Bay with volleyball courts and calm water. It's pleasant, but it is not a Gulf beach. For surf and wide sand you drive about 15 minutes to St. Pete Beach.

Is Gulfport affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $385K median sale (Momentum, Oct 2026). Bungalows and cottages cost less than homes on the Gulf islands, though prices have climbed. Low streets flooded in Helene, so elevation and flood insurance can change the math from block to block.

What is the nightlife like in Gulfport?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. You park once and walk. Dinner on Beach Boulevard, then live music at a waterfront bar on Shore Boulevard, or the Night Market on a first Friday or third Saturday. It's mellow and winds down earlier than downtown St. Pete.

How busy does Gulfport get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Slow on an ordinary day, with no resort strip and free parking in the village. Market Tuesdays, Night Markets and festivals pack Beach Boulevard, and waterfront repairs were still under way in 2026.

Is Gulfport good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The Gulfport Municipal Marina has a public ramp with 70 trailer spaces, a fuel dock and a kayak launch beside Clam Bayou. Boca Ciega Yacht Club races on the bay, and the Gulf is about four miles out.

Which airport is closest to Gulfport?

Close to both. Tampa (TPA) is about 30 minutes up I-275, and St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) is about the same. Downtown St. Petersburg is 15 minutes away.

What should I know before moving to Gulfport?

The main things to plan for: Flooding in low lying streets. Hit hard by Helene in 2024. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
