About Key Biscayne

Key Biscayne is an island village across the Rickenbacker Causeway from Miami. Crandon Park covers the north end and Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park, home of the Cape Florida Lighthouse, covers the south end, with the village in between. Key Biscayne K-8 Center is on the island and MAST Academy is on Virginia Key.

It's among the most expensive places in Florida, with a median sale around $1.49M. The causeway is the only way on and off, and Miami's airport is about 25 minutes away.
