About Weeki Wachee

Weeki Wachee is less a town than a stretch of Hernando County built around one famous spring and the clear river that flows out of it to the Gulf. People here paddle, swim, fish and boat, and many live in quiet subdivisions a few minutes inland. Down by the coast, Hernando Beach has canal homes with boats out back. It's a mix of retirees, young families and Tampa commuters who want more room for less money.

The trade offs: riverfront and canal homes near the coast sit in storm surge zones, and Helene flooded low areas of western Hernando in 2024. Summer weekends get busy at the springs and on the river, and the state park limits paddle launches. Tampa's airport is about 55 minutes away, and most errands mean driving US 19.

Common questions

Is Weeki Wachee walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. You will drive for nearly everything. Neighborhoods are spread out with few sidewalks, and shopping is along US 19 and in Spring Hill.

Does Weeki Wachee have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No Gulf beach in town. Rogers Park has a small river beach for swimming, and Pine Island beach in Hernando Beach is about 15 minutes west.

Is Weeki Wachee affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $375K median sale (Momentum, Oct 2026). Affordable for Florida, with typical values in the mid $300Ks in North Weeki Wachee. Riverfront homes in Weeki Wachee Gardens cost more and carry real flood insurance costs.

What is the nightlife like in Weeki Wachee?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Evenings start with sunset on the river, maybe at Upper Deck by Rogers Park, then home early. Weekends bring friends over for a cookout after a day on the water. For a big night you drive to Tampa.

How busy does Weeki Wachee get?

It is quiet most of the year. Mostly calm, wooded and residential. US 19 and SR 50 get busy, and summer weekends bring crowds to the springs and Rogers Park, but most streets are peaceful.

Is Weeki Wachee good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The Weeki Wachee River is spring fed and clear blue, with kayaking, paddleboarding and manatees in cooler months. State park launches are limited and need a reservation, and Rogers Park downstream has a ramp and a river beach.

Which airport is closest to Weeki Wachee?

Tampa (TPA) is about 55 minutes south on the Suncoast Parkway. St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) and Orlando (MCO) are both farther.
