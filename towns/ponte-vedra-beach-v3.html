About Ponte Vedra Beach

Ponte Vedra Beach is spread out and green, with gated golf communities, older homes near the ocean and wild preserve land to the south. Days revolve around golf, the beach, boating on the Intracoastal and kids' sports. It draws families, executives and retirees who want space, privacy and a polished feel close to Jacksonville.

The trade offs: it's expensive, and public beach access is limited, so where you live decides how easily you get to the sand. HOA dues can be high. It's a driving town with little you can walk to, hurricanes are a real risk on the coast, and the area gets busy when The Players comes in March.

Where it shines

Active outdoorsTop 5%

The GTM Research Reserve protects more than 76,000 acres along this coast, with trails, fishing and a visitor center on Guana River Road. Closer in, Bird Island Park has a boardwalk through native plantings, and golf is everywhere.

Where it falls short

WalkabilityBottom 15%

This is a driving town. Homes, golf courses and shops are spread out along A1A and Ponte Vedra Boulevard, and the main cluster of shops and restaurants is Sawgrass Village. Plan on a car for nearly every errand.

AffordabilityBottom 13%

One of the most expensive places to buy in Northeast Florida. Oceanfront houses and golf course communities set the prices, and anything near the beach needs wind and flood coverage on top. Lower prices start well inland.

On the waterBottom 3%

Dining and artsBottom 45%

Resort dining rooms and the restaurants at Sawgrass Village cover most nights out. The Ponte Vedra Concert Hall reopened in August 2025 after a $12.9 million renovation, and First Coast Cultural Center hangs rotating art exhibitions.

NightlifeBottom 42%

Very quiet after dark. Apart from show nights at the Ponte Vedra Concert Hall, an evening out is dinner at a resort or in Sawgrass Village and home early. For bars and late music you drive about 20 minutes north to Jacksonville Beach.

Meet peopleBottom 39%

Plenty of organized ways to join in. About 900 volunteers work with the GTM Research Reserve, The Players signs up volunteers for tournament week every March, and First Coast Cultural Center in Sawgrass Village runs art classes, lectures and exhibitions.

Common questions

Is Ponte Vedra Beach walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. This is a driving town. Homes, golf courses and shops are spread out along A1A and Ponte Vedra Boulevard, and the main cluster of shops and restaurants is Sawgrass Village. Plan on a car for nearly every errand.

Does Ponte Vedra Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Tall dunes and a long, uncrowded shore. Public access is limited, though. Mickler's Landing is the main spot, with about 200 parking spaces, restrooms and summer lifeguards, and a few more lots sit farther south along the GTM reserve.
