About Ormond Beach

Ormond Beach feels like a settled hometown. Big live oaks shade the roads, Granada Boulevard runs from the mainland over the river to the ocean, and people spend their days biking, paddling the Tomoka River or walking the beach. It draws retirees, families and people who want to be near Daytona without living in the middle of it.

The trade offs: hurricanes and storm surge are a real risk, especially beachside and near the river, and flood insurance can add up. Event weeks in Daytona still spill traffic north. Newer communities west of I 95 are a fair drive from the beach, and the best beachside streets cost more than people expect.

Where it shines

Dining and artsTop 27%

A good run of independent restaurants on Granada Boulevard, plus a local art museum and a performing arts center. The festival calendar includes the Granada Grand Festival of the Arts, the Celtic Festival and Taste of Ormond.

Active outdoorsTop 29%

The Ormond Scenic Loop winds under old oaks and along the river and ocean. Tomoka State Park has paddling on the Tomoka River, a nature trail through hardwood hammock, camping and more than 160 bird species on its list.

Common questions

Is Ormond Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Downtown is a stretch of Granada Boulevard on both sides of the bridge, with City Hall Plaza and Rockefeller Gardens by the river and the beach about a mile east. It makes a pleasant walk, but most errands still need a car.

Does Ormond Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. The same wide, hard packed sand as Daytona with a lot less going on behind it. Some stretches allow cars and others are traffic free, and there is public access all along A1A.

Is Ormond Beach affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $383K median sale over the last six months (Resideline, Oct 2026). Generally below the coastal Florida median, with plenty of choice on the mainland. Beach side and riverfront homes cost more and carry higher wind and flood insurance, so compare both sides of the bridge.

What is the nightlife like in Ormond Beach?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Dinner on Granada Boulevard, maybe a drink on a patio after, and the evening is over at a reasonable hour. When you want it later and louder, Seabreeze Boulevard and Main Street in Daytona Beach are about 15 minutes south.

How busy does Ormond Beach get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Noticeably calmer than Daytona, with oak shaded streets off the main roads. Granada Boulevard and A1A get busy, and Bike Week and Biketoberfest bring riders through town and out along the scenic loop.

Is Ormond Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. You have the Halifax River through the middle of town and the Tomoka River to the north. Tomoka State Park has a boat ramp and canoe rentals at the Tomoka Outpost. The ocean is a long run south to Ponce Inlet.

Which airport is closest to Ormond Beach?

Daytona Beach (DAB) is about 20 minutes south. Sanford (SFB) is around 50 minutes and Orlando (MCO) about an hour and 15. Jacksonville (JAX) is roughly an hour and a half north.
