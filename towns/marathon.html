About Marathon

Marathon sits in the Middle Keys, a run of islands along the Overseas Highway that includes Vaca Key, Boot Key, Fat Deer Key and Grassy Key. The Seven Mile Bridge starts at its south end, Sombrero Beach is the town beach, and Fishermen's Community Hospital is in town. Life centers on the water, from the reef to the flats to the marinas.

The trade offs: insurance in Monroe County is the highest in the state, a hurricane means a long drive up one road, and the nearest airline service is in Key West, about an hour away.

Common questions

Is Marathon walkable?

Not really. Marathon runs along US 1 across several keys, so most errands mean a drive or a bike ride.

Does Marathon have a good beach?

Yes, one good town beach. Sombrero Beach is the town beach, and Bahia Honda State Park is about 20 minutes south.

Is Marathon affordable?

No, it is expensive. Home prices: About $715K median sale, last 12 months (Momentum Realty, 2026). Below Islamorada and Key West prices, but still well above most of Florida, and Keys insurance costs far more than on the mainland.

What is the nightlife like in Marathon?

Low key. Waterfront bars with live music, like Sparky's Landing and The Hurricane, and most nights end early. Key West is about an hour south for a bigger night.

How busy does Marathon get?

Busiest in winter and on holiday weekends. Calm most of the year, with more traffic on US 1 in winter, on holiday weekends and during the July lobster mini season.

Is Marathon good for boating and fishing?

Yes, it is one of the best boating towns in the state. Ocean on one side and Florida Bay on the other, with Boot Key Harbor in town and Sombrero Reef offshore.

Which airport is closest to Marathon?

Key West (EYW) is about an hour south. Miami and Fort Lauderdale are two to three hours up US 1. Marathon's own airport is general aviation only.

What should I know before moving to Marathon?

The main things to plan for: A long evacuation drive, high insurance. Statewide about $10,384 a year for $250K (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address.
