About Port St. Joe

Port St. Joe sits on St. Joseph Bay, with a short main street, a marina and quiet neighborhoods under the pines. People here fish, paddle the grass flats, and drive out to the beaches of Cape San Blas. It feels like a working town more than a resort, and the bay sunsets are part of the routine.

The trade offs are real. Hurricane Michael hit hard here in 2018, so ask about roofs, elevation and insurance on any house. The state closed the 2026 scallop season in St. Joseph Bay, which matters if scalloping is part of why you came. The closest airport is about an hour away, and shopping and big medical care mean a drive.

Where it shines

BeachesTop 6%

The town faces the bay, so the Gulf beach is a drive. Cape San Blas and St. Joseph Peninsula State Park have 10 miles of white sand and tall dunes with very little built behind them, about half an hour away.

Peace and quietTop 15%

Quiet nearly all year. Downtown is a block off US 98 and never feels rushed. Summer brings more boats and more cars headed for Cape San Blas, but nothing like the bigger beach towns.

Active outdoorsTop 20%

Hard to beat for wild coast. St. Joseph Peninsula State Park has three nature trails, a wilderness preserve, camping and a boat ramp at Eagle Harbor. The St. Joseph Bay State Buffer Preserve runs guided tram tours.

Coastal charmTop 23%

Some charm: a few older blocks, local spots and low buildings.

Where it falls short

NightlifeBottom 15%

Very quiet after dark. You can find a songwriter with a guitar and a local craft beer some nights, and the Blast on the Bay songwriters festival comes each October, but most evenings end with the sunset.

Airport accessBottom 9%

Northwest Florida Beaches (ECP) is about an hour northwest and Tallahassee (TLH) is closer to two hours. Both are small airports. This is a town you drive to.

Common questions

Is Port St. Joe walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Downtown sits one block off US 98 and is easy to cover on foot, with shops, restaurants, the marina and the bayfront at George Core Park close together. Beaches and bigger errands take a car.

Does Port St. Joe have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. The town faces the bay, so the Gulf beach is a drive. Cape San Blas and St. Joseph Peninsula State Park have 10 miles of white sand and tall dunes with very little built behind them, about half an hour away.

Is Port St. Joe affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $460K median sale (Momentum, Nov 2026). Good value for a Gulf town. Houses in town cost far less than on Cape San Blas or along 30A. Much of the housing was repaired or rebuilt after Michael in 2018, so ask about roof age and insurance.

What is the nightlife like in Port St. Joe?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Dinner downtown or with a bay view, a walk along the water at George Core Park for the sunset, and maybe a songwriter playing at one of the bars. In October there are fall concerts up the road at Beacon Hill. By 9 the town is quiet.

How busy does Port St. Joe get?

It is one of the quietest places on the Florida coast. Quiet nearly all year. Downtown is a block off US 98 and never feels rushed. Summer brings more boats and more cars headed for Cape San Blas, but nothing like the bigger beach towns.

Is Port St. Joe good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. St. Joseph Bay is shallow, clear and calm, good for flats fishing, paddling and snorkeling. Point South Marina is the boating hub downtown and there are public ramps around town. Scallop season was closed for 2026 after a low count.

Which airport is closest to Port St. Joe?

Northwest Florida Beaches (ECP) is about an hour northwest and Tallahassee (TLH) is closer to two hours. Both are small airports. This is a town you drive to.
