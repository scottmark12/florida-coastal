About Treasure Island

Treasure Island has one of the widest beaches on the Gulf, and a paved beach trail runs along part of it. Behind the beach, small islands of canal homes like Isle of Capri and Isle of Palms have docks on Boca Ciega Bay. Sunset Beach at the south end feels like its own little neighborhood. It's a mix of retirees, second home owners and families who want a beach town close to St. Pete.

Helene's surge flooded much of the island in 2024, and many homes are being repaired, raised or rebuilt. Flood insurance matters a lot. Prices and property taxes are high, and winter and spring bring visitor traffic.

Where it shines

BeachesTop 8%

One of the widest beaches on the Gulf coast, a long walk from the dune line to the water. Gulf Front Park is the center of things, and Sunset Beach at the south tip is narrower and quieter.

On the waterTop 8%

Passes at both ends, John's Pass to the north and Blind Pass to the south, mean quick runs to the Gulf. Canal front homes on Isle of Capri and Isle of Palms sit on Boca Ciega Bay, and charters leave from John's Pass.

NightlifeTop 24%

Beach bars carry the nights. Caddy's on Sunset Beach reopened in April 2026 with a rebuilt second floor patio, Sloppy Joe's has live music at sunset, and the Sunday drum circle plays until the sun goes down.

WalkabilityTop 26%

The Treasure Island Beach Trail is a paved mile beside the sand, and the middle of the island has shops and restaurants close together. The island is long and thin, so the ends need a bike or a car.

Where it falls short

Active outdoorsBottom 24%

The beach trail and the sand are most of it. Treasure Bay has golf and tennis, and the wide beach is good for flying kites. Bigger parks and trails are a drive away at Fort De Soto or on the mainland.

AffordabilityBottom 24%

On the expensive side for Florida. Mid century condos are the way in and waterfront houses run high. After the 2024 storms, elevation, flood insurance and rebuilding rules matter on every listing.
