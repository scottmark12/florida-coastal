About Juno Beach

Juno Beach is a tiny town, a little over a square mile, stretched along the ocean between Jupiter and Palm Beach Gardens. Mornings belong to the pier anglers and beach walkers, and summer nights belong to the sea turtles nesting on the sand. Most people live in condos along A1A or in small neighborhoods just inland, and a lot of them are retirees and seasonal residents.

The trade offs: oceanfront buildings face hurricanes and beach erosion, and condo insurance and special assessments can be big. Lighting rules are strict during turtle season, March through October. There isn't much shopping in town, so you'll drive to Jupiter or Palm Beach Gardens. The airport is about 25 minutes away.

A week here

Most morningsAn early walk out the Juno Beach Pier with the anglers who are there every morning.
Late afternoonA swim at Loggerhead Park, then a walk along the dunes as the beach empties.
SaturdayA bike ride up the path along A1A to Carlin Park and back, passing the same riders every week.
Saturday nightCaptain Charlie's Reef Grill on US 1. A plain little fish house where you pick the catch and how it's cooked, with a wine list bigger than the room. In season, the locals get there by five.
SundayIn summer, look for fresh turtle tracks on the sand at sunrise. The rest of the year, a long, slow beach day by the pier.

Where it shines

Active outdoorsTop 15%

Juno Dunes Natural Area has scrub trails across the road from the ocean, and Jupiter's river parks and John D. MacArthur Beach State Park are each about 10 minutes away.

BeachesTop 18%

A wide, natural beach backed by dunes, with Juno Beach Park and lifeguards by the pier. It is one of the busiest sea turtle nesting beaches in the country, so it stays wild.

Airport accessTop 18%

West Palm Beach (DJT) is about 25 minutes south on Interstate 95 or the turnpike, with nonstops to most big East Coast cities.

Common questions

Is Juno Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Good for walking and biking along the ocean, less so for errands. A path runs along the beach road, but groceries and most dining are a short drive away.

Does Juno Beach have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. A wide, natural beach backed by dunes, with Juno Beach Park and lifeguards by the pier. It is one of the busiest sea turtle nesting beaches in the country, so it stays wild.

Is Juno Beach affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $800K median sale over the past year (Horizon Palm Realty, Nov 2026). Expensive. Typical values are in the low $800Ks, most of the stock is oceanfront and Intracoastal condos, and wind insurance and building assessments add up.

What is the nightlife like in Juno Beach?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Evenings are slow and outdoorsy. You catch the sunset from the pier, grab dinner a few minutes down US 1, and in summer you might join a guided turtle walk after dark.
