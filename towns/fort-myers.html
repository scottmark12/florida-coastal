About Fort Myers

Fort Myers sits on the wide Caloosahatchee River. The River District downtown has brick streets, restaurants and condos by the water, and McGregor Boulevard is lined with royal palms and older homes. People walk the riverfront, go to spring training in March and drive out to the beaches on weekends. It's a real working city, with retirees, families and young professionals side by side.

Ian flooded parts of the city along the river in 2022, so check flood zones near the water. Winter traffic is heavy, and the Gulf beaches are a drive, not a walk. Prices are lower than in the beach towns, but insurance still runs high in Lee County, and some neighborhoods change block by block.

Your week here

  1. Most morningsA walk or bike ride along the Caloosahatchee on the downtown riverfront.
  2. Late afternoonThe boardwalk at Six Mile Cypress Slough Preserve, where the regulars know which bend has the gators.
  3. SaturdayBike the John Yarbrough Linear Park trail, then meet friends for lunch in the River District.
  4. Saturday nightThe Prawnbroker on McGregor. Local grouper, smoked fish dip and a fish market up front, and it's been feeding locals since 1982.
  5. SundayPaddle the Orange River near Manatee Park, where the manatees gather on cool winter days.

Common questions about Fort Myers

Is Fort Myers walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. The River District is a compact grid of brick streets. From First Street you can walk to restaurants, Florida Rep, the art center and the riverfront. Outside downtown the city is built for cars.

Does Fort Myers have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No beach in town. Downtown sits on the river, well inland. Fort Myers Beach and the Sanibel causeway are each about 30 to 40 minutes away, and longer in winter traffic.

Is Fort Myers affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $310K typical home value (Zillow, May 2026). More affordable than most of the Southwest Florida coast, with a wide range from downtown condos to older houses off McGregor. Homes near the river carry flood insurance, so check the zone.

What is the nightlife like in Fort Myers?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Dinner on First Street, then a play at Florida Rep or a show at the Caloosa Sound Amphitheater. After that, a drink on Sidney's Rooftop or at the bars around First and Hendry. Third Fridays, Music Walk puts bands on the street.

How busy does Fort Myers get?

It gets busy, especially in season. This is the busy hub of the region. US 41, Colonial Boulevard and the river bridges are heavy most of the day and worse in winter, and downtown is loud on event nights. Calm is a few blocks back.

Is Fort Myers good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The Caloosahatchee is wide here and part of the Okeechobee Waterway, so you can run west to the Gulf or east across the state. Marinas line the river. The Gulf is a long ride down, not out the back door.

Which airport is closest to Fort Myers?

Hard to beat. Southwest Florida (RSW) is about 20 to 25 minutes from downtown, and Punta Gorda (PGD) is about 35 minutes north on I-75 as a backup.
