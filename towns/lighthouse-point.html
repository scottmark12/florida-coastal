The scorecard

Tap any score for the local's take.

Nightlife1

Very little. Nauti Dawg at the marina closes at 9, Cap's Place is a dinner spot, and the streets are dark soon after. Nights out happen in Pompano Beach, Deerfield Beach or Fort Lauderdale.

Meet people3

Decent for a small city. The Doreen Gauthier library runs four book clubs and a Friends group, the Lighthouse Point Yacht Club has a cruise group and clay courts for members, and the lighthouse society runs boat tours.

Peace and quiet4

Most streets dead end at a canal, so there is almost no through traffic. The noise is Federal Highway along the west edge and boats idling out toward the inlet on weekend mornings.

Walkability2

Good for a stroll or a bike ride on quiet streets, but shops and restaurants sit out along Federal Highway and most trips take a car. The beach is a drive over the bridge.

On the water5

Nearly the whole city is built on canals dug in the 1950s and 60s, with docks behind the houses and a short run to Hillsboro Inlet. Lighthouse Point Marina has a dock and dine cafe, and the Bahamas are a day's run in good weather.

Beaches1

No beach in the city. It sits on the mainland side of the Intracoastal. The nearest sand is Pompano Beach or Deerfield Beach, about 10 to 15 minutes by car.

Dining and arts2

A modest list with one landmark. Cap's Place, Broward County's oldest restaurant, is reached only by its own boat launch. Nauti Dawg serves on the marina dock, and a few neighborhood spots sit near Federal Highway.

Outdoors and wellness2

Small city parks and ball fields, no trails. Most outdoor time here happens on a boat. For hiking or long bike rides you head to the county parks inland or the beachfront in Deerfield Beach and Pompano Beach.

Affordability1

Among the priciest stretches of the Broward coast. Most homes sit on deep water canals and are priced like it, with flood insurance and seawall upkeep on top. A few condos near Federal Highway cost less.

Airport access4

FLL is about 25 to 30 minutes south on I-95. The Palm Beach airport is about 40 minutes north, and Miami (MIA) is close to an hour.

Common questions about Lighthouse Point

Is Lighthouse Point walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Good for a stroll or a bike ride on quiet streets, but shops and restaurants sit out along Federal Highway and most trips take a car. The beach is a drive over the bridge.

Does Lighthouse Point have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No beach in the city. It sits on the mainland side of the Intracoastal. The nearest sand is Pompano Beach or Deerfield Beach, about 10 to 15 minutes by car.

Is Lighthouse Point affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $825K median sale price, all home types (fall 2026). Among the priciest stretches of the Broward coast. How busy does Lighthouse Point get?

It is quiet most of the year. Most streets dead end at a canal, so there is almost no through traffic. The noise is Federal Highway along the west edge and boats idling out toward the inlet on weekend mornings.
