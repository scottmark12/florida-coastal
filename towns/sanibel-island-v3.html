About Sanibel Island

Sanibel is an island where nature comes first. Much of it is protected wildlife refuge, the beaches are famous for shells, and miles of bike paths connect everything. There are no high rises, and life moves at the speed of a bicycle.

Hurricane Ian hit hard in 2022, and many homes have since been rebuilt higher and newer. Insurance and flood costs are high, there's one causeway on and off, and the airport is 45 minutes to an hour away.

Where it shines

Active outdoorsTop 9%

Among the best on the coast. Ding Darling has Wildlife Drive (closed Fridays), the Indigo Trail and the Bailey Tract, and Tarpon Bay for paddling. Add 25 miles of bike paths and a Sunday farmers market in season.

BeachesTop 14%

Miles of Gulf beach known around the country for shells. Lighthouse Beach on the east end was rebuilt with new sand after Ian, and Bowman's Beach is the long, undeveloped one to the west. Parking is paid.

Coastal charmTop 17%

A charming town with a real old center, local shops and pretty streets.

WalkabilityTop 21%

Better by bike than on foot. About 25 miles of shared use paths link the shops along Periwinkle Way to the beaches and the refuge, and it is all flat. Things are spread out, so walking alone will not cover it.

Where it falls short

Meet peopleBottom 32%

A decent list for a small island. The Sunday farmers market at City Hall, classes and films at BIG ARTS, group rides with the Sanibel Bicycle Club, and volunteer shifts with SCCF or the Ding Darling refuge.

Dining and artsBottom 29%

Mostly casual island spots, with Doc Ford's and the rebuilt Lighthouse Cafe back open. BIG ARTS brings concerts, films and gallery shows, and the Shell Show and Festival ran for the 89th time in March 2026.

NightlifeBottom 22%

Very little after dark. A few restaurant bars and a beachfront tiki bar at Sundial have music at sunset, kitchens close early, and the island keeps its lights low at night on purpose.

AffordabilityBottom 8%

Among the most expensive towns on the Southwest Florida coast, even after Ian. Flood and wind insurance are a big part of the monthly cost, and many rebuilt homes sit higher and sell for more.

Common questions

Is Sanibel Island walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Better by bike than on foot. About 25 miles of shared use paths link the shops along Periwinkle Way to the beaches and the refuge, and it is all flat. Things are spread out, so walking alone will not cover it.

Does Sanibel Island have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. Miles of Gulf beach known around the country for shells. Lighthouse Beach on the east end was rebuilt with new sand after Ian, and Bowman's Beach is the long, undeveloped one to the west. Parking is paid.

Is Sanibel Island affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $910K median sale (Momentum, Sep 2026). Among the most expensive towns on the Southwest Florida coast, even after Ian. Flood and wind insurance are a big part of the monthly cost, and many rebuilt homes sit higher and sell for more.

What is the nightlife like in Sanibel Island?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Nights here are dinner and a sunset. You might catch a guitarist at a restaurant bar or a concert or film at BIG ARTS, and then it is home by 9 or 10. The beach after dark is for stars, not bars.

How busy does Sanibel Island get?

It is quiet most of the year. No traffic lights, low buildings and dark, quiet nights. The tradeoff is Periwinkle Way on winter afternoons, when cars heading for the causeway back up for a long way.

Is Sanibel Island good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Bay on one side, Gulf on the other. Sanibel Marina sits on the east end, Tarpon Bay Explorers rents kayaks inside the refuge, and Pine Island Sound is minutes away. Fewer slips and ramps than a mainland boating town.

Which airport is closest to Sanibel Island?

Southwest Florida (RSW) is about 45 minutes away over the rebuilt causeway. In winter, allow extra time to get off the island in the afternoon.

What should I know before moving to Sanibel Island?

The main things to plan for: Hit hard by Hurricane Ian in 2022 and mostly rebuilt. Check flood zones. The causeway is the only road.
