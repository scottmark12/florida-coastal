About Apalachicola

Apalachicola is a working river town on the Forgotten Coast, with brick warehouses, old cottages under live oaks and shrimp boats tied up at the docks. It's small enough to walk end to end. People come for the seafood, the quiet and the feeling that Florida forgot to change this place.

The trade off is distance. The closest big airport is Tallahassee, about an hour and a half away, the hospital is small, and the beach is across the bridge on St. George Island. If you want slow days, a porch and a town where people know your name, it's hard to beat.

Common questions

Is Apalachicola walkable?

Yes, for the most part. More than 900 historic buildings sit on a walkable grid by the river, and there's a full grocery store in town. For big box shopping you'll drive.

Does Apalachicola have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. There's no beach in town, but St. George Island is 11 miles away over two bridges. Dr. Beach named its state park the number one beach in America in 2023.

Is Apalachicola affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $300K to $385K in town (Zillow and Redfin, Aug 2026). Still one of the better values on the coast for a town with this much character. With so few sales, prices can jump around from month to month.

What is the nightlife like in Apalachicola?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. A night out here is dinner on the water, a beer at the Oyster City Brewing taproom and maybe live music at High Five. It's an early town, and most places are closed by 10.

How busy does Apalachicola get?

It is one of the quietest places on the Florida coast. Most days it's you, the river and a few slow blocks of brick storefronts. The exception is festival weekends, when the Florida Seafood Festival brings tens of thousands of people to Battery Park in November.

Is Apalachicola good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. A true working waterfront. Launch at Battery Park or Scipio Creek and you're on the river or the bay in minutes. Wild oyster harvesting reopened in 2026 after five years closed.

Which airport is closest to Apalachicola?

Plan on about 90 minutes to Tallahassee (TLH) or 80 to Panama City Beach (ECP). There is a small hospital in town with a 24 hour ER.

What should I know before moving to Apalachicola?

The main things to plan for: Storm surge and flooding in low areas. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
