About Redington Beach

Redington Beach is almost all homes. There are no big resorts, just single family houses on short streets between the Gulf and Boca Ciega Bay. People walk dogs, ride bikes, and wave at the same neighbors every morning. Many owners are retirees or families who have been here a long time, and it stays steady most of the year.

Helene and Milton flooded many homes here in 2024. Some are still empty, being rebuilt, or waiting on permits, so the street can look patchy. Storm surge is a real risk and flood insurance is a big monthly cost. Ask about elevation, flood history and what work has been permitted before you fall for a house.

Where it falls short

Meet peopleBottom 29%

Neighbors meet through the small town calendar, like the July 4 Liberty and Libations morning from the Parks and Recreation Board and an Easter egg hunt. Most clubs and leagues are over the bridge in Seminole.

Dining and artsBottom 26%

Little to none in town. You drive a few minutes to North Redington Beach, Madeira Beach or Seminole to eat out.

AffordabilityBottom 21%

Coastal charmBottom 17%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

NightlifeBottom 11%

Almost none in town. It is a residential place, so evenings are porches and sunsets, and anything lively is a short drive to Madeira Beach or North Redington Beach.

Active outdoorsBottom 5%

Beach walks, biking quiet streets and paddling the bay are the outdoor life. Bigger parks like Fort De Soto and Sand Key are a drive away.

Common questions

Is Redington Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. You can walk or bike to the beach from almost anywhere in town, but there is not much else to walk to. Groceries and dining mean driving.

Does Redington Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. A soft, wide Gulf beach shared with the surrounding towns, mostly used by residents and quiet even in season.

Is Redington Beach affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $815K median sale over the last year (Horizon Palm Realty, Nov 2026). Expensive. Single family homes near the Gulf trade in the high hundreds of thousands and up, and older homes may need elevation or rebuilding after 2024. Insurance is a big line item.

What is the nightlife like in Redington Beach?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Evenings here are quiet: a walk to the beach for sunset, then dinner at home or a short drive to a restaurant in the next town. Neighbors chat across driveways and docks.

How busy does Redington Beach get?

It is one of the quietest places on the Florida coast. About as quiet as the beach gets. There is very little commercial activity, so beyond construction from storm rebuilding the streets are calm year round.

Is Redington Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. Bay side homes have water access to the Intracoastal, and the Gulf is right there for swimming and surf fishing. Boating is easy, but marinas are in neighboring towns.

Which airport is closest to Redington Beach?

St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) is about 25 minutes away and Tampa International (TPA) about 40 minutes.

What should I know before moving to Redington Beach?

The main things to plan for: Many homes flooded by Helene and Milton in 2024, some still unpermitted or vacant; storm surge risk, high flood insurance. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
