About Longboat Key

Longboat Key is a long, narrow barrier island between Anna Maria Island and Sarasota. Most homes are condos along the Gulf, with single family homes on the bay side and around the golf courses. Life is quiet: beach walks, tennis, golf, boating, and dinner over the bridge in Sarasota, where St. Armands Circle and the arts scene are a short drive away. Many residents are retirees and seasonal owners.

The key is low and exposed, so storm surge and flood insurance matter. There's one main road, Gulf of Mexico Drive, and it gets slow in winter. There's not much nightlife and only a few everyday shops on the island itself. Condos make up most sales, so check association fees and building reserves.

A week here

Most morningsWalk the beach up to Beer Can Island at the north tip, where the pass meets the Gulf.
Late afternoonBike the path along Gulf of Mexico Drive, which runs most of the length of the key.
SaturdayTennis at the town's public courts with your regular group, then lunch over the bridge on St. Armands Circle.
Saturday nightMar Vista Dockside in the Village. Grouper sandwiches and fried shrimp at tables under the buttonwood trees on Sarasota Bay, with boats tying up at the dock.
SundayChairs in the sand at a beach access off Gulf of Mexico Drive for sunset, with the same neighbors who come every week.

Where it falls short

Meet peopleBottom 35%

It takes a little effort. The chamber puts on the Savor the Sounds concerts at the Karon Family Pavilion and the yearly Taste of the Keys, the town has a public tennis center, and Let's Talk Longboat sessions run monthly.

WalkabilityBottom 24%

The key is about ten miles long with one main road, Gulf of Mexico Drive, so most errands are a drive. A path runs alongside it for bikes, and from many spots you can walk to a beach access or a park but not much else.

NightlifeBottom 23%

Early nights. There is no bar strip on the key, just a few restaurant lounges along Gulf of Mexico Drive, and the beaches close at 11. For a show or a late drink you drive to St. Armands Circle or downtown Sarasota.

Dining and artsBottom 15%

A short list of good dinner spots, like The Lazy Lobster, plus the restaurants at the St. Regis resort. There is not much variety on the key itself, but St. Armands Circle and Sarasota's theaters are a short drive south.

Coastal charmBottom 12%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

AffordabilityBottom 9%

One of the most expensive places on this coast. Gulf front and bay front homes run far above the Florida median, older condos are the lower rung, and wind and flood insurance on a barrier island adds a lot.

Common questions

Is Longboat Key walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. The key is about ten miles long with one main road, Gulf of Mexico Drive, so most errands are a drive. A path runs alongside it for bikes, and from many spots you can walk to a beach access or a park but not much else.

Does Longboat Key have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. Miles of soft white sand with very few people on it. The town keeps 12 public beach accesses, most with small lots of 15 to 40 spaces, and there are no lifeguards. In winter, get to the lot early.
