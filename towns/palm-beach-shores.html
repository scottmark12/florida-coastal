About Palm Beach Shores

Palm Beach Shores is a tiny town wrapped by the Atlantic, the Palm Beach Inlet and the Intracoastal. It still has a 1950s beach town feel, with low buildings, cottages and boats everywhere. People walk or bike to the beach, fish the inlet and run over to Peanut Island on weekends.

Living on a barrier island means real storm and surge risk, so plan on flood insurance. Prices keep climbing as older buildings give way to luxury condos. The inlet stays busy with boat traffic, and for most shopping you'll head over the bridge.

Common questions

Is Palm Beach Shores walkable?

Yes, for the most part. Very walkable for daily life by the water. The whole town is eleven blocks, so the beach, the inlet and the marina are a few minutes on foot, though groceries are just north on Singer Island or over the bridge.

Does Palm Beach Shores have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. A park runs the length of the town along the ocean and inlet, and the water near the inlet can be very clear on an incoming tide. Singer Island's public beaches continue north.

Is Palm Beach Shores affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $1.18M median sale (Horizon Palm Realty, Nov 2026). Pricey and getting more so. Typical values were near $880K in late 2026, up about 8 percent in a year, while new oceanfront condos list in the millions.

What is the nightlife like in Palm Beach Shores?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Evenings start at the marina, watching the charter boats come in with the sun going down. Then it is dinner on the docks or a drink at a resort bar, and an early night.

How busy does Palm Beach Shores get?

It is quiet most of the year. Very calm on the streets, which are short and mostly residential. Weekends bring boats through the inlet and visitors to the beach and marinas.

Is Palm Beach Shores good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. Some of the best water access in the county. Sailfish Marina sits right on the inlet, charter boats run out daily, and Peanut Island and the Blue Heron Bridge snorkel spot are minutes away.

Which airport is closest to Palm Beach Shores?

West Palm Beach (DJT) is about 20 minutes away over the Blue Heron Bridge and Interstate 95.

What should I know before moving to Palm Beach Shores?

The main things to plan for: Barrier island storm and surge exposure, rising prices as older buildings give way to luxury condos, and inlet boat traffic.
