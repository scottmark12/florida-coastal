About Navarre

Navarre is where most people who love Navarre Beach actually live. It's a long line of neighborhoods along Highway 98 and Santa Rosa Sound, with parks on the water, plenty of young families and a lot of military households from Hurlburt Field and Eglin. Weekends are about the sound, the beach across the causeway and kids' ball games.

The big complaint is traffic. Highway 98 is the main road, and at rush hour and in summer it backs up with few ways around it. Homes near the sound face hurricane and surge risk, so check flood zones closely. Both airports are about 45 minutes away, and the town is still filling in, so expect construction.

NightlifeTop 36%

Pretty low key. Most evenings out are a dinner on the water or a sports bar along Highway 98, and the island bars across the causeway pick up in summer. For a real night out people drive to Pensacola or Fort Walton Beach.

AffordabilityTop 44%

Middle of the road for the Emerald Coast. Zillow put the typical home around $417K in August 2026, well under the island, though insurance on the water and newer builds can push costs up.

Dining and artsTop 47%

Mostly casual. Seafood shacks, barbecue on the water, burger spots, chains and a brewery line Highway 98. It covers the basics, and for more variety people head to Gulf Breeze, Pensacola or Destin.

Meet peopleBottom 38%

Easier than you would think for a place without a downtown. Youth and adult sports, a seasonal Saturday market, Navarre Park events and the beach cleanups and turtle volunteering on the island give people plenty of ways in. A lot of social life runs through neighborhood groups.

Common questions

Is Navarre walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. Not a walking town. Navarre grew up along Highway 98, so groceries, schools and restaurants are spread out and you drive to most of them. Some sound side streets and Navarre Park are nice for a stroll or bike ride.

Does Navarre have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. The town itself sits on the sound, but Navarre Beach is a short causeway drive away, with sugar white sand, uncrowded stretches and a long Gulf pier. Locals treat it as their backyard beach.

Is Navarre affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $415K median sale (Resideline, Nov 2026). Middle of the road for the Emerald Coast. Zillow put the typical home around $417K in August 2026, well under the island, though insurance on the water and newer builds can push costs up.

What is the nightlife like in Navarre?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Sunset on the sound from a waterfront deck, then home early, or over the causeway for live music at an island spot in summer. Evenings are mostly casual and centered on the water.

How busy does Navarre get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. The neighborhoods off the sound are calm, but Highway 98 is the one main road and it backs up at rush hour and on summer weekends. Regional leaders have been meeting about it since 2023, and widening and a parallel access road are planned but years out.

Is Navarre good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Santa Rosa Sound is right there, calm and shallow, great for kayaks, paddleboards and small boats. Public ramps on the sound and up on East Bay make it easy to get out, and the Gulf pier on Navarre Beach is minutes away.

Which airport is closest to Navarre?

You are between two airports, so you pick by price. Pensacola (PNS) is about 45 minutes and Destin Fort Walton Beach (VPS) is roughly 45 minutes to an hour, depending on Highway 98 traffic.

What should I know before moving to Navarre?

The main things to plan for: Highway 98 congestion at rush hour, hurricane and surge exposure along the sound, few road alternatives.
