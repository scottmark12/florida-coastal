About West Palm Beach

West Palm Beach is the working downtown of Palm Beach County. Clematis Street, the waterfront and the Norton Museum of Art are a short walk or bike ride from historic neighborhoods like El Cid and Northwood, with Brightline trains to Miami and Orlando. You'll find young professionals, families, retirees and a growing number of people who moved from the Northeast.

The trade offs: prices have climbed fast, traffic on I 95 and Okeechobee Boulevard is heavy, and neighborhoods change quickly from block to block. Older homes may need roof, electrical and plumbing work before you can insure them. Downtown condos can carry high fees.

Where it shines

NightlifeTop 4%

Clematis Street runs late. E.R. Bradley's has live music on the waterfront every night, Respectable Street has been booking bands since 1987, and the latest rooms stay open until 3 or 4am on weekends.

Meet peopleTop 6%

Plenty of standing dates. Celis Run Club leaves the Meyer Amphitheater on Wednesdays at 6pm, Clematis by Night is a free concert downtown every Thursday, and the GreenMarket fills Saturday mornings from October through May.

WalkabilityTop 7%

From Clematis Street you can walk to the waterfront, the restaurants along Rosemary Avenue and the train station. Flagler Drive adds miles of flat path beside the lagoon for a bike or a run. Errands beyond downtown still need a car.

Dining and artsTop 7%

Restaurants and rooftops fill Clematis Street and Rosemary Avenue, the Norton Museum of Art sits just south of downtown, and Clematis by Night took a national title for best outdoor concert series in 2026. One loss: SunFest has not been held since 2024.

Airport accessTop 10%

The Palm Beach airport is about 10 minutes from downtown, which is as easy as it gets in Florida. Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about an hour south, and Tri-Rail and Amtrak stop at the station just west of downtown.

Common questions

Is West Palm Beach walkable?

Yes, for the most part. From Clematis Street you can walk to the waterfront, the restaurants along Rosemary Avenue and the train station. Flagler Drive adds miles of flat path beside the lagoon for a bike or a run. Errands beyond downtown still need a car.

Does West Palm Beach have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. There is no ocean beach in the city. The lagoon is for boating and looking at, not swimming. The nearest sand is across the bridges in Palm Beach or up on Singer Island, about 10 to 20 minutes by car.

Is West Palm Beach affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $404K median sale over the past year, $385K in October (Horizon Palm Realty, Nov 2026). Prices climbed fast over the past several years and sit well above the Florida median, with new condo towers downtown at the top. Older condos and neighborhoods farther west cost less. Budget for wind and flood insurance near the water.

What is the nightlife like in West Palm Beach?

Really good for a beach town. Start with dinner on Clematis Street or a rooftop like Spruzzo at The Ben, then walk to E.R. Bradley's for a band by the water. If you want to keep going, Respectable Street runs until 4am Wednesday through Saturday. On Thursdays it all starts early with the free Clematis by Night concert.

How busy does West Palm Beach get?

It gets busy, especially in season. This is the county's downtown, so expect traffic on Okeechobee Boulevard, event crowds on the waterfront and noise near Clematis on weekend nights. The older streets north and south along Flagler Drive are calmer.

Is West Palm Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The Lake Worth Lagoon is the front yard, with city docks at the foot of Clematis and marinas along Flagler Drive. One catch for trailer boats: the Currie Park ramps are closed for a rebuild until spring 2027, so you launch a few miles north.

Which airport is closest to West Palm Beach?

The Palm Beach airport is about 10 minutes from downtown, which is as easy as it gets in Florida. Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about an hour south, and Tri-Rail and Amtrak stop at the station just west of downtown.
