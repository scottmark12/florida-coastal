Shalimar

A tiny, leafy town on Choctawhatchee Bay and its bayous, just north of Fort Walton Beach, with shady streets, docks and a waterfront golf course.

A day in Shalimar

You take the boat out of the bayou in the morning, play a round by the bay in the afternoon and are home in time to watch the sunset off the dock.

The scorecard

Tap any score for the local's take.

Nightlife1

Barely any. Shalimar is mostly homes, and evenings are dinner on the dock or a quick drive to Fort Walton Beach, about ten minutes south, for bars and live music.

Meet people2

Small town social. The town is under a thousand people, so you get to know neighbors on the water, at the golf club and at the volunteer run library. Bigger clubs and events are in Fort Walton Beach and Niceville.

Peace and quiet4

Calm on the side streets and bayfront, which is the appeal. Eglin Parkway carries steady traffic past the edge of town, and you will hear jets from Eglin now and then.

Walkability2

Pleasant for a walk under the oaks, but there is not much to walk to. Shopping and restaurants are along Eglin Parkway or down in Fort Walton Beach.

On the water4

This is a boating town. Choctawhatchee Bay, Garnier Bayou and Poquito Bayou wrap around it, many homes have docks, and the county runs free ramps at Meigs Park and Poquito Bayou.

Beaches2

No Gulf beach here, just bay shoreline. The white sand on Okaloosa Island is about 15 minutes south over the bridge.

Dining and arts2

Limited in town. A handful of casual spots sit along Eglin Parkway, and Fort Walton Beach, Niceville and Destin cover everything else within 10 to 25 minutes.

Outdoors and wellness3

Golf by the bay at Shalimar Pointe, sailing and paddling on the bayous, and easy reach to Okaloosa Island and Eglin's big tracts of open land.

Affordability3

Mid range. Prices swing a lot because so few homes sell, from older bayou cottages to big waterfront homes. Redfin put the three month median sale near $522K in September 2026, on a small sample.

Airport access4

One of the closest towns to Destin Fort Walton Beach (VPS), usually 10 to 15 minutes. It is a smaller airport with nonstops to major hubs and seasonal routes, and Pensacola (PNS) is about an hour and 15 minutes.

Common questions about Shalimar

Is Shalimar walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Pleasant for a walk under the oaks, but there is not much to walk to. Shopping and restaurants are along Eglin Parkway or down in Fort Walton Beach.

Does Shalimar have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No Gulf beach here, just bay shoreline. The white sand on Okaloosa Island is about 15 minutes south over the bridge.
