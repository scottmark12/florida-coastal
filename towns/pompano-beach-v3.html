About Pompano Beach

Pompano Beach is a Broward County city with some of the widest sand in Greater Fort Lauderdale. The rebuilt pier and the Pompano Beach Fishing Village anchor the beachfront, and the Hillsboro Inlet is at the north end.

It costs less than much of the coast nearby, with a median sale around $395K, and Fort Lauderdale's airport is about 20 minutes away. Like the rest of Broward, insurance is high, so check the flood zone for any address.

Common questions

Is Pompano Beach walkable?

Only by the beach. The beach blocks around the pier and the Fishing Village are walkable. The rest of the city is spread out, so plan on a car.

Does Pompano Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Some of the widest sand in Greater Fort Lauderdale, with the rebuilt pier and the beachfront Fishing Village.

Is Pompano Beach affordable?

Yes, for a beach town in South Florida. Home prices: About $395K median sale, last 12 months (Momentum Realty, Oct 2026). Beach condos often sell for $335K to $650K, low for an ocean view in South Florida, but canal homes cost far more and Broward insurance is high.

What is the nightlife like in Pompano Beach?

Yes, around the pier. The beachfront around the pier and the Fishing Village has restaurants and bars.

How busy does Pompano Beach get?

Busy, especially in winter. A large city with busy main roads. The beach blocks are calmer, but A1A and Atlantic Boulevard stay busy, more so in winter.

Is Pompano Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Ocean beaches, the Intracoastal and canals, with Hillsboro Inlet at the north end for boats heading out to the Gulf Stream.

Which airport is closest to Pompano Beach?

Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about 20 minutes away.

What should I know before moving to Pompano Beach?

The main things to plan for: Flood zones vary, high insurance. Statewide about $10,384 a year for $250K (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address.
