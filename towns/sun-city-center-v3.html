About Sun City Center

Sun City Center is a retirement town inland from Tampa Bay, built around golf, clubs and community. People get around in golf carts, and there are more than 100 clubs, from woodworking and dance to cards and choir. Most neighborhoods are age restricted to 55 and over, and many residents came from the Midwest and the Northeast. Homes are mostly condos, villas and modest single family houses, and prices are some of the lowest in the Tampa Bay area.

The trade offs: there's no beach, and the Gulf is a drive away. The town is car and cart dependent, and summers are hot. Most homes are 55 plus, so it isn't for younger families. Prices dipped over the past year, which helps buyers but is worth knowing if you plan to sell soon. Tampa's airport is about 40 minutes away.

Where it shines

AffordabilityTop 14%

Prices run well below most of the Florida coast, with plenty of condos and villas at the low end. Budget for association fees. Being inland also means less storm surge exposure than the bayfront towns.

Where it falls short

NightlifeBottom 30%

Evenings wrap up early. The night out is a stage show at the Community Association theater or a dance put on by one of the clubs, and the streets are quiet well before 9.

Coastal charmBottom 21%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

BeachesBottom 15%

No beach. The closest sand is a small bay beach at E.G. Simmons Conservation Park in Ruskin, and the Gulf beaches of Anna Maria Island are about an hour south.

Dining and artsBottom 9%

Mostly casual and chain restaurants along State Road 674, plus stage shows at the community theater. The Firehouse Cultural Center in Ruskin adds plays, comedy and jazz about 10 minutes away. Bigger nights mean Tampa or Sarasota.

Active outdoorsBottom 2%

Recreation here is more courts and greens than trails. There is golf, lawn bowling, pickleball, tennis and indoor and outdoor pools. For woods and a river, Little Manatee River State Park is about 15 minutes south.

On the waterBottom 1%

Well inland, so the only water in town is golf course ponds and the community pools. The nearest boat ramp and bay access are about 15 minutes west at E.G. Simmons Conservation Park in Ruskin.

Common questions

Is Sun City Center walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. You get around by golf cart more than on foot. Carts are street legal in daylight and most shopping centers have cart parking, so errands are easy without a car, but the distances are too long to call it walkable.

Does Sun City Center have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No beach. The closest sand is a small bay beach at E.G. Simmons Conservation Park in Ruskin, and the Gulf beaches of Anna Maria Island are about an hour south.

Is Sun City Center affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $250K median sale (Momentum, Nov 2026). Prices run well below most of the Florida coast, with plenty of condos and villas at the low end. Budget for association fees. Being inland also means less storm surge exposure than the bayfront towns.

What is the nightlife like in Sun City Center?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. An early dinner along State Road 674, then a stage show at the Community Association theater or a club dance. It all wraps up early, and for anything later you drive to Tampa or Sarasota.

How busy does Sun City Center get?

It is quiet most of the year. Wide, slow streets where golf carts share the road, and no visitor crowds at any time of year. The busy spot is State Road 674, the main route to I-75, which carries more traffic as the area around it grows.

Is Sun City Center good for boating and fishing?

Boating is not a big part of life here. Well inland, so the only water in town is golf course ponds and the community pools. The nearest boat ramp and bay access are about 15 minutes west at E.G. Simmons Conservation Park in Ruskin.

Which airport is closest to Sun City Center?

Tampa International (TPA) is about 40 minutes north on I-75 and Sarasota Bradenton (SRQ) is about 40 minutes south. The interstate is a few minutes from the center of town.

What should I know before moving to Sun City Center?
