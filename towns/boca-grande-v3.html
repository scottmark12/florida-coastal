About Boca Grande

Boca Grande is the village on Gasparilla Island, reached by the Boca Grande Causeway, which has a toll. Golf carts are the main way around, the Boca Grande Lighthouse is on the island, and the area is known for tarpon fishing.

It's one of the most expensive places on the Florida coast, with a median sale around $2.4M. There's no hospital on the island, and the nearest airport with airline service is Punta Gorda, about an hour away.

Common questions

Is Boca Grande walkable?

Yes, the village is. A compact village where golf carts are the main way around.

Does Boca Grande have a good beach?

Yes. Gulf beaches line the island.

Is Boca Grande affordable?

No, it is one of the most expensive places in the state. Home prices: About $2.4M median sale (Broker One, June 2026, ZIP 33921). Among the most expensive places in Florida, with a median sale around $2.4M.

What is the nightlife like in Boca Grande?

Very little. Dinner and a drink, then home. The Pink Elephant and the Gasparilla Inn are the evening spots.

How busy does Boca Grande get?

Busy in season, very quiet in summer. Very quiet, with golf carts on the side streets and a toll causeway as the only road in. Winter and the late spring tarpon run bring more people.

Is Boca Grande good for boating and fishing?

Yes, it is famous for it. Known as the tarpon fishing capital of the world, with Boca Grande Pass at the south end and marinas on the bay side.

Which airport is closest to Boca Grande?

Punta Gorda (PGD) is about an hour. Fort Myers (RSW) and Sarasota (SRQ) are about an hour and 20 minutes.

What should I know before moving to Boca Grande?
