About Panama City

Panama City sits on St. Andrews Bay, with a downtown that's coming back, a working marina and older neighborhoods under big oaks. People here boat the bay, fish the bayous and head across the bridge to the beach when they want the Gulf. It's a regular town with real jobs, schools and Tyndall Air Force Base nearby, not a resort.

Hurricane Michael hit hard in 2018 and parts of town are still rebuilding, so look closely at roofs and repairs. Waterfront streets can flood. Summer traffic to the beach backs up, and some blocks are more polished than others, so it pays to see a street before you buy.

Common questions

Is Panama City walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Two walkable pockets, downtown along Harrison Avenue and Beck Avenue in St. Andrews, each good for a few blocks of shops and restaurants. They sit a few miles apart and everything else takes a car.

Does Panama City have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No Gulf beach in town. You get calm bayside beaches and small waterfront parks like Carl Gray Park, and the white sand is across the bridge in Panama City Beach, about 20 minutes from downtown.

Is Panama City affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $284K typical home value (Zillow via Stacker, Sep 2026). Noticeably cheaper than the beach towns on either side. Older bungalows near downtown and St. Andrews, plus newer houses inland, keep the price of entry low for the coast. Insurance still deserves a close look.

What is the nightlife like in Panama City?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Dinner at an oyster bar in St. Andrews or a table downtown near the marina, then a drink and a band somewhere along Harrison Avenue. In fall you can bring a chair to the Backstage Pass concerts on the Marina Lawn. It wraps up earlier than the beach.

How busy does Panama City get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Calmer than the beach across the bridge. Downtown and St. Andrews are easygoing most days and visitor crowds are light. US 98 and 23rd Street carry steady traffic, and the bridge to the beach slows down in summer.

Is Panama City good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. St. Andrews Bay is deep, protected and close to the pass out to the Gulf. There are marinas downtown, in St. Andrews and in Millville, public ramps around the bay, and a December Boat Parade of Lights now in its 40th year.

Which airport is closest to Panama City?

Northwest Florida Beaches (ECP) is about 30 minutes northwest of downtown, with easy parking and short lines. It is a small airport, so many trips include a connection.

What should I know before moving to Panama City?

The main things to plan for: Hurricane exposure. Parts of town are still rebuilding from Michael in 2018. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
