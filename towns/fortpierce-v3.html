About Fort Pierce

Fort Pierce is an old fishing town on the Indian River with a real working waterfront. Downtown has a marina, a historic theater and one of the best farmers markets on the coast every Saturday. Across the bridge, Hutchinson Island has quiet beaches, an inlet with a state park and snorkeling, and a laid back beach bar crowd. Prices are some of the most affordable on this coast.

The trade offs: some parts of town are rougher than others, so the neighborhood matters a lot here. The island takes the brunt of hurricanes, and the lagoon has had water quality problems. There's no big airport close by, so you'll drive to Palm Beach or Melbourne.

Where it shines

Active outdoorsTop 8%

The Saturday farmers market is the big outdoor ritual downtown. Beyond it you have the state park at the inlet, manatee watching in the cooler months by the Manatee Center, and lagoon islands and preserves to paddle.

AffordabilityTop 15%

Among the more affordable places on Florida's southeast coast. Houses and condos, including some close to the water, cost much less than in Martin or Palm Beach County. Older homes can mean higher insurance, so get quotes early.

Coastal charmTop 18%

Some charm: a few older blocks, local spots and low buildings.

On the waterTop 24%

A real working waterfront. The marina sits downtown on the Indian River Lagoon, and Fort Pierce Inlet is one of the easier, deeper inlets on this coast, so the ocean is minutes away. Fishing from the jetties and bridges is a daily habit here.

Where it falls short

Dining and artsBottom 30%

A short list, but with character. A handful of independent restaurants and waterfront spots downtown, the A.E. Backus Museum with its Florida landscapes and Highwaymen paintings, and shows at the restored Sunrise Theatre.

Common questions

Is Fort Pierce walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Downtown is a compact grid by the water. You can walk from the marina and the river walk to the Sunrise Theatre, the Backus Museum, the Manatee Center and a few blocks of shops and restaurants. The beach is across the bridge, a drive or a bike ride.

Does Fort Pierce have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. The beaches are over the bridge on Hutchinson Island, with public accesses on both sides of the inlet and a state park on the north side known for its surf. They are natural, low rise and rarely crowded, with fewer services than bigger beach towns.

Is Fort Pierce affordable?

What is the nightlife like in Fort Pierce?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A night out is dinner by the marina and maybe a show at the Sunrise Theatre, with a band at a waterfront bar on weekends. Friday Fest nights are the busiest downtown gets. Most other evenings are quiet by 9 or 10.

How busy does Fort Pierce get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Downtown is small and calm on weekdays, then busy on Saturday mornings for the market. U.S. 1 carries steady traffic, and the port and the rail line add some noise. The beach side on Hutchinson Island is quieter.

Is Fort Pierce good for boating and fishing?
