About 30A (Santa Rosa Beach)

30A isn't one town. It's a string of beach neighborhoods along Scenic Highway 30A in South Walton, each with its own feel, tied together by an 18.5 mile bike path. Some are old Florida cottages, some are planned towns you've seen in magazines, and in between are fifteen coastal dune lakes, a kind of lake found in only a handful of places in the world.

Life here runs on the beach and the bike. Mornings are coffee and a ride, afternoons are on the sand, and most nights are done by 10. It's quiet most of the year and packed in summer. The tradeoff is the price: this is one of the most expensive stretches of the Florida coast.

Where it shines

BeachesTop 2%

26 miles of sugar white sand with dozens of public accesses. Dr. Beach named Grayton Beach State Park the number one beach in America in 2020.

Active outdoorsTop 2%

State parks at both ends, 15,000 acres of state forest behind you and dune lakes in between. Topsail Hill alone has more than 10 miles of trails.

Coastal charmTop 8%

A charming town with a real old center, local shops and pretty streets.

WalkabilityTop 8%

Seaside, Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach are built for walking, and the 18.5 mile Timpoochee Trail links them by bike. Between towns you'll still want wheels.

Dining and artsTop 22%

Dinner on a roof deck, markets and events at the Seaside amphitheater, and Digital Graffiti lighting up the white walls of Alys Beach each May.

Meet peopleTop 24%

The calendar does the work. Saturday farmers markets in Seaside, daily pickleball open play at the South Walton Sports Complex, and more than 300 shows in one long weekend when the 30A Songwriters Festival comes each January.

Where it falls short

Airport accessBottom 28%

Two regional airports, each about 45 to 50 minutes away: Northwest Florida Beaches (ECP) and Destin Fort Walton (VPS).

On the waterBottom 15%

Fifteen rare coastal dune lakes for paddleboards and kayaks, and public ramps onto Choctawhatchee Bay for bigger boats.

AffordabilityBottom 9%

One of the priciest stretches of the Florida coast. Budget for the home, the insurance and often HOA fees on top.

Common questions

Is 30A walkable?

Yes, for the most part. Seaside, Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach are built for walking, and the 18.5 mile Timpoochee Trail links them by bike. Between towns you'll still want wheels.

Does 30A have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. 26 miles of sugar white sand with dozens of public accesses. Dr. Beach named Grayton Beach State Park the number one beach in America in 2020.

Is 30A affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $915K median in Santa Rosa Beach (Redfin, Aug 2026). One of the priciest stretches of the Florida coast. Budget for the home, the insurance and often HOA fees on top.

What is the nightlife like in 30A?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. A night out on 30A starts with the sunset. Bud & Alley's rings a bell on the roof deck when the sun hits the water, then it's dinner and live music at a place like The Red Bar or Stinky's Fish Camp. Most nights are done by 10.

How busy does 30A get?

It is quiet most of the year. Fall and winter are calm, and you can ride the bike path for miles without stopping. Spring break and summer are the tradeoff, when the two lane road slows to a crawl.

Is 30A good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Fifteen rare coastal dune lakes for paddleboards and kayaks, and public ramps onto Choctawhatchee Bay for bigger boats.

Which airport is closest to 30A?

Two regional airports, each about 45 to 50 minutes away: Northwest Florida Beaches (ECP) and Destin Fort Walton (VPS).

What should I know before moving to 30A?

The main things to plan for: Peak season traffic, HOA rules, hurricane exposure. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
