About Palm Coast

Palm Coast is green, tidy and built for an easy routine. Neighborhoods sit along saltwater canals and wooded paths, so people walk dogs, ride bikes and play golf or pickleball most days. Many residents are retirees and families who moved from up north for newer homes, good value and a quieter pace, with the ocean on the barrier island across the Intracoastal.

The trade offs: canal homes can flood in big storms, so check the flood zone and the elevation before you buy. It's very car dependent, with shopping along a few main roads and not much of a walkable downtown. The airport in Daytona is about 35 minutes away, and the newer sections west of US 1 are a fair drive from the beach.

Most morningsCoffee, then a ride on the Lehigh Trail, nodding to the same cyclists you see every week.

Where it shines

Active outdoorsTop 12%

More than 130 miles of connected trails link the parks and preserves, including Linear Park, Long Creek Nature Preserve and Bird of Paradise Nature Reserve. Add a city golf course, an aquatics center and Waterfront Park on the Intracoastal.

On the waterTop 13%

Twenty three miles of saltwater canals put a dock behind many homes, all leading to the Intracoastal. Waterfront Park sits right on the waterway and gets a new pavilion in October 2026. Ocean access is the weak spot, a long run by boat.

Where it falls short

NightlifeBottom 33%

Palm Coast goes to bed early. European Village has a courtyard ringed with restaurants and bars, and the city puts on the odd evening event like Beats and Eats, with food trucks and live music. After that, lights out.

Dining and artsBottom 25%

Mostly plazas and chains, with European Village as the exception, a courtyard of restaurants and bars. The Palm Coast Songwriters Festival comes around each year and The Stage at Town Center hosts city events. St. Augustine is close for more.

Coastal charmBottom 22%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Meet peopleBottom 18%

The city rec department does the heavy lifting. There is tennis and pickleball at the Southern Recreation Center, golf at Palm Harbor, mahjong meetups, a farmers market at European Village and a holiday boat parade each December.

WalkabilityBottom 17%

You will drive for nearly everything. There is no traditional downtown, and stores sit in plazas along Palm Coast Parkway and SR 100. The trails are for exercise, not errands. European Village is the one spot built for strolling.

Airport accessBottom 14%

Daytona Beach (DAB) is about 35 to 40 minutes south with a short list of routes. Jacksonville (JAX) is about an hour and 15 minutes north and Orlando (MCO) around an hour and a half. I-95 runs right through town.

Common questions

Is Palm Coast walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. You will drive for nearly everything. There is no traditional downtown, and stores sit in plazas along Palm Coast Parkway and SR 100. The trails are for exercise, not errands. European Village is the one spot built for strolling.

Does Palm Coast have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. No beach inside the city proper. You cross the Hammock Dunes Bridge to a quiet stretch of coquina sand, or drive south to Flagler Beach. The county has 19 miles of coastline, and it rarely feels crowded.

Is Palm Coast affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $356K median sale (Redfin, May 2026). Newer houses for less than most of coastal Florida, and being inland of the barrier island helps on insurance. Saltwater canal homes cost more and can flood in storms, so check elevation and flood zone before you buy.

What is the nightlife like in Palm Coast?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. A night out usually means dinner in the courtyard at European Village, where the restaurants and bars face each other, or a city event like Beats and Eats with food trucks and a band. It is an early evening, and St. Augustine is about 30 minutes north if you want more.

How busy does Palm Coast get?

It is quiet most of the year. Quiet is the default. Neighborhoods are spread out under pines and oaks and there is no tourist district to speak of. Traffic builds on Palm Coast Parkway at rush hour, and that is about the worst of it.

Is Palm Coast good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Twenty three miles of saltwater canals put a dock behind many homes, all leading to the Intracoastal. Waterfront Park sits right on the waterway and gets a new pavilion in October 2026. Ocean access is the weak spot, a long run by boat.
