About Port Salerno

Port Salerno is an old fishing village around the Manatee Pocket, a sheltered harbor near the St. Lucie Inlet. Working docks sit next to seafood spots, art studios and a boardwalk along the water. Boaters love how quickly they can get out the inlet, and the town feels casual and friendly.

Homes around the Pocket face hurricane and flood exposure, and river water quality can suffer after Lake Okeechobee releases. Winters bring more traffic and visitors. West Palm Beach airport is about 40 minutes away, and Stuart has the hospital and big stores.

Common questions

Is Port Salerno walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. The waterfront core is walkable, with a boardwalk along the Pocket and several restaurants within a few blocks of each other. For groceries and errands you drive to Federal Highway.

Does Port Salerno have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No beach in the village. Bathtub Beach and Stuart Beach are about 15 to 20 minutes by car, or closer by boat to the inlet beaches.

Is Port Salerno affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $392K median sale (Redfin, Aug 2026). One of the more approachable ways onto the water in Martin County, with a recent median under $400K. Waterfront homes and anything with a dock cost much more, and insurance matters.

What is the nightlife like in Port Salerno?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Sunset over the Pocket from a dock bar, then dinner at District Table or Two Georges and a nightcap at The Pearl. Or join an evening ghost tour through the old village.

How busy does Port Salerno get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Mostly calm, with a busy few blocks on weekend nights and boat traffic in the morning. The streets back from the water are quiet.

Is Port Salerno good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. The best part. The Manatee Pocket is a protected harbor right by the St. Lucie Inlet, with marinas, a public boat ramp, charter boats and sailing trips. The ocean is a few minutes out.

Which airport is closest to Port Salerno?

Palm Beach is about 40 minutes south. Orlando is about two hours, and nearby airports have only limited service.

What should I know before moving to Port Salerno?

The main things to plan for: Hurricane and flood exposure around the Pocket, river water quality after Lake Okeechobee releases.
