Redington Shores

A small barrier island town with a big public beach park, bay side canal streets full of boat lifts, and a hometown calendar of picnics and holiday parades.

A day in Redington Shores

You take the boat out from your canal in the morning, spend the afternoon at the beach access park and finish with a casual dinner a short walk up Gulf Boulevard.

The scorecard

Tap any score for the local's take.

Nightlife2

Mostly neighborhood bars and grills along Gulf Boulevard, plus sunset on the sand. For music and a bigger crowd you head south to John's Pass in Madeira Beach, about 10 minutes away.

Meet people3

Easier than you would think for a town this size. Town Hall runs a fall picnic, a Trunk or Treat, a community yard sale, a Christmas tree lighting and both a holiday boat parade and a golf cart parade, and residents sit down with commissioners at regular coffee meetups.

Peace and quiet4

Generally quiet, especially on the bay side streets. In 2026 there is still plenty of construction as storm damaged homes come down and new elevated ones go up, so weekday noise is real.

Walkability3

Somewhat walkable. You can reach the beach, the town nature park and a few restaurants on foot, but groceries and bigger errands mean driving over the bridge to Seminole or down to Madeira Beach.

On the water4

Strong water access. Bay side streets run along canals off the Intracoastal with docks and lifts, there is a local yacht club, and the Gulf is a short boat ride through the passes.

Beaches4

The county beach access park has a wide stretch of Gulf sand, restrooms and showers, and the rest of the shoreline is lined with condos and homes. Shorebirds nest along parts of the beach in summer.

Dining and arts3

Casual and local, mostly grills, seafood and pizza along Gulf Boulevard. Coco Wood Grill reopened after Helene. For more choice, Madeira Beach and Seminole are 10 to 15 minutes away.

Outdoors and wellness3

Beach walks, paddling the bay and fishing from docks are the daily routine. Del Bello Nature Park in town is a small green space, and Sand Key Park and Fort De Soto are short drives.

Affordability2

Pricey for the area. Redfin put the median sale price around $625K in September 2026 with homes sitting about 117 days. Rebuild costs, elevation rules and flood insurance weigh heavily on older homes.

Airport access4

St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) is about 25 minutes away and Tampa International (TPA) about 40 minutes.
