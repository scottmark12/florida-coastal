About Holly Hill

Holly Hill is a small city on the mainland bank of the Halifax River. Riverside Drive runs along the water with homes, condos and a waterfront park, and the rest of town is quiet streets of older single family homes and a few new townhome rows. It's one of the more affordable places near the beach in Volusia County, and you can be on the sand in Daytona in about ten minutes.

The trade offs: low areas flood in hurricanes and big rain, and Redfin rates most homes here at risk over the next 30 years. Some of the housing is older and may need work. Daytona event weeks bring traffic. Shopping and dining are mostly along Ridgewood Avenue and in the cities next door.

Common questions

Is Holly Hill walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Riverside Drive is a pleasant walk along the water. Most of the city is laid out for driving, though, with errands along Ridgewood Avenue.

Does Holly Hill have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No beach in the city itself. The Atlantic beach in Daytona and Ormond Beach is about 10 minutes across the river, and you can still drive on parts of it.

Is Holly Hill affordable?

Yes. It is one of the more affordable spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $247K median sale (Redfin, July 2026). Very affordable for the coast. Redfin put the median sale at about $247K in July 2026, and riverfront condos at Marina Grande run higher.

What is the nightlife like in Holly Hill?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. Sunset on Riverside Drive, a casual dinner nearby, then maybe a short drive over to the beach side in Daytona. Most evenings are relaxed and inexpensive.

How busy does Holly Hill get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. A mix. The streets near the river are calm, while Ridgewood Avenue and Nova Road are busy commercial roads, and Daytona's event weeks add noise and traffic.

Is Holly Hill good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The Halifax River runs the length of town, with riverfront parks, docks at the condo buildings and boat ramps nearby. The open ocean is across the bridges.

Which airport is closest to Holly Hill?

Daytona Beach (DAB) is about 15 minutes away, with a few airlines and some direct flights. Orlando (MCO) is about an hour.

What should I know before moving to Holly Hill?

The main things to plan for: Hurricane and river flooding in low areas, event week traffic, an older housing stock in places.
