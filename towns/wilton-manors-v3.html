About Wilton Manors

Wilton Manors is a little city almost fully ringed by the Middle River, just north of downtown Fort Lauderdale. Its heart is Wilton Drive, a strip of restaurants, bars and shops where people walk, bike and run into friends. It is one of the best known LGBTQ communities in the country, and it feels like a small town where everyone says hello.

The trade offs: low streets flood during king tides and heavy rain, so ask about each home's flood history. Prices have climbed well above much of Broward County, and older ranch homes may need work to insure. Weekend nights on the Drive get loud and parking is tight.

Where it shines

Meet peopleTop 4%

About as easy as it gets. Something is on along Wilton Drive every night, from karaoke to line dancing, and the city calendar adds Taste of the Island, a citywide yard sale, a historical society and street parties like Wicked Manors.

Dining and artsTop 6%

Wilton Drive covers a lot of ground for one street, from Greek and Thai to sushi, tacos and pizza, with a few galleries mixed in. Taste of the Island gathers the restaurants each November. Big theaters and museums are 10 minutes south.

NightlifeTop 6%

Wilton Drive lines up bars, lounges, dance floors and show rooms within a few walkable blocks, busy seven nights a week. Hunters Nightclub runs until 2am on weeknights and 3am on Fridays and Saturdays.

Airport accessTop 6%

FLL is about 20 minutes south by I-95 or US 1, and Miami (MIA) is around 45 to 50 minutes. Brightline's Fort Lauderdale station is about 10 minutes away for trains to Miami, West Palm Beach and Orlando.

WalkabilityTop 9%

The whole city is under two square miles, and Wilton Drive packs its restaurants, bars and shops onto one main street. From most blocks you can walk or bike to dinner. Bigger errands and the beach take a car.

Where it falls short

AffordabilityBottom 26%

Pricey for a city with no beach. Being this close to downtown Fort Lauderdale keeps house prices well above the Florida median. Condos and townhomes cost less, and low lying streets near the river need flood coverage.

Active outdoorsBottom 14%

Pocket parks, not big ones. Colohatchee Park has a mangrove boardwalk and a dog park, Richardson Historic Park has a riverside nature preserve, and Mickel Field has a walking trail. For real trails you drive.

Common questions

Is Wilton Manors walkable?

Yes, for the most part. The whole city is under two square miles, and Wilton Drive packs its restaurants, bars and shops onto one main street. From most blocks you can walk or bike to dinner. Bigger errands and the beach take a car.

Does Wilton Manors have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No beach in town. The city sits inland, ringed by river and not by ocean. Fort Lauderdale beach is the nearest sand, roughly a 10 to 15 minute drive east.

Is Wilton Manors affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $693K median sale (Resideline, Oct 2026). Pricey for a city with no beach. Being this close to downtown Fort Lauderdale keeps house prices well above the Florida median. Condos and townhomes cost less, and low lying streets near the river need flood coverage.

What is the nightlife like in Wilton Manors?

Really good for a beach town. You park once and walk. Dinner on Wilton Drive, then bars, lounges and dance floors one after another down the same street, with karaoke, DJs or line dancing depending on the night. Hunters Nightclub stays open until 2am on weeknights and 3am on Fridays and Saturdays.

How busy does Wilton Manors get?

It gets busy, especially in season. Wilton Drive is lively every night, weekends bring crowds and tight parking, and the big street parties close the Drive to traffic. The side streets toward the river are noticeably calmer.

Is Wilton Manors good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The two forks of the Middle River wrap around the city, so you can paddle a loop around it, and Colohatchee Park has a public boat ramp. This is small boat and kayak water. The big marinas are in Fort Lauderdale.

Which airport is closest to Wilton Manors?

FLL is about 20 minutes south by I-95 or US 1, and Miami (MIA) is around 45 to 50 minutes. Brightline's Fort Lauderdale station is about 10 minutes away for trains to Miami, West Palm Beach and Orlando.
