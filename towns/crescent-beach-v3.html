About Crescent Beach

Crescent Beach is a quiet stretch of beach houses and condos south of St. Augustine Beach, between the ocean and the Matanzas River. The beach is wide and hard packed, and most days it's just neighbors, dogs and people fishing the surf. There's not much commercial here, which is the point. Locals paddle the river, fish the inlet and drive into St. Augustine when they want a night out.

The trade offs: it's a barrier island, so storms are a real concern, and A1A has washed over and eroded to the south near Summer Haven and Matanzas Inlet. Both airports are about an hour away. Groceries, doctors and restaurants mostly mean a drive north.

Where it shines

Peace and quietTop 8%

One of the calmest stretches on this coast. Summer weekends bring day trippers and cars on the beach, but most of the year it is beach houses, condos and the sound of the surf.

Active outdoorsTop 14%

A big strength. Fort Matanzas, Faver Dykes State Park and Matanzas State Forest are all close, and the river and marsh are full of birds and dolphins.

BeachesTop 22%

Wide, flat and hard packed, with gentle waves on calm days. You can drive and park on the sand at the Crescent Beach ramp, with seasonal hours during sea turtle season.

Where it falls short

WalkabilityBottom 33%

Not much to walk to. A1A is the one main road, so you drive for groceries and errands, though the beach itself is a great long walk.

Dining and artsBottom 28%

A short list of casual seafood spots, including Commander's Shellfish Camp on the river. For more choice you head north to St. Augustine Beach or downtown.

On the waterBottom 17%

The Matanzas River runs right behind the barrier island. Frank Butler Park West has a boat ramp onto the river, and the water is great for kayaks, fishing and crabbing.

Meet peopleBottom 14%

Fewer organized groups than in town. People meet at the beach, the boat ramp and the parks, and most clubs, classes and leagues are up in St. Augustine Beach or St. Augustine.

Airport accessBottom 11%

This is the trade off. Jacksonville (JAX) is about an hour and ten minutes and Daytona Beach (DAB) is about an hour, so travel days start early.

NightlifeBottom 8%

Almost none, and people like it that way. A drink at a river side seafood spot or a beach bar is the big night out, and St. Augustine is about 20 minutes north when you want more.

Common questions

Is Crescent Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Not much to walk to. A1A is the one main road, so you drive for groceries and errands, though the beach itself is a great long walk.

Does Crescent Beach have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. Wide, flat and hard packed, with gentle waves on calm days. You can drive and park on the sand at the Crescent Beach ramp, with seasonal hours during sea turtle season.

Is Crescent Beach affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $440K median sale (Redfin, June 2026). Middle of the road for coastal St. Johns County. Condos and older homes keep the median down, while oceanfront and riverfront houses run much higher, and insurance is a real line item.

What is the nightlife like in Crescent Beach?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Sunset over the Matanzas River, a casual seafood dinner on the water, and home early. Evenings here are quiet and slow.

Which airport is closest to Crescent Beach?
