About Neptune Beach

Neptune Beach is mostly homes, with very little commercial strip. People walk to the beach at the end of their street, bike to Beaches Town Center for coffee and dinner, and see the same neighbors at the park and the market. It suits families and retirees who want a calm beach town that still has places to go.

The trade offs are price and space. Homes near the ocean are expensive and lots are small, and older cottages often need work. Like its neighbors, it faces hurricane and flood risk, so check flood zones and insurance before you buy. The airport is about 35 minutes away.

Where it shines

Meet peopleTop 9%

A small town with a few steady meeting points. The Beaches Green Market sets up on Saturdays at Jarboe Park, Dancin' in the Street takes over the Town Center every May, and The BookMark is the kind of bookstore where you run into people you know.

WalkabilityTop 11%

The town is small enough that most streets are an easy walk or bike ride from Beaches Town Center, where restaurants, coffee, a bookstore and shops share a few blocks with the Atlantic Beach side. The sand is at the end of the street.

Dining and artsTop 16%

Good eating for a small town, nearly all of it in Beaches Town Center. North Beach Fish Camp, Doro, Mezza Luna and Hawkers sit within a block or two of each other, with The BookMark and a row of shops in between.

Active outdoorsTop 17%

Jarboe Park is the town's main green space and home to the Saturday green market. Beyond that, the outdoors is the beach itself. For wooded trails, a lake and camping, Hanna Park is about ten minutes north.

NightlifeTop 23%

A solid handful of spots, all in one corner of town. Pete's Bar, Lemon Bar and the Flying Iguana anchor Beaches Town Center, and the mood is more corner bar than club. For later and louder you go two miles south to Jax Beach.

Where it falls short

AffordabilityBottom 17%

Pricey. The town is small and built out, so few homes come up for sale and anything near the ocean runs well above the Florida median. The blocks west of Third Street cost less, and beach town insurance applies everywhere.

On the waterBottom 13%

Common questions

Is Neptune Beach walkable?

Yes, for the most part. The town is small enough that most streets are an easy walk or bike ride from Beaches Town Center, where restaurants, coffee, a bookstore and shops share a few blocks with the Atlantic Beach side. The sand is at the end of the street.

Does Neptune Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Wide, firm sand with public access at nearly every street end. Parking at the accesses is limited, so walking or biking over is the usual move. The surf is decent and the sand is less crowded than Jax Beach to the south.

Is Neptune Beach affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $790K median sale (Momentum, Q2 2026). Pricey. The town is small and built out, so few homes come up for sale and anything near the ocean runs well above the Florida median. The blocks west of Third Street cost less, and beach town insurance applies everywhere.

What is the nightlife like in Neptune Beach?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Nights here stay in the Town Center. Dinner at North Beach Fish Camp or Doro, a drink at Pete's Bar or by the ocean at Lemon Bar, and then you walk home. It is lively without being a late night scene.

How busy does Neptune Beach get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Most of town is calm streets a few blocks from the ocean. The Town Center corner is the exception, with steady evening crowds and tight parking, and Atlantic Boulevard and Third Street carry real traffic. Summer weekends are the busy stretch.

Is Neptune Beach good for boating and fishing?

There is some access, but it is limited. Water here means the ocean. There is no marina or boat ramp in town, so boats launch on the Intracoastal in the neighboring towns or up at Mayport. Surfing and paddling off the beach are the easy options.

Which airport is closest to Neptune Beach?

Jacksonville (JAX) is about 35 minutes away, on the north side of the city. That is a short airport run for a Florida beach town.
