Bal Harbour

A tiny oceanfront village at the north tip of Miami Beach, known for a luxury shopping center and a quiet beach path.

A day in Bal Harbour

You jog the beach path at sunrise, have a long lunch at the Bal Harbour Shops and walk the beach at dusk.

Sophisticated Laid Back Remote Work from Paradise

The scorecard

Nightlife2

Evenings are quiet. Dinner at the Bal Harbour Shops, which close at 9pm, or a drink at one of the oceanfront hotels. Once a month the village puts on its Sounds by the Sea concert by the water, and that is about it.

Meet people2

A small calendar for a small village. The Market at Bal Harbour sets up on weekends, Sounds by the Sea brings a monthly concert, and Waterfront Park has a community center with classes. Bigger clubs and leagues are a drive away.

Peace and quiet4

No bar strip and no boardwalk crowds, just a beach path and the sound of the inlet. The tradeoff is Collins Avenue and 96th Street, which back up around the Shops, mostly in winter.

Walkability4

You can walk the whole village end to end. A beach path runs along the oceanfront to the jetty, the Bal Harbour Shops sit at 96th Street and Collins, and Surfside's Harding Avenue shops are just across the street to the south.

On the water3

Haulover Inlet is at the north edge of the village. Just over the bridge, Bill Bird Marina at Haulover Park has 152 wet slips, a fuel dock, a boat ramp and a charter fishing fleet. The village itself has no public marina.

Beaches4

A wide, uncrowded stretch of sand backed by a landscaped beach path instead of a boardwalk. Public parking is scarce, so most people arrive on foot. The jetty at the north end is where you watch boats run the inlet.

Dining and arts4

The open air Bal Harbour Shops hold Makoto, Carpaccio and a handful of other restaurants among the designer stores, and the oceanfront hotels add dining rooms like Atlantikós. Lots of polish, not much that is casual or cheap.

Outdoors and wellness2

Green space is small. Waterfront Park is 1.5 acres with a lawn, fitness stations and a basketball court, and the beach path is good for a run. For room to roam, Haulover Park's 177 acres are just over the bridge.

Affordability1

One of the most expensive addresses on the Florida coast. Nearly everything is an oceanfront condo or a large house on the bay side, and fees, insurance and taxes follow the price.

Airport access4

Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL) are each roughly 30 to 35 minutes away. Having two to choose from helps, but neither is close and rush hour adds time.

Common questions about Bal Harbour

Is Bal Harbour walkable?

Yes, for the most part. You can walk the whole village end to end. A beach path runs along the oceanfront to the jetty, the Bal Harbour Shops sit at 96th Street and Collins, and Surfside's Harding Avenue shops are just across the street to the south.

Does Bal Harbour have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. A wide, uncrowded stretch of sand backed by a landscaped beach path instead of a boardwalk. Public parking is scarce, so most people arrive on foot. The jetty at the north end is where you watch boats run the inlet.

Is Bal Harbour affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: $1.28M median. One of the most expensive addresses on the Florida coast. Nearly everything is an oceanfront condo or a large house on the bay side, and fees, insurance and taxes follow the price.

What is the nightlife like in Bal Harbour?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A night out here is dinner, not a late night. A table at Makoto or Carpaccio at the Shops, or a hotel dining room on the ocean, then a walk on the beach path. For music past ten you head down to South Beach or over to the mainland.

How busy does Bal Harbour get?

It is quiet most of the year. No bar strip and no boardwalk crowds, just a beach path and the sound of the inlet. The tradeoff is Collins Avenue and 96th Street, which back up around the Shops, mostly in winter.

Is Bal Harbour good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. Haulover Inlet is at the north edge of the village. Just over the bridge, Bill Bird Marina at Haulover Park has 152 wet slips, a fuel dock, a boat ramp and a charter fishing fleet. The village itself has no public marina.

Which airport is closest to Bal Harbour?

Miami (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale (FLL) are each roughly 30 to 35 minutes away. Having two to choose from helps, but neither is close and rush hour adds time.
