About Titusville

Titusville sits on the Indian River straight across from the launch pads, so rockets are part of everyday life here. It's a laid back, down to earth town with a small historic downtown, a lot of retirees and space workers, and the Merritt Island refuge and Canaveral National Seashore right next door. If you like birds, fishing, kayaking and empty beaches, there's a lot to do.

The trade offs: Titusville is about 45 minutes from Orlando's airport and a bit of a drive to the bigger shopping and hospitals to the south. Brevard gets hurricanes, and the lagoon has had water quality problems with algae and fish kills in some years. Launch days bring traffic and crowds to the riverfront.

Common questions

Is Titusville walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. The historic downtown is a few blocks of shops, cafes, the Playhouse and the American Space Museum, with a bike trail running through. Beyond that it is a long, narrow city along US 1 and you drive.

Does Titusville have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. Playalinda Beach is the nearest ocean beach, undeveloped and part of the longest wild stretch of sand on Florida's east coast. It is a drive out through the refuge, and it closes for launches.

Is Titusville affordable?

Yes. It is one of the more affordable spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $292K median sale (Momentum, Nov 2026). Among the lowest prices on the Florida coast. Homes near downtown and the river cost far less than the beach towns to the south. Riverfront property still needs flood insurance.

What is the nightlife like in Titusville?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Dinner downtown, a musical at the Titusville Playhouse, and you are home by 10. On launch nights the riverfront fills up to watch the rocket climb over the lagoon. In summer, a guided paddle through glowing water is the late option.

How busy does Titusville get?

It is quiet most of the year. Slow most days, with little traffic. The exception is a big launch, when US 1 and the riverfront fill with cars and viewers for a few hours and then empty again.

Is Titusville good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The city marina sits on the Indian River Lagoon with the launch pads across the water. Summer nights bring bioluminescence kayak tours in the refuge. Water quality in the lagoon is an ongoing concern.

Which airport is closest to Titusville?

Orlando (MCO) is about 45 minutes west, with flights nearly everywhere. Sanford (SFB) is roughly the same drive, and Melbourne (MLB) is about 45 minutes south.

What should I know before moving to Titusville?

The main things to plan for: Hurricane exposure, lagoon water quality. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
