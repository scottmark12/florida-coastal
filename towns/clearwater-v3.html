About Clearwater

Clearwater is the mainland city behind Clearwater Beach. Downtown sits on a bluff over the bay, with a rebuilt waterfront park and a concert lawn, and the sand is a short drive or trolley ride across the causeway. Neighborhoods range from older brick ranch streets near downtown to golf and lake communities up in Countryside, so you see retirees, families and plenty of people who work in the Tampa Bay area.

The beach brings traffic, especially in season and on spring weekends, and the causeway can back up. Homes near the water face storm surge, and Helene and Milton were a hard reminder in 2024. Prices on the beach itself are much higher than on the mainland.

Dining and artsTop 31%

Ruth Eckerd Hall and the 1921 Capitol Theatre bring in touring acts, and the Clearwater Jazz Holiday fills Coachman Park each October. Cleveland Street's restaurant row is still filling in, with new spots since 2025.

NightlifeTop 33%

The BayCare Sound at Coachman Park is a 4,000 seat waterfront amphitheater with touring acts, and the Bilheimer Capitol Theatre on Cleveland Street books shows too. For a late night you cross the causeway to the beach bars.

AffordabilityTop 32%

Homes on the mainland cost a good deal less than on the barrier islands next door, with plenty of choice inland. Near the water, check flood zones, since insurance swings a lot from street to street.

Meet peopleTop 42%

The Market Marie sets up at Coachman Park on second Saturdays. The city runs adult softball and kickball leagues, plus shuffleboard, horseshoes and table tennis clubs, and the Threshers play at BayCare Ballpark.

Active outdoorsBottom 38%

Common questions

Is Clearwater walkable?

Does Clearwater have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. No sand in town itself. Clearwater Beach, ranked second in the country by Tripadvisor for 2026, is about two miles over the Memorial Causeway by car, bike or ferry.

What is the nightlife like in Clearwater?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A typical night is dinner on Cleveland Street and a concert at The BayCare Sound or a show at the Capitol Theatre. Downtown winds down after the encore, so the late bars mean a ride over the causeway to the beach.

How busy does Clearwater get?

It gets busy, especially in season. It's a real city with real traffic. Beach traffic funnels through downtown onto the Memorial Causeway and is heaviest on weekends and in spring. Streets away from the main roads are calmer.

Is Clearwater good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Clearwater Harbor Marina sits downtown beside Coachman Park, and the Clearwater Ferry runs Thursday through Sunday to the beach and Dunedin. Clearwater Pass and the Gulf are a short run across the harbor.

Which airport is closest to Clearwater?

About as easy as it gets. St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) is around 15 to 20 minutes away and Tampa (TPA) about 30, depending on which side of the city you start from.
