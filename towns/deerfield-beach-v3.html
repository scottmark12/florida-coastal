About Deerfield Beach

Deerfield Beach is at the north end of Broward County, next to Boca Raton. The beachfront has a fishing pier, a broadwalk and nine lifeguard stands along the sand.

The median sale is around $330K, but that includes many inland condos, and houses run higher. Fort Lauderdale's airport is about 30 minutes away and Palm Beach's about 35, and Broward Health North is in town.
