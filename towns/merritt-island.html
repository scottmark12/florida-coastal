Merritt Island

A big, leafy island between the Indian and Banana Rivers, full of canal homes and boat docks, with the space center and a huge wildlife refuge at its north end.

A day in Merritt Island

You take the boat out from your backyard canal in the morning, run errands on Courtenay Parkway after lunch and drive the refuge loop to watch birds at dusk.

The scorecard

Tap any score for the local's take.

Nightlife1

Quiet. There are a few waterfront bars and grills and some local wine and coffee spots, and that is mostly it. For a real night out people go to Cocoa Village, Cocoa Beach or Port Canaveral, all 10 to 20 minutes away.

Meet people3

Easier than the size suggests if you join something. Kiwanis Island Park has a community center with a gym, courts and ballfields, the veterans memorial center hosts events, and boating and fishing clubs give a lot of people their crew.

Peace and quiet4

Calm. Most of the island is residential streets, canals and big trees, and the north end is open refuge land. The busy part is the commercial strip along Courtenay Parkway and SR 520, plus traffic around big launches.

Walkability1

Not walkable. It is an unincorporated area built around the car, with long blocks and errands spread along the main roads. People walk and bike in their own neighborhoods and on the refuge roads.

On the water5

This is the draw. Many homes sit on canals with docks, the Indian River is on one side and the Banana River on the other, and boats can reach the ocean through the Port Canaveral locks. Kayak launches and ramps are all over the island.

Beaches2

No ocean beach on the island. Cocoa Beach and Cape Canaveral are about 15 to 20 minutes east over the causeway, and Playalinda Beach in the national seashore is a longer drive north through the refuge.

Dining and arts2

Mostly chains and local casual places along the main roads, plus a handful of waterfront grills. For more choice you head to Cocoa Village or the beach towns, both close.

Outdoors and wellness5

Among the best on the Space Coast. Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge covers the north end with marsh, scrub and birding drives, and the Kennedy Space Center visitor complex is next door. You need a refuge pass for Black Point Wildlife Drive and some boat ramps.

Affordability3

A middle ground. You get more house, land and water access for the money than on the beach, but canal and riverfront homes carry a premium, and flood insurance matters in the low lying areas.

Airport access4

Orlando (MCO) is about 45 minutes west on the Beachline, and Melbourne (MLB) is about 35 minutes south. Easy for a Space Coast address.

Common questions about Merritt Island

Is Merritt Island walkable?

No. Plan on driving for almost everything. Not walkable. It is an unincorporated area built around the car, with long blocks and errands spread along the main roads. People walk and bike in their own neighborhoods and on the refuge roads.

Does Merritt Island have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No ocean beach on the island. Cocoa Beach and Cape Canaveral are about 15 to 20 minutes east over the causeway, and Playalinda Beach in the national seashore is a longer drive north through the refuge.

Is Merritt Island affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $400K median sale price, all home types (fall 2026). A middle ground. You get more house, land and water access for the money than on the beach, but canal and riverfront homes carry a premium, and flood insurance matters in the low lying areas.

What is the nightlife like in Merritt Island?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Most evenings are low key, a sunset cruise on the river or dinner on a waterfront deck. On a launch night people head to the causeways or the river to watch. For a livelier evening you drive 10 minutes to Cocoa Village.

How busy does Merritt Island get?

It is quiet most of the year. Calm. Most of the island is residential streets, canals and big trees, and the north end is open refuge land. The busy part is the commercial strip along Courtenay Parkway and SR 520, plus traffic around big launches.

Is Merritt Island good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. This is the draw. Many homes sit on canals with docks, the Indian River is on one side and the Banana River on the other, and boats can reach the ocean through the Port Canaveral locks. Kayak launches and ramps are all over the island.

Which airport is closest to Merritt Island?

Orlando (MCO) is about 45 minutes west on the Beachline, and Melbourne (MLB) is about 35 minutes south. Easy for a Space Coast address.
