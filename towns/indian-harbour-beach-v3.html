About Indian Harbour Beach

Indian Harbour Beach is a small, quiet town on the barrier island between Satellite Beach and Indialantic. It's mostly homes, with ocean access along A1A, canal neighborhoods on the river side and a big park in the middle of town. People here walk, bike, boat and fish, and many have been in the same house for years.

The trade offs: it's a barrier island, so hurricanes and flooding are real, and canal homes need flood insurance. There isn't much nightlife or a downtown, so you'll drive to Melbourne for more. Prices on the water climb fast.

Common questions

Is Indian Harbour Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. Partly walkable. The town is only about a mile and a half square, so you can walk or bike to the beach, the park and a few errands. For most shopping you drive.

Does Indian Harbour Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Good Atlantic beach with several public access points along A1A and little crowding. It feels like the beaches in Satellite Beach and Indialantic on either side.

Is Indian Harbour Beach affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $454K typical home value (Zillow, Sept 2026). Mid priced for the barrier island, around $454K typical. Oceanfront and canal front homes cost a lot more, and flood and wind insurance are a real line in the budget.

What is the nightlife like in Indian Harbour Beach?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. Evenings are home and backyard nights. You walk to the beach to watch the sky change, or take the paddleboard out on the canal. For dinner and drinks you cross the causeway to Eau Gallie.

How busy does Indian Harbour Beach get?

It is quiet most of the year. Calm. It is mostly residential streets and canals, with no tourist strip. The busier roads are A1A and the causeway approach.

Is Indian Harbour Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Strong. Many homes sit on canals off the Banana River with docks, Bicentennial Park has a kayak and paddleboard launch, and the Indian River and the Eau Gallie marinas are just across the causeway.

Which airport is closest to Indian Harbour Beach?

Melbourne (MLB) is about 15 minutes away across the causeway, a small airport with a handful of routes. Orlando (MCO) is about an hour.

What should I know before moving to Indian Harbour Beach?
