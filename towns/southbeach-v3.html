About Miami (South Beach)

South Beach is the southern end of Miami Beach: pastel Art Deco buildings, a wide Atlantic beach, Lincoln Road, and some of the best restaurants in the country. Behind the busy strip are leafy streets around Flamingo Park where people walk dogs, play tennis and know their neighbors.

It's almost all condos, costs are high, and visitors fill it every weekend. King tide flooding, condo assessments and insurance are real costs. If you want a walkable, international city life on the beach, though, it's hard to match.

Where it shines

NightlifeTop 1%

Nights run long here. The Betsy has live music nightly, Mango's Tropical Cafe keeps a stage going on Ocean Drive, Greystone Bar has jazz Thursday through Saturday, and Mac's Club Deuce on 14th Street pours until 5am.

Meet peopleTop 1%

There is something to join most days. A Sunday farmers market and a free weekly yoga class on Lincoln Road, an Art Walk the third Thursday of each month, and free WALLCAST concerts on the lawn at SoundScape Park.

WalkabilityTop 1%

You can live here without a car. Lincoln Road is a pedestrian mall, the paved Beachwalk runs north along the ocean from South Pointe Park, and groceries, cafes and the sand are all a few flat blocks apart.

Dining and artsTop 2%

Restaurants line Lincoln Road, Ocean Drive and the side streets between them. The New World Symphony plays at New World Center, art museums and a major December art fair are close by, and Art Deco Weekend marks its 50th year in January 2027.

Airport accessTop 3%

Miami International (MIA) is about 20 to 25 minutes across the causeway when traffic behaves. Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is a second choice about 45 minutes north.

BeachesTop 23%

Wide public sand runs the whole length of the neighborhood, with Lummus Park and its Muscle Beach outdoor gym along Ocean Drive. The water is warm and clear on a calm day. You share it with a lot of visitors.

Where it falls short

AffordabilityBottom 17%

Among the most expensive places to live on the Florida coast. Even small condos cost a lot, and monthly fees, flood and wind insurance and assessments on older buildings add to the bill.

Peace and quietBottom 2%

About as busy as the Florida coast gets. Ocean Drive and Collins Avenue draw crowds all year, winter and spring weekends back up the causeways, and parking is a daily puzzle. The blocks south of Fifth Street are calmer.

Common questions

Is South Beach walkable?

Yes. It is one of the most walkable towns on the Florida coast. You can live here without a car. Lincoln Road is a pedestrian mall, the paved Beachwalk runs north along the ocean from South Pointe Park, and groceries, cafes and the sand are all a few flat blocks apart.

Does South Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Wide public sand runs the whole length of the neighborhood, with Lummus Park and its Muscle Beach outdoor gym along Ocean Drive. The water is warm and clear on a calm day. You share it with a lot of visitors.

Is South Beach affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $524K typical value across Miami Beach (Zillow, May 2026). Among the most expensive places to live on the Florida coast. Even small condos cost a lot, and monthly fees, flood and wind insurance and assessments on older buildings add to the bill.

What is the nightlife like in South Beach?

Big city nightlife, with late hours and plenty of choice. A night out starts late. Dinner on Lincoln Road or Ocean Drive, live music at The Betsy or salsa at Mango's Tropical Cafe, and if you are still going, Mac's Club Deuce is open until 5am. On WALLCAST nights you can bring a blanket to SoundScape Park instead.

How busy does South Beach get?

It is busy most of the year. About as busy as the Florida coast gets. Ocean Drive and Collins Avenue draw crowds all year, winter and spring weekends back up the causeways, and parking is a daily puzzle. The blocks south of Fifth Street are calmer.

Is South Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Miami Beach Marina has 400 slips right beside Government Cut, so the ocean is minutes away, with fishing charters and a dive shop on site. South Pointe Park has a pier at the tip of the island for watching the ships go out.

Which airport is closest to South Beach?

Miami International (MIA) is about 20 to 25 minutes across the causeway when traffic behaves. Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is a second choice about 45 minutes north.

What should I know before moving to South Beach?

The main things to plan for: Flooding, traffic, high insurance. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
