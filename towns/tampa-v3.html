About Tampa

Tampa is a real city: a downtown riverwalk, historic Ybor City, Cuban bakeries, pro sports and an airport most people love. Neighborhoods range from old brick streets and bungalows in Seminole Heights to waterfront homes in South Tampa and newer suburbs to the north. You'll find every age here, from young professionals to retirees who want restaurants and doctors close by.

Traffic is real, and so is flooding in low areas near the bay, which Helene and Milton showed in 2024. South Tampa is pricey, and the Gulf beaches are about 30 to 45 minutes away. Summer is long and hot.

Where it shines

Dining and artsTop 1%

Films and concerts at the 1926 Tampa Theatre, touring Broadway at the Straz Center, the Tampa Museum of Art on the river and a food hall at Armature Works. Ybor City and South Tampa carry most of the restaurant scene.

Airport accessTop 2%

Tampa International (TPA) is about 15 minutes from downtown and is the busiest airport on Florida's Gulf coast. St. Pete-Clearwater (PIE) is about 30 minutes across the bay.

NightlifeTop 2%

Ybor City's 7th Avenue is the late night strip, with clubs open until 3am and concerts at The Ritz Ybor. SoHo, Water Street and Armature Works fill in the rest, and the free streetcar runs until 2am on Fridays and Saturdays.

Meet peopleTop 21%

Plenty of ways in. Run clubs meet along the Riverwalk and in Tampa Heights, Tampa Bay Club Sport runs adult leagues from kickball to pickleball, and the Ybor City Saturday Market sets up in Centennial Park every week.

Where it falls short

Coastal charmBottom 32%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Active outdoorsBottom 29%

The Riverwalk strings together Curtis Hixon, Water Works and Julian B. Lane parks, and Bayshore Boulevard has miles of unbroken bayfront sidewalk. For real woods and river paddling you drive north to Hillsborough River State Park.

BeachesBottom 18%

No Gulf beach here. Ben T. Davis Beach on the Courtney Campbell Causeway and Picnic Island are small bay beaches, fine for a sunset. For Gulf sand, plan on 40 minutes or more to Clearwater Beach or St. Pete Beach.

On the waterBottom 12%

The Hillsborough River runs through downtown and the bay wraps around South Tampa, so paddling and boating are close to home, with public ramps at Davis Islands and Ballast Point. The open Gulf is a long run down the bay.

Peace and quietBottom 2%

This is the big city, and it sounds like one. Rush hour stacks up on I-275, I-4 and the Howard Frankland Bridge, downtown fills for hockey and concerts, and Gasparilla packs Bayshore Boulevard every winter.

Common questions

Is Tampa walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Downtown, Water Street and the Channel District link up on foot along the 2.6 mile Riverwalk, and the free TECO Line Streetcar carries you on to Ybor City. Outside that core, most errands take a car.

Does Tampa have a good beach?

Not really. The beach is not the draw here. No Gulf beach here. Ben T. Davis Beach on the Courtney Campbell Causeway and Picnic Island are small bay beaches, fine for a sunset. For Gulf sand, plan on 40 minutes or more to Clearwater Beach or St. Pete Beach.

Is Tampa affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $479K median (Redfin, summer 2026). Middle of the pack for the Florida coast. Redfin's citywide median sale was about $479,000 in summer 2026. South Tampa and anything on the water run well above that, and neighborhoods north and east of downtown come in lower.

What is the nightlife like in Tampa?

Really good for a beach town. Start with dinner at Armature Works or on Water Street, ride the free streetcar to Ybor City and catch a show at The Ritz Ybor or the Tampa Theatre. The streetcar runs until 2am on Fridays and Saturdays, and the 7th Avenue clubs go until 3.

How busy does Tampa get?

It is busy most of the year. This is the big city, and it sounds like one. Rush hour stacks up on I-275, I-4 and the Howard Frankland Bridge, downtown fills for hockey and concerts, and Gasparilla packs Bayshore Boulevard every winter.

Is Tampa good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The Hillsborough River runs through downtown and the bay wraps around South Tampa, so paddling and boating are close to home, with public ramps at Davis Islands and Ballast Point. The open Gulf is a long run down the bay.

Which airport is closest to Tampa?

Tampa International (TPA) is about 15 minutes from downtown and is the busiest airport on Florida's Gulf coast. St. Pete-Clearwater (PIE) is about 30 minutes across the bay.

What should I know before moving to Tampa?

The main things to plan for: Flooding in low areas (Helene and Milton in 2024), traffic. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
