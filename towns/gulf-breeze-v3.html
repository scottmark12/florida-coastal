About Gulf Breeze

Gulf Breeze sits between downtown Pensacola and Pensacola Beach, with tree lined streets, good schools and a small town pace. People here boat Santa Rosa Sound, walk the trails at Naval Live Oaks, and are at the beach in about ten minutes. It's mostly year round residents, many of them families and retirees.

Water surrounds it on three sides, so check flood zones and elevation on every house. The bridges are the only way in and out; the Pensacola Bay Bridge was shut for months after a barge strike in 2020. Summer weekends bring heavy beach traffic through town, and prices run higher than Pensacola.
