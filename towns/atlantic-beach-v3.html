About Atlantic Beach

Atlantic Beach feels like a small town that happens to sit on the ocean. Oak shaded streets lead to the beach, people bike to the shops and cafes in Beaches Town Center, and Hanna Park's trails and lake are right to the north. It draws families, retirees and longtime locals who like a quieter pace than Jacksonville Beach.

The honest trade offs: prices have climbed, and the older cottages near the ocean rarely come up for sale. Hurricanes and flooding are real risks here, especially close to the ocean and the marsh, so check flood zones and insurance early. The airport is about 35 minutes away, and summer weekends bring beach traffic.

Where it shines

Active outdoorsTop 3%

Hanna Park is just north, a big City of Jacksonville park with beach, a freshwater lake, a campground and miles of wooded bike trails. In town, Dutton Island Preserve adds marsh views and a place to launch a kayak.

NightlifeTop 11%

Low key but real. Poe's Tavern, Coop 303 and the rest of Beaches Town Center keep the Atlantic Boulevard corner busy most evenings, with Pete's Bar just across the street in Neptune Beach. It all winds down earlier than Jax Beach.

Meet peopleTop 15%

Plenty going on for a small town. Dancin' in the Street marked its 40th year in the Town Center in May 2026, the Beaches Green Market runs on Saturdays next door in Neptune Beach, and the surf breaks and bike trails at Hanna Park have their regulars.

WalkabilityTop 17%

From the south end you can walk or bike to Beaches Town Center for coffee, dinner and shops, and the beach is at the end of the street. The farther north you go, the more that walk turns into a bike ride.

Dining and artsTop 22%

Beaches Town Center covers most of it, with Poe's Tavern, Coop 303, Salumeria 104 and a mix of pizza, seafood and coffee spots. For theater and museums you drive ten minutes south to Players by the Sea and the Beaches Museum.

Common questions

Is Atlantic Beach walkable?

Yes, for the most part. From the south end you can walk or bike to Beaches Town Center for coffee, dinner and shops, and the beach is at the end of the street. The farther north you go, the more that walk turns into a bike ride.

Does Atlantic Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. A wide stretch of firm sand with public access at the street ends and limited parking, which keeps the crowds down. The beach runs north into Hanna Park, where there are big parking lots and a well known surf break.

Is Atlantic Beach affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $640K median sale (Momentum, Q2 2026). Expensive for Northeast Florida. Houses near the ocean and the Town Center run well above the Florida median, and prices have been rising. The west side of town toward Mayport Road costs noticeably less.

What is the nightlife like in Atlantic Beach?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. An evening usually means the Town Center. Dinner at Coop 303 or Salumeria 104, a burger and a beer at Poe's Tavern, and a bike ride home. Bigger nights are ten minutes south in Jax Beach.

How busy does Atlantic Beach get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. The tree shaded streets stay calm, and the north end is quieter still. The Town Center corner and Atlantic Boulevard are busy, Mayport Road carries traffic to the Navy base, and summer weekends bring beach crowds.

Is Atlantic Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The west side of town meets the marsh and the Intracoastal, with paddle launches at Dutton Island Preserve. Powerboats put in a few minutes north at Mayport, right by the mouth of the St. Johns River.

Which airport is closest to Atlantic Beach?

Jacksonville (JAX) is about 35 minutes away. Atlantic Beach is the closest of the three beach towns to the airport, though only by a few minutes.

What should I know before moving to Atlantic Beach?

The main things to plan for: Hurricane exposure, rising prices. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
