About St. George Island

St. George Island is a long, skinny island off Eastpoint, with beach houses on stilts, a lighthouse in the center and miles of state park beach at the east end. There are no high rises and no chain stores. People bike, fish, collect shells and ride golf carts to the beach. Some live here full time, but many homes are second homes and rentals.

Storm surge is the big risk. Michael brought at least 5 feet of it in 2018, and there's one bridge on and off. Flood insurance matters, and homes are expensive for this part of Florida. Groceries, doctors and the airport are on the mainland, and Tallahassee is close to two hours away. Summers and spring break get busy.

Where it shines

BeachesTop 8%

This is the reason to come. The state park at the east end has nine miles of undeveloped beach, and Dr. Beach named it the number one beach in America in 2023. The main public beach at Lighthouse Park has a bathhouse, pavilions and parking. Leashed dogs are allowed on the county beaches, though not in the state park.

Active outdoorsTop 12%

The state park covers the east end of the island, with nine miles of beach, the 2.5 mile Gap Point Trail out to the bay, kayak rentals and almost 300 kinds of birds. Its campground has been closed for renovations since July 2026.

Peace and quietTop 20%

No high rises. The county caps buildings at 47 feet. Summer and spring break fill the rental houses and the public beach, fall is lighter, and winter is very quiet, with some places closed for the season.

Where it falls short

On the waterBottom 31%

The bay side is the boating side. A free county ramp sits by the bridge, Regatta Park has a kayak launch, and the state park has two unpaved ramps for smaller boats. There is no marina on the island, so slips and fuel mean Apalachicola or Carrabelle.

Dining and artsBottom 26%

A short list, mostly casual. Blue Parrot sits right on the Gulf, Paddy's is the raw bar, and there is pizza, donuts, ice cream and coffee near the center. The lighthouse has a small museum and Sea Oats Art Gallery shows local work. For more choices, Apalachicola is 11 miles away over two bridges.

Meet peopleBottom 23%

The island runs on a few standing dates. The Civic Club meets the third Thursday of each month at the firehouse with a potluck, the lighthouse holds a sunset and full moon climb every month, and the Charity Chili Cookoff each March has run for more than 40 years. The Sea Turtle Patrol takes volunteers.

Airport accessBottom 3%

A long drive. Tallahassee (TLH) is about 74 miles and an hour and 40 minutes away, and Northwest Florida Beaches (ECP) is a little over two hours west.

AffordabilityBottom 2%

Expensive. Redfin put the median sale near $1.1 million for the island's zip code over the three months ending August 2026, and that figure includes the mainland side of the bridge. The county requires new homes here to sit above flood level and have impact windows, so price out wind and flood insurance early.

Coastal charmTop 37%

Some charm: a few older blocks, local spots and low buildings.

WalkabilityTop 45%

The middle of the island is the walkable part. The bridge lands at Lighthouse Park, with the public beach, a small grocery, restaurants and shops within a few blocks, and a paved path runs along Gulf Beach Drive to the state park. Farther out you need a bike or a car.

NightlifeBottom 47%

A few places, and they keep island hours. Paddy's Raw Bar has 16 taps and live music some nights, and Mango Mike's and the Sand Bar are the other spots near the center of the island. Lively in summer, sleepy in winter.

Common questions

Is St. George Island walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. The middle of the island is the walkable part. The bridge lands at Lighthouse Park, with the public beach, a small grocery, restaurants and shops within a few blocks, and a paved path runs along Gulf Beach Drive to the state park. Farther out you need a bike or a car.

Does St. George Island have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. This is the reason to come. The state park at the east end has nine miles of undeveloped beach, and Dr. Beach named it the number one beach in America in 2023. The main public beach at Lighthouse Park has a bathhouse, pavilions and parking. Leashed dogs are allowed on the county beaches, though not in the state park.

Is St. George Island affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $1.25M median sale (Redfin, Sep 2026). Expensive. Redfin put the median sale near $1.1 million for the island's zip code over the three months ending August 2026, and that figure includes the mainland side of the bridge. The county requires new homes here to sit above flood level and have impact windows, so price out wind and flood insurance early.

What is the nightlife like in St. George Island?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A night out is oysters and a cold drink at Paddy's Raw Bar or dinner on the Gulf at the Blue Parrot, with a band if it's the season. Once a month the better plan is the sunset and full moon climb at the lighthouse. It's an early island.

How busy does St. George Island get?

It is quiet most of the year. No high rises. The county caps buildings at 47 feet. Summer and spring break fill the rental houses and the public beach, fall is lighter, and winter is very quiet, with some places closed for the season.

Is St. George Island good for boating and fishing?

Yes, with decent access to ramps and marinas. The bay side is the boating side. A free county ramp sits by the bridge, Regatta Park has a kayak launch, and the state park has two unpaved ramps for smaller boats. There is no marina on the island, so slips and fuel mean Apalachicola or Carrabelle.

Which airport is closest to St. George Island?

A long drive. Tallahassee (TLH) is about 74 miles and an hour and 40 minutes away, and Northwest Florida Beaches (ECP) is a little over two hours west.

What should I know before moving to St. George Island?
