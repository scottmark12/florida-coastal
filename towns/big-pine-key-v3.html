About Big Pine Key

Big Pine Key is the largest of the Lower Keys and home to the National Key Deer Refuge. It's quiet and spread out, with dark night skies, the Blue Hole and nature trails in the refuge, Looe Key reef offshore and Bahia Honda State Park about five minutes north.

Key West's airport is 30 to 40 minutes away. Shopping and dining are limited, home sales are thin, and Keys insurance and evacuation distance apply here too.
