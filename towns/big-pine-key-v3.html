About Big Pine Key

Big Pine Key is the largest of the Lower Keys and home to the National Key Deer Refuge. It's quiet and spread out, with dark night skies, the Blue Hole and nature trails in the refuge, Looe Key reef offshore and Bahia Honda State Park about five minutes north.

Key West's airport is 30 to 40 minutes away. Shopping and dining are limited, home sales are thin, and Keys insurance and evacuation distance apply here too.

Common questions

Is Big Pine Key walkable?

No. Spread out and rural. Plan on a car or a bike.

Does Big Pine Key have a good beach?

Not on the island itself. The big beaches are at Bahia Honda State Park, about five minutes north.

Is Big Pine Key affordable?

No, it is expensive. Home prices: About $600K to $880K, with thin sales (Broker One, June 2026; Momentum, 12 months 2026). Below Key West prices but still expensive, with thin sales and Keys insurance costs.

What is the nightlife like in Big Pine Key?

Very little. A small, quiet place with few restaurants and bars. Most nights out mean a drive to Key West or Marathon.

How busy does Big Pine Key get?

Rarely, outside winter and holiday weekends. Dark night skies and little going on off US 1. Much of the island is part of the National Key Deer Refuge.

Is Big Pine Key good for boating and fishing?

Yes, it is one of the best boating towns in the state. Shallow flats all around, and Looe Key reef is offshore.

Which airport is closest to Big Pine Key?

Key West (EYW) is about 30 to 40 minutes away.

What should I know before moving to Big Pine Key?

The main things to plan for: A long evacuation drive, high insurance. Statewide about $10,384 a year for $250K (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address.
