About Melbourne

Melbourne is the Space Coast's main city on the mainland, sitting on the Indian River. It has two walkable old downtowns: downtown Melbourne, with restaurants and the theater, and the Eau Gallie Arts District, with murals, galleries and river views. People here work in aerospace, healthcare and tech, and the beach in Indialantic is just over the causeway.

The trade offs: beyond the old downtowns, Melbourne is a spread out city of strip malls and busy roads, and Wickham Road and US 1 traffic can be slow. Riverfront areas can flood, and Brevard gets hurricanes. Prices change a lot from one neighborhood to the next, so it pays to know the streets.

Common questions

Is Melbourne walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Downtown Melbourne and the Eau Gallie Arts District are both walkable, with shops, restaurants and the river close together. Most of the rest of the city, including Viera, means driving.

Does Melbourne have a good beach?

It has a modest one. Better beaches are a drive away. No ocean beach in the mainland city. Indialantic and the beaches across the causeways are about 15 minutes away, so beach days are easy.

Is Melbourne affordable?

Yes, it costs less than most of the Florida coast. Home prices: About $350K typical home value (Zillow, Dec 2025). One of the more affordable places on this coast, with a typical value around $350K. Riverfront homes and newer Viera neighborhoods cost more, and insurance is a growing part of the budget.

What is the nightlife like in Melbourne?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. A typical night is a patio dinner downtown, then a pint at Meg O'Malley's or a brewery on New Haven Avenue. On the first Friday you walk Highland Avenue in Eau Gallie for art, vendors and music. Weeknights are mellow and weekends are busy.

How busy does Melbourne get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. Depends on the part of town. Downtown can be lively on weekends, the main roads are busy, and the riverfront and older neighborhoods are calm and shady.

Is Melbourne good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. Good. The Indian River runs along the city, the Eau Gallie River and Crane Creek have marinas and waterfront restaurants, and ramps and launches are easy to find. Boats get to the ocean through Sebastian or Port Canaveral inlets, which is a long run.

Which airport is closest to Melbourne?

Melbourne Orlando International (MLB) is in town, small and easy, with Delta adding seasonal weekend LaGuardia flights from December 2026. Orlando (MCO) is about an hour for more routes.

What should I know before moving to Melbourne?

The main things to plan for: Hurricane exposure, riverfront flooding, traffic on Wickham Road and US 1, a spread out city beyond the old downtowns.
