About Clearwater Beach

Clearwater Beach is a barrier island with powdery white sand, gentle Gulf water and a sunset celebration at Pier 60. The middle of the island is lively, with hotels and restaurants along the beach, but the north end and Island Estates are quiet and residential.

It's busy in season and on weekends, the causeway backs up, and most of the housing is condos. Houses on the water cost a lot. Mainland Clearwater and Dunedin are close by for more space.

Where it shines

BeachesTop 3%

Wide, soft white sand with public access right at Pier 60. Tripadvisor's 2026 awards ranked it the second best beach in the country and 17th in the world.

WalkabilityTop 10%

Once you're on the island you can park the car. Beach Walk runs along the sand to Pier 60, the marina and the shops on Mandalay Avenue are a few minutes on foot, and the Jolley Trolley covers the rest.

NightlifeTop 12%

The night starts at Pier 60, where a free sunset celebration with street performers and artisans runs every evening, weather permitting. After dark the beach bars along the strip carry on with live bands.

Meet peopleTop 14%

Most of what's on is built for visitors, but there are ways in. The Clearwater Community Sailing Center on Sand Key has lessons and memberships, and the Sugar Sand Festival takes over Pier 60 each spring.

Airport accessTop 23%

Two airports within easy reach. St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) is about 30 minutes and Tampa (TPA) about 35 to 40, a little longer when the causeway is backed up.

Where it falls short

Active outdoorsBottom 33%

Sand Key Park, just over the Clearwater Pass bridge, has 95 acres of beach and parkland. The causeway path to downtown makes a good bike ride, and Caladesi Island, third on Dr. Beach's 2026 list, is a short trip north.

Peace and quietBottom 16%

This is one of the busiest beaches in Florida. The roundabout and the Memorial Causeway back up on sunny weekends and all through spring, and Pier 60 draws a crowd every evening.

Coastal charmBottom 7%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

Dining and artsTop 25%

Plenty of seafood grills and beach bars, mostly casual and aimed at visitors. The Clearwater Marine Aquarium is the main attraction, and for theater and concerts you cross the causeway to downtown.

Common questions

Is Clearwater Beach walkable?

Yes, for the most part. Once you're on the island you can park the car. Beach Walk runs along the sand to Pier 60, the marina and the shops on Mandalay Avenue are a few minutes on foot, and the Jolley Trolley covers the rest.

Does Clearwater Beach have a good beach?

Yes. It is among the best on the Florida coast. Wide, soft white sand with public access right at Pier 60. Tripadvisor's 2026 awards ranked it the second best beach in the country and 17th in the world.

Is Clearwater Beach affordable?

It sits around the middle for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $650K to $800K median sale, mostly condos (2026). Mostly condos, from older low rise units to new towers, so entry prices cover a wide range. Figure on flood insurance and association fees on top, which add up fast on a barrier island.

What is the nightlife like in Clearwater Beach?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Most nights start on the sand at Pier 60 for the sunset, with buskers and craft tables. Then it's dinner along Beach Walk or Mandalay Avenue and a beach bar with a live band. Everything is close enough to do on foot.

How busy does Clearwater Beach get?

It gets busy, especially in season. This is one of the busiest beaches in Florida. The roundabout and the Memorial Causeway back up on sunny weekends and all through spring, and Pier 60 draws a crowd every evening.

Is Clearwater Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The Clearwater Beach Marina, home to the fishing fleet and tour boats, is wrapping up a $46 million rebuild in 2026 with new docks and a raised seawall. Clearwater Pass puts you in the Gulf in minutes.

Which airport is closest to Clearwater Beach?

Two airports within easy reach. St. Pete Clearwater (PIE) is about 30 minutes and Tampa (TPA) about 35 to 40, a little longer when the causeway is backed up.

What should I know before moving to Clearwater Beach?

The main things to plan for: Visitor crowds, storm surge. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
