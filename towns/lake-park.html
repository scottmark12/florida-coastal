About Lake Park

Lake Park is a compact town on the Intracoastal, just north of West Palm Beach. Life centers on the public marina, the waterfront park and a small downtown on Park Avenue. You'll find working families, retirees and a lot of boaters, and prices run lower than in the towns around it.

The beach isn't in town. It's a short drive or bike ride over the bridge to Singer Island. Homes near the Intracoastal face storm surge, and US 1 and the freight rail line bring noise to some streets. Big new waterfront towers are changing the skyline, so ask what's planned near any home you like.
