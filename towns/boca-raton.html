The scorecard

Tap any score for the local's take.

Nightlife3

Decent, and on the early side. Mizner Park Amphitheater books concerts and festivals, and the bars and lounges at Mizner Park and Royal Palm Place fill up on weekends. For a late night, Delray Beach or Fort Lauderdale is the move.

Meet people4

Plenty of ways in. Leagues, a challenge ladder and a find a game program at The Racquet Center, adult classes and leagues through the city, lectures and art classes at the museum, and city events like Boca Street Fest and the Night Market.

Peace and quiet3

The neighborhoods are calm and the beaches rarely feel packed. The tradeoff is the roads. Glades Road, Palmetto Park Road and the I-95 ramps back up at rush hour and through winter.

Walkability3

Downtown works on foot, with Mizner Park, Royal Palm Place and the Brightline station close together, and the beach is a bike ride east over the Palmetto Park Road bridge. The rest of the city is built for driving.

On the water4

The Intracoastal widens into Lake Boca Raton right by Boca Inlet. Silver Palm Park has a public boat ramp open around the clock with 26 trailer spaces, though you need the city's annual launch permit.

Beaches4

Three city beach parks, Spanish River, Red Reef and South Beach, keep the sand natural behind a line of dunes. Red Reef has snorkeling close to shore. Parking is by annual permit or a daily fee, now card only.

Dining and arts4

Mizner Park and Royal Palm Place hold most of the downtown restaurants, with the Boca Raton Museum of Art on the plaza. Festival of the Arts BOCA brings music and authors each March. Its 21st season is set for March 2027.

Outdoors and wellness3

Gumbo Limbo Nature Center has a boardwalk through coastal forest, a 40 foot observation tower and outdoor sea turtle tanks. Add reef snorkeling at Red Reef Park and big city parks like Sugar Sand. Long wild trails are farther west.

Affordability2

Expensive overall, with a wide spread. Houses east of US 1 and in the golf communities run high, and insurance costs more near the water. Condos and townhomes farther west bring the price of entry down.

Airport access4

Midway between two airports. About 30 to 35 minutes to the Palm Beach airport or to Fort Lauderdale (FLL), and Brightline trains stop downtown. Boca Raton Airport handles private planes only.

Common questions about Boca Raton

Is Boca Raton walkable?

In parts. Some errands are walkable, most are a short drive. Downtown works on foot, with Mizner Park, Royal Palm Place and the Brightline station close together, and the beach is a bike ride east over the Palmetto Park Road bridge. The rest of the city is built for driving.

Does Boca Raton have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Three city beach parks, Spanish River, Red Reef and South Beach, keep the sand natural behind a line of dunes. Red Reef has snorkeling close to shore. Parking is by annual permit or a daily fee, now card only.

Is Boca Raton affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: $630K median. Expensive overall, with a wide spread. Houses east of US 1 and in the golf communities run high, and insurance costs more near the water. Condos and townhomes farther west bring the price of entry down.

What is the nightlife like in Boca Raton?

Good bars and some live music, though it is only busy now and then. Dinner at Mizner Park or Royal Palm Place, then a concert on the lawn at Mizner Park Amphitheater when one is booked, or drinks at a downtown bar. It is a dinner and a show town more than a late one, and it quiets down earlier than Delray Beach.

How busy does Boca Raton get?

It is busy in season and calm the rest of the year. The neighborhoods are calm and the beaches rarely feel packed. The tradeoff is the roads. Glades Road, Palmetto Park Road and the I-95 ramps back up at rush hour and through winter.

Is Boca Raton good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The Intracoastal widens into Lake Boca Raton right by Boca Inlet. Silver Palm Park has a public boat ramp open around the clock with 26 trailer spaces, though you need the city's annual launch permit.

Which airport is closest to Boca Raton?

Midway between two airports. About 30 to 35 minutes to the Palm Beach airport or to Fort Lauderdale (FLL), and Brightline trains stop downtown. Boca Raton Airport handles private planes only.
