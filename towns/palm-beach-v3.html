About Palm Beach

Palm Beach is a long, narrow island of estates, shaded streets and a handful of shopping blocks. Mornings start on the Lake Trail, afternoons at the town beaches, and winter fills the calendar with charity events. Many owners are seasonal, so the island is busy from December to April and quiet in summer.

The obvious trade off is price. Houses here are among the most expensive in the country, and even condos on the south end cost more than most Florida homes. Bridges and traffic slow down in season, roads close at times for security, and the island faces the full weather in a hurricane. Groceries and daily errands are often a trip across the bridge.

Where it shines

Dining and artsTop 11%

Dinner is the main event. Buccan, Café Boulud and Sant Ambroeus are minutes apart, Worth Avenue has the galleries, and the Flagler Museum fills the Whitehall mansion. The Society of the Four Arts is under renovation, so its concerts and talks have moved.

Airport accessTop 11%

About 15 minutes over the bridge to the Palm Beach airport, a little longer in winter traffic or when a drawbridge is up. Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about an hour south.

Meet peopleTop 12%

You meet through standing programs more than bars. Talks and concerts from the Society of the Four Arts, tee times at the Palm Beach Par 3, and tennis and pickleball at the Phipps Ocean Park racquet center. Most of it is busiest in winter.

Coastal charmTop 14%

A charming town with a real old center, local shops and pretty streets.

WalkabilityTop 20%

Worth Avenue and its little courtyards, the Royal Poinciana Plaza and the ocean are a short bike ride apart, and the Lake Trail runs for miles along the lagoon. One stretch by the Four Arts is rerouted during construction.

Where it falls short

AffordabilityBottom 5%

This is one of the most expensive markets in Florida, and the gap is wide. Houses, condos and even small apartments cost far more than the same thing across the bridge, and barrier island insurance and upkeep add to it.

Peace and quietTop 35%

Clipped hedges, low speed limits and almost no through traffic keep most of the island very quiet, especially in summer. Winter brings more cars to the bridges, and road closures on South Ocean Boulevard can snarl things at times.

BeachesTop 40%

Clean sand, clear water and room to spread out. The main public beach is where the midtown streets meet the ocean, and the catch is parking, which is metered and scarce. Phipps Ocean Park closed for a full rebuild, with reopening set for October 2026.

NightlifeTop 44%

Evenings here are dinner and a cocktail, not a crawl. The bar at Café Boulud and the lounges at The Breakers are the usual stops, and most of the island is quiet well before midnight. For bands and late rooms you cross the bridge to Clematis Street.

On the waterTop 50%

The lagoon side is lined with docks, and the town's public marina sits close to Worth Avenue with slips sized for large yachts. The inlet at the north tip of the island puts you in the ocean fast, and the Gulf Stream runs close offshore.

Active outdoorsBottom 39%

Outdoors here means the Lake Trail on a bike and a walk on the beach. Pan's Garden is a small native plant garden near Worth Avenue. There are no hiking trails or big parks on the island, so you drive to the mainland for those.

Common questions

Is Palm Beach walkable?

Yes, for the most part. Worth Avenue and its little courtyards, the Royal Poinciana Plaza and the ocean are a short bike ride apart, and the Lake Trail runs for miles along the lagoon. One stretch by the Four Arts is rerouted during construction.

Does Palm Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a very good one. Clean sand, clear water and room to spread out. The main public beach is where the midtown streets meet the ocean, and the catch is parking, which is metered and scarce. Phipps Ocean Park closed for a full rebuild, with reopening set for October 2026.

Is Palm Beach affordable?

No. It is one of the most expensive spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $1.85M median condo and $12.9M median single family sale (Elliman/Miller Samuel, Q2 2025). This is one of the most expensive markets in Florida, and the gap is wide. Houses, condos and even small apartments cost far more than the same thing across the bridge, and barrier island insurance and upkeep add to it.

What is the nightlife like in Palm Beach?

Quiet, with a handful of places to go. A night out is a reservation, a walk along Worth Avenue or through the Royal Poinciana Plaza, and maybe one more drink at a hotel bar. The island winds down early. If you want live music after dinner, Clematis Street is a few minutes across the bridge.

How busy does Palm Beach get?

It is quiet most of the year. Clipped hedges, low speed limits and almost no through traffic keep most of the island very quiet, especially in summer. Winter brings more cars to the bridges, and road closures on South Ocean Boulevard can snarl things at times.

Is Palm Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The lagoon side is lined with docks, and the town's public marina sits close to Worth Avenue with slips sized for large yachts. The inlet at the north tip of the island puts you in the ocean fast, and the Gulf Stream runs close offshore.

Which airport is closest to Palm Beach?

About 15 minutes over the bridge to the Palm Beach airport, a little longer in winter traffic or when a drawbridge is up. Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is about an hour south.

What should I know before moving to Palm Beach?

The main things to plan for: Extremely high prices, road closures and traffic at times. Florida homeowners insurance averages about $10,384 a year for $250K of dwelling coverage (MoneyGeek, 2026). Get a quote and check the flood zone for any address before you commit.
