About West Melbourne

West Melbourne is a growing inland city right next to Melbourne, and most of it was built in the last few decades. Life here is easy and practical: newer houses with lakes out back, shopping close by, good access to I 95 and a short drive to the airport. A lot of families and working people live here, along with retirees who want a newer house without beach prices.

The trade offs: it's inland, so the beach is a 20 minute drive over the causeway. Traffic on the main roads can back up, and much of the city is strip malls and big box stores rather than a historic downtown. Many newer neighborhoods have an HOA, and Brevard is still hurricane country.

Where it shines

AffordabilityTop 31%

Good value for the Space Coast. Newer subdivisions price below the beach towns, and being inland usually means lower wind and flood insurance costs.

Dining and artsTop 34%

Mostly chains and plaza restaurants around Hammock Landing and US 192, with a few independents mixed in. For galleries, festivals and shows you head to downtown Melbourne, the Eau Gallie Arts District or the King Center.

Where it falls short

Peace and quietBottom 33%

Quiet inside the neighborhoods, busy on the roads between them. US 192, Minton Road and Wickham Road carry heavy traffic at rush hour and on weekend shopping runs. There are no visitor crowds.

Airport accessBottom 33%

Melbourne (MLB) is about 10 minutes away, as close as it gets, though it has a short list of nonstops. Orlando (MCO) is about an hour up I-95 and the Beachline.

NightlifeBottom 28%

Very little after dinner. The restaurants around Hammock Landing and along US 192 close early, and most of the city is dark by 9. Downtown Melbourne's bars and live music are about 15 minutes east.

Coastal charmBottom 17%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

BeachesBottom 14%

No beach here. The closest sand is in Indialantic, straight east on US 192 and over the causeway, about 20 minutes without traffic.

WalkabilityBottom 13%

Built for cars. Shopping is in big plazas like Hammock Landing off US 192, with wide roads and parking lots in between. There is no walkable center.

Active outdoorsBottom 2%

West Melbourne Community Park has sports fields and open space, and that is most of it in town. Bigger trails are in Palm Bay at Turkey Creek Sanctuary, about 15 minutes south.

On the waterBottom 2%

Inland, with no waterfront of its own. The nearest boat ramps and marinas are on the Indian River in Melbourne, about 15 minutes east. Retention ponds are the only water in most neighborhoods.
