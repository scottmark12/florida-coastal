About North Palm Beach

North Palm Beach is a small village between the Intracoastal and Lake Worth Lagoon, just south of Jupiter and Juno Beach. Many homes sit on canals with docks, and condo buildings line the water at Old Port Cove and along US 1. Daily life is calm and outdoorsy: boating, fishing, golf at the village country club and long walks on the beach at John D. MacArthur Beach State Park across the bridge. It draws retirees, boaters and families who want a quieter version of Palm Beach County.

The trade offs: waterfront homes face storm surge, so check flood zones and insurance before you buy. Canal homes are expensive, and older condo buildings may have assessments. Winters bring more traffic on US 1 and PGA Boulevard, and the nightlife is mostly a short drive away in Palm Beach Gardens.

Where it shines

On the waterTop 8%

Water on nearly every side. The Intracoastal, Lake Worth and the canals behind many houses lead to the ocean through the inlet a few miles south. Big marinas line U.S. 1, Lakeside Park is an easy kayak launch, and Munyon Island is a short paddle away.

Airport accessTop 14%

About 20 minutes to the Palm Beach airport by I-95 or U.S. 1, one of the easier airport runs on this coast. Fort Lauderdale (FLL) is a little over an hour south.

Where it falls short

Coastal charmBottom 27%

Mostly new build, chains and towers. Not much old town feel.

NightlifeBottom 26%

Early nights. A few restaurants and marina bars along U.S. 1 serve dinner and a drink, and the village is mostly dark by 9 or 10. For music or anything late you drive to Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter or West Palm.

WalkabilityBottom 25%

You can walk or bike the neighborhood streets to a park or the country club, but shops and restaurants are strung along U.S. 1 and most errands take a car. The state park beach is a short drive or a longer bike ride over the bridge.

Dining and artsBottom 20%

A short list. Farmer's Table at the country club and a handful of casual and waterfront places along U.S. 1 cover dinner. For a wider choice of restaurants, shopping or a show, Palm Beach Gardens is about 10 minutes west.

Active outdoorsTop 30%

MacArthur Beach State Park is the backyard, with nature trails, kayaking through the estuary and out to Munyon Island, and a nature center. Add Lakeside Park on the water and the village golf course and you can be outside every day.

Peace and quietTop 39%

Residential streets and canals with very little through traffic. U.S. 1 carries the cars and gets busier in winter, but a block off it things are calm, and there is no nightlife strip or big attraction pulling in crowds.

BeachesBottom 41%

The village has no ocean beach of its own, but John D. MacArthur Beach State Park is just over the bridge, with 1.6 miles of undeveloped sand and a reef close enough to snorkel. Entry is $5 a car. It is a nature beach with few services.

Meet peopleBottom 38%

The village runs its own social calendar. The recreation department hosts duplicate bridge, mah jongg, canasta and table tennis, the country club has a public Jack Nicklaus Signature golf course, and MacArthur Beach State Park holds a monthly beach cleanup.

AffordabilityBottom 36%

Waterfront houses with ocean access cost a lot, and prices overall sit well above the Florida median. Older condos along U.S. 1 and the lake are the lower cost way in. Flood and wind insurance are a real line in the budget on the canals.

Common questions

Is North Palm Beach walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. You can walk or bike the neighborhood streets to a park or the country club, but shops and restaurants are strung along U.S. 1 and most errands take a car. The state park beach is a short drive or a longer bike ride over the bridge.

Does North Palm Beach have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. The village has no ocean beach of its own, but John D. MacArthur Beach State Park is just over the bridge, with 1.6 miles of undeveloped sand and a reef close enough to snorkel. Entry is $5 a car. It is a nature beach with few services.

Is North Palm Beach affordable?

It is on the expensive side for the Florida coast. Home prices: About $628K median sale over the last 12 months (Horizon Palm Realty, Nov 2026). Waterfront houses with ocean access cost a lot, and prices overall sit well above the Florida median. Older condos along U.S. 1 and the lake are the lower cost way in. Flood and wind insurance are a real line in the budget on the canals.

What is the nightlife like in North Palm Beach?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. A night out is dinner on the water or at the country club, then home. The village is quiet by 9 or 10. When you want live music or a late bar, it is a short drive to Palm Beach Gardens or up to Jupiter.

How busy does North Palm Beach get?

It is quiet most of the year. Residential streets and canals with very little through traffic. U.S. 1 carries the cars and gets busier in winter, but a block off it things are calm, and there is no nightlife strip or big attraction pulling in crowds.

Is North Palm Beach good for boating and fishing?

Yes. Life here revolves around the water. Water on nearly every side. The Intracoastal, Lake Worth and the canals behind many houses lead to the ocean through the inlet a few miles south. Big marinas line U.S. 1, Lakeside Park is an easy kayak launch, and Munyon Island is a short paddle away.
