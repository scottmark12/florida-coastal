About Carrabelle

Carrabelle sits where the Carrabelle River meets the Gulf on the Forgotten Coast. The harbor is the center of town, with fishing boats, a few shops and a slow, friendly pace. People fish, boat out to Dog Island, and paddle the creeks in Tate's Hell State Forest. Many residents are retirees and longtime local families, and everyone seems to know everyone.

The trade offs are distance and storms. Storm surge is a real risk along the river and the beach, so flood zones matter. There are few services in town, and Tallahassee is over an hour away for the airport, big stores and hospitals. Sales are thin, so prices jump around.

Where it falls short

Meet peopleBottom 24%

Small but real. A farmers market at the Crooked River Lighthouse on the first and third Saturdays, an artist association, library clubs and the Riverfront Festival each April. It does not take long to know the faces.

Dining and artsBottom 25%

A handful of places, mostly seafood and breakfast spots like Fisherman's Wife and Carrabelle Junction, and they tend to close early. For galleries, theater or a bigger dinner out you drive to Apalachicola.

WalkabilityBottom 23%

NightlifeBottom 14%

Early nights. Fathom's on the harbor has live music some evenings and Harry's Bar has been pouring since 1942, but that is about it. Most of town is dark by 9.

Airport accessBottom 11%

Common questions

Is Carrabelle walkable?

Not really. You will drive for most things. The few blocks around Marine Street and the harbor are an easy stroll, with a cafe, a history museum and the docks. The beach is a mile and a half west and bigger shopping is a long drive, so you need a car.

Does Carrabelle have a good beach?

Yes, a decent one. Carrabelle Beach is a mile and a half west of town, with restrooms, picnic areas and calm, shallow water. It is a quiet, sheltered beach, not Emerald Coast surf. The white sand of St. George Island is a drive west.

Is Carrabelle affordable?

Yes. It is one of the more affordable spots on the Florida coast. Home prices: About $260K median sale (Redfin, Aug 2026). Among the lowest home prices on the Florida coast, with older cottages, river lots and manufactured homes in the mix. Much of town sits low, so price out flood and wind insurance before you fall for a place.

What is the nightlife like in Carrabelle?

Very quiet. Most of town is dark early. A night out is a seafood dinner by the harbor, maybe a band at Fathom's, and a drink at Harry's Bar. Then it is the porch. If you want more than that, Apalachicola is a little over half an hour west.

How busy does Carrabelle get?

It is one of the quietest places on the Florida coast. About as quiet as the Florida coast gets. US 98 runs through town with no real traffic, the harbor is mostly working boats and a few charters, and the nights are dark enough for stars.

Is Carrabelle good for boating and fishing?

Yes. This is a strong boating town. The Carrabelle River opens right into the Gulf. C-Quarters Marina has 67 slips and fuel, there are public ramps and a fishing pier in town, and it is a short run out to Dog Island. Strong on fishing, light on extras.

Which airport is closest to Carrabelle?

Tallahassee (TLH) is about an hour and 15 minutes away on two lane roads. Northwest Florida Beaches (ECP) is closer to two hours west. Plan on a real drive for any flight.

What should I know before moving to Carrabelle?
