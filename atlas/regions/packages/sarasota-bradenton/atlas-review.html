Manual fixes

  • Anna Maria Island card uses the Anna Maria city median and representative place anchor; it does not claim that median covers Holmes Beach or the entire island.
  • Districts use mapped town place-label anchors; recipe colors are an editorial interpretation of cited local architectural references.
  • Existing Census land context continues beyond the OSM data rectangle, avoiding artificial water edges at the package boundary.
  • Added source-backed tree canopy from ESA WorldCover 2021 (10 m) and FWC mapped forest, because OSM tree mapping alone omitted residential canopy.
  • Adjusted the regional camera and input label priorities so the primary city and northern island labels stay readable around the controls; geographic anchors are unchanged.
